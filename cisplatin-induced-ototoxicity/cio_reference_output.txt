PART 5 — LATE PROGRESSION BELONGS TO THE LABILE POOL, NOT TO THE PLATINUM
          THAT A MASS SPECTROMETER STILL FINDS DECADES LATER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cochlear platinum is split into a LABILE, redox-active pool (half-life
TLAB_D) and a BOUND, inert pool (half-life TRET_D) that carries almost all
of the retained mass.  Only the first drives damage.  Sweeping each in turn
makes that a falsifiable claim rather than a modelling convenience.

           1 month       9.54      13.33          3.79                0.0001

  The audiogram does not move when TRET_D is changed by a factor of 240,
  while the measured cochlear platinum moves by the same factor.  The model
  therefore predicts that retained platinum is a MARKER of past exposure and
  not the agent of continuing damage — and that an intervention aimed at
  chelating it years later would change nothing.
