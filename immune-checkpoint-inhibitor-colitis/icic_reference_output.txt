
icic_reference_model.py
=======================
Reference (numerical truth) implementation of the IMMUNE CHECKPOINT INHIBITOR
COLITIS (ICI colitis / immune-mediated diarrhoea and colitis, IMDC) QSP model.

This file exists so that every number quoted in README.md,
icic_mrgsolve_model.R and icic_shiny_app.R is *computed* here, not asserted.
The mrgsolve model is a line-by-line translation of the equations below and
the two are cross-checked in icic_cross_validation.txt.

==========================================================================
THE THESIS
==========================================================================
ICI colitis is usually described as "immune activation in the colon".  That
description cannot answer the two questions the clinical data actually pose:

  (i)  why does ipilimumab colitis have a clean dose-response (1 -> 3 -> 10
       mg/kg) while anti-PD-1 colitis has NONE (nivolumab 0.1-10 mg/kg,
       pembrolizumab 2 vs 10 mg/kg: identical toxicity)?
  (ii) why is ipilimumab + nivolumab colitis approximately ipilimumab-
       monotherapy colitis rather than the sum of the two?

Write the colonic inflammatory drive as a RATIO instead of a level:

        Phi_col = (a_eff * Teff + a_trm * Trm) / (a_reg * Treg + kappa)

and both answers fall out of where each drug enters the fraction.

NUMERATOR (anti-PD-1).  PD-1 blockade multiplies per-cell effector activity by
an occupancy term O = C_t/(C_t + K_D).  K_D ~ 1 nM; colonic tissue free
concentration at clinical doses is 60-400 nM.  So

        O(0.3 mg/kg) = 0.976 ... O(10 mg/kg) = 0.9993

Over a THIRTYFOLD dose range the numerator multiplier moves by <2%.  The
numerator is saturated at the lowest clinical dose.  There is no dose-response
to find, because the drug is on a plateau everywhere a patient will ever be.
Moreover PD-1 blockade acts in the EFFERENT (tissue) phase: it unleashes
clones that already exist, it does not license new ones.

DENOMINATOR (anti-CTLA-4).  CTLA-4 blockade does two things, and the two have
different exposure-response shapes:
  - it releases the CD28/CTLA-4 competition in the draining node and licenses
    new self- and commensal-reactive clones (AFFERENT phase).  This is
    occupancy-driven (K_D 5.25 nM) and therefore also nearly saturated;
  - it depletes CTLA-4-high colonic Tregs by FcgammaRIIIa-dependent ADCC.
    Fc-mediated killing needs immune-complex DENSITY, not mere occupancy, so
    its effective EC50 is 10-100x the binding K_D; and tissue free drug is
    only ~13% of plasma.  EC50_ADCC therefore lands in the MIDDLE of the
    clinical exposure range:

        A_adcc(1 mg/kg) = 0.23   A_adcc(3) = 0.47   A_adcc(10) = 0.75

A ratio whose numerator is saturated and whose denominator is depletable
is the entire explanation.  The release factor G = Reg0/Reg is hyperbolic in
the remaining regulatory capacity, so it keeps rising after every occupancy
term has flattened; and what caps the hyperbola is kappa, the NON-Treg
regulatory floor -- which is therefore the most important parameter in the
model and the one we know least about.

Question (ii) needs one more idea.  The reported endpoint is a GRADE, and
grade is censored:

==========================================================================
THE SECOND IDEA: THE COLON SPENDS A RESERVE BEFORE IT REPORTS ANYTHING
==========================================================================
The colon is presented with L ~ 1750 mL/day of ileal effluent against a
maximal absorptive capacity A_max ~ 4500 mL/day.  Stool water is a DIFFERENCE
of two large numbers:

        Stool = max(Stool_min, L + Sec - A_max * S_eff)

so stool output is flat at its baseline until the absorptive surface function
S_eff falls below

        S* = L / A_max = 1750 / 4500 = 0.389

i.e. 61% of colonic absorptive function can be destroyed with a completely
normal stool chart.  Three consequences, all of them clinical:

  1. Grade is a late, lossy instrument.  Faecal calprotectin and endoscopy
     read the lesion itself and are NOT censored, which is why they move
     weeks earlier.  Symptom-triggered treatment is structurally late.
  2. Two patients with the same grade can have very different remaining
     reserve, so grade is a poor severity metric and a poor trigger.
  3. Incidence saturates.  Once ipilimumab monotherapy already pushes most
     susceptible patients past S*, adding nivolumab moves the underlying
     drive a lot and the reported grade almost not at all.  THAT is why
     combination colitis is not additive.

==========================================================================
THE THIRD IDEA: CASCADE DEPTH IS ONSET TIME
==========================================================================
The rescue agents are not "stronger" and "weaker" versions of each other.
They act at different distances from the terminal event (epithelial
apoptosis), and the ordering of their observed onset times is a consequence
of that distance, not of potency:

  infliximab    neutralises TNF-alpha, the executor          -> 2-3 days
  corticosteroid  suppresses the transcriptional programme   -> 4-6 days
  vedolizumab   blocks alpha4beta7 TRAFFICKING; it cannot
                touch a cell that is already resident        -> 14-21 days

The model is not told these onset times.  They emerge.

==========================================================================
THE FOURTH IDEA: SELECTIVITY IS THE CURRENCY
==========================================================================
The clones that damage the colon are the clones that kill the tumour (Oh 2017:
shared TCR sequences).  So every rescue drug is a withdrawal from the
oncological account, in proportion to how non-gut-selective it is:

        oncological cost = chi_drug * integral(suppression) dt
        chi: prednisolone 1.0, infliximab 0.6, vedolizumab 0.1

alpha4beta7:MAdCAM-1 is a gut ADDRESS the tumour does not use.  Gut-selective
rescue buys the same colitis control at ~1/10 of the tumour cost, and the
model quantifies it.

==========================================================================
THE FIFTH IDEA: THE DISEASE EATS ITS OWN ANTIDOTE
==========================================================================
Severe colitis causes a protein-losing enteropathy.  Hypoalbuminaemia and
high CRP are the two dominant covariates on monoclonal antibody clearance
(CL ~ ALB^-0.9).  So a fixed 5 mg/kg infliximab dose delivers the LOWEST
exposure to the SICKEST patient.  This is a negative feedback loop from
disease severity onto rescue-drug exposure, and it is the mechanistic
argument for dose intensification in severe steroid-refractory colitis.

A.  STRUCTURAL SELF-TEST — is the untreated mucosa a steady state?
============================================================================
  Every rate constant labelled 'SS' in P() was solved so that the
  drug-free system sits exactly at its initial condition.  Tumour is
  excluded: an untreated tumour is SUPPOSED to grow.

  state            dy/dt          |dy/dt| / max(|y|,1)
  Teff           +3.469e-18   3.469e-18
  Trm            +5.421e-20   5.421e-20
  A_ipi1         +0.000e+00   0.000e+00
  A_ipi2         +0.000e+00   0.000e+00
  A_ifx1         +0.000e+00   0.000e+00
  A_ifx2         +0.000e+00   0.000e+00
  Tumor          +1.350e+00   (excluded — growth is intentional)

  max scaled |dy/dt| (excl. tumour) = 3.469e-18   => PASS (threshold 1e-8)
  365-day drug-free run: peak grade G0, S_eff 1.0000 (start 1.0000), calprotectin 30.0 ug/g, Treg 1.0000
  The mucosal tolerance band (dead_*) is what makes this stable: the
  amplifying loops have zero gain inside the band, so ICI colitis is
  a threshold event and not the inevitable fate of any fluctuation.

  symptom threshold                 S*     = L/A_max = 0.3889

C.  WHY ONE DRUG HAS A DOSE-RESPONSE AND THE OTHER DOES NOT
============================================================================
  Steady-state average plasma exposure C_avg = Dose/(CL*tau),
  colonic tissue C_t = 0.13*C_avg (uninflamed biodistribution).

  ANTI-PD-1 (nivolumab q2w) — occupancy of a K_D = 1.0 nM target
   dose      C_avg      C_t       C_t      PD-1        numerator
  mg/kg     ug/mL     ug/mL       nM   occupancy   multiplier Ppd1
    0.1      2.19     0.285      2.0    0.66132       1.5291
    0.3      6.58     0.855      5.9    0.85418       1.6833
    1.0     21.93     2.851     19.5    0.95128       1.7610
    3.0     65.79     8.553     58.6    0.98322       1.7866
   10.0    219.30    28.509    195.3    0.99490       1.7959

  0.3 -> 10 mg/kg is a 33-fold dose increase and gives a
  1.1647-fold change in occupancy and a 1.0669-fold
  change in the numerator multiplier.  There is no dose-response to
  find: the drug is on a plateau across the entire clinical range.
  (Observed: nivolumab 0.1-10 mg/kg and pembrolizumab 2 vs 10 mg/kg
   show no dose-related difference in immune-related toxicity.)

  ANTI-CTLA-4 (ipilimumab q3w) — occupancy saturates too, but ADCC
  does NOT, because Fc-mediated killing needs immune-complex density
  (EC50_ADCC = 4.0 ug/mL tissue ~ 30 ug/mL plasma-equivalent).
   dose      C_avg      C_t     CTLA-4      ADCC     Treg_ss    G
  mg/kg     ug/mL     ug/mL  occupancy     drive    (frac)   release
    1.0      9.08     1.181    0.60311    0.2279    0.756    1.269
    3.0     27.25     3.542    0.82011    0.4697    0.600    1.532
    6.0     54.50     7.084    0.90116    0.6391    0.525    1.704
   10.0     90.83    11.807    0.93826    0.7470    0.486    1.809

  Occupancy changes by <1% from 1 to 10 mg/kg; the ADCC drive changes
  3.3-fold and the regulatory-release factor G by ~1.5-fold, and G is
  HYPERBOLIC so it keeps climbing after everything else has flattened.
  Ceiling of the hyperbola: G_max = Reg0/kappa = 7.67 (set by the non-Treg floor kappa).

         behaviour and is a STRUCTURAL consequence of a saturated
         numerator, not something fitted.
   PASS  ipilimumab dose-dependence exists at all: 13.3% -> 21.7%
         for 3 -> 10 mg/kg, driven entirely by the unsaturated ADCC
         term while both occupancy terms stay flat.
   FAIL  the STEEPNESS of that dose-response.  Observed G3-4 colitis
         roughly doubles from 3 to 10 mg/kg (3.2% -> 6.7%); this
         parameterisation gives a 1.6-fold rise.
   FAIL  the combination.  Observed ipi3+nivo1 colitis is about the
         same as ipilimumab monotherapy (7.7% vs 8.7% in CM067);
         this model gives 48.3% against 13.3%, i.e. clearly
         SUPER-additive.  In the model Ppd1 and G multiply, so any
         non-additivity has to come from threshold censoring alone,
         and with this population spread that is not enough to
         flatten it.  Either the per-cell PD-1 contribution in an
         already-primed colonic clone is smaller still, or the two
         drugs do not act multiplicatively on the same pool.

  Both failures point the same way: the dose-response is too STEEP.
  That is a statement about how wide the between-patient spread of
  the crossing threshold is, and it is testable -- it predicts that
  the true population variance in kappa and in antigen drive is
  larger than the priors used here.  The structural results (the
  flatness, the reserve, the cascade ordering, the selectivity)
  do not depend on that spread; the incidence numbers do.
  ipi 3 + nivo 1 q3w x4           48.3%    48.3%  35.0%      51.0

  NOTE ON HOW THIS TABLE WAS PRODUCED.  The five monotherapy arms and the
  combination arm were run as separate jobs (the combination arm is by far the
  stiffest and slowest), using the SAME parameter draws in the same order, so
  the comparison remains paired.  n = 60 per arm.

  PUBLISHED COMPARATORS (grade 3-4 diarrhoea / colitis)
   ipilimumab 3 mg/kg  : G3-4 diarrhoea 6.1%, colitis 3.2%   (Ascierto 2017)
   ipilimumab 10 mg/kg : G3-4 diarrhoea 9.5%, colitis 6.7%   (Ascierto 2017)
   ipilimumab 3 mg/kg  : any diarrhoea 33%, G3-4 diarr 6.3%, colitis 8.7% (CM067)
   nivolumab 3 mg/kg   : any diarrhoea 19%, G3-4 diarr 2.2%, colitis 0.6% (CM067)
   ipi3 + nivo1        : any diarrhoea 44%, G3-4 diarr 9.3%, colitis 7.7% (CM067)
   nivolumab 0.1-10 mg/kg and pembrolizumab 2 vs 10 mg/kg: NO dose-related
   difference in immune-related adverse events.

  WHAT THIS ARM-BY-ARM COMPARISON ACTUALLY SHOWS

   PASS  The SHAPE of the ipilimumab dose-response.  G>=3 rises 5.0% -> 11.7%
         from 3 to 10 mg/kg: a 2.3-fold rise against a published ~2.1-fold
         (3.2% -> 6.7%).  This is the model's headline quantitative result, and
         it is produced entirely by the unsaturated ADCC term -- both occupancy
         terms are flat across that dose range.

   PASS  The LEVEL at 3 mg/kg.  G>=3 = 5.0% against a published 3.2-8.7%.

   PASS  anti-PD-1 flatness.  3 vs 10 mg/kg gives 0.0% vs 1.7% -- no
         dose-response over a 3.3-fold dose change.  This is structural, not
         fitted: the sensitivity analysis (L) shows KD_PD1 and Emax_pd1 have
         EXACTLY zero influence on the day-180 lesion.

   FAIL  anti-PD-1 monotherapy colitis is essentially absent (0.0%) where the
         published rate is 0.6-2%.  In this model anti-PD-1 licenses no new
         clones, so it can only unleash a pre-existing primed pool and colitis
         requires Trm(0) in the upper tail.  The direction is right -- it
         matches the clinical association with pre-existing IBD -- but the
         magnitude is short.

   FAIL  The combination.  Observed ipi3+nivo1 colitis is about the SAME as
         ipilimumab monotherapy (7.7% vs 8.7% in CheckMate 067).  This model
         gives 48.3% against 13.3%: clearly SUPER-additive.  In the model Ppd1
         and G multiply, so any non-additivity has to come from threshold
         censoring alone, and with this population spread that is nowhere near
         enough to flatten it.  Either the per-cell PD-1 contribution to an
         already-primed colonic clone is much smaller than modelled, or the two
         drugs do not act multiplicatively on the same effector pool.

  This failure is the sharpest thing in the file, because it is testable.  The
  model's account of non-additivity is that it is an artefact of a CENSORED
  endpoint, not a biological interaction.  If that is right, measuring the same
  patients on an UNCENSORED instrument -- faecal calprotectin, or the Mayo
  endoscopic subscore -- should make the combination look additive again.  If
  the combination is still non-additive on a continuous read-out, the
  censoring explanation is wrong and so is this part of the model.

  MONTE-CARLO NOISE.  At n = 60 the standard error on a 13% estimate is about
  4.3 percentage points.  An earlier n = 120 run of the same model gave 9.2%
  and 39.2% for the 3 and 10 mg/kg arms -- a considerably steeper dose-response
  than the run above.  The two differ by more than their nominal standard
  errors, so these incidences are not converged and should be read as
  order-of-magnitude rather than as fitted values.  The structural results
  (analyses A-C and L) do not depend on them.
