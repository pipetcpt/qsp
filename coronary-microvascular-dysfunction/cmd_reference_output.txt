SECTION 0.  CALIBRATION AGAINST MEASURED CORONARY PHYSIOLOGY
==============================================================================
Seven constants are solved (not tuned by hand) against seven published
targets.  Everything else in the model is fixed a priori.

targets:
  T1 control hyperaemic MR      2.20 mmHg/(cm/s)   Rahman 2019 (MR_hyp<2.5)
  T2 structural hyperaemic MR   3.60               Rahman 2019 (>=2.5)
  T3 functional hyperaemic MR   2.30 = 4.2/1.83    Rahman 2019 (internally
                                                    consistent with T4)
  T4 functional resting MR      4.20               Rahman 2019 (4.2+-1.0)
  T5 control hyperaemic endo/epi 1.02              transmural physiology
  T6 untreated functional SAQ   55.0               CorMicA baseline ~55-60

4.2 / 2.30 = 1.83 - because CFR = MR_rest/MR_hyp identically when both are
measured at the same aortic pressure.  Calibrating two of them therefore

            functional MR_rest 4.200 -> CFR 1.826 (observed 1.83)
            control hyperaemic endo/epi 1.020

  K_ANG                       = 3.3997  -> SAQ 54.98
  NOCI_THRESH                 = 3.8575  -> Bruce 480.1 s

These five flow constants and two symptom constants are the ONLY values

SECTION II.  RESTING TONE IS NOT A FREE PARAMETER
==============================================================================
Autoregulation sets tone so that resting supply meets resting demand.
Given the measured HYPERAEMIC MR (which fixes R_min), the RESTING MR is
therefore predicted with no further parameter.  Only the control group's
hyperaemic MR was calibrated; the structural group's resting MR was not.

endotype        MR_hyp   MR_rest_pred   MR_rest_obs    error
------------------------------------------------------------
control           2.20           7.56           7.3     3.5%
structural        3.60           8.07           6.9    16.9%

The structural endotype's resting MR is a PREDICTION and it lands inside
the measured 6.9+-1.7.  Autoregulation reduces resting tone to compensate
for the raised R_min, and that compensation is what consumes the reserve.

Now the functional endotype, controller intact, offset switched off:
  offset 0.00 -> MR_rest 7.73  CFR 3.36  tone 0.527
  offset 0.92 -> MR_rest 4.20  CFR 1.83  tone 0.298   (observed 4.2 / 1.83)

BUG B1 and the model's central claim: a working autoregulator CANNOT
produce a low resting MR beside a normal minimal MR.  Setting tone by hand
does not survive one step of the loop - the controller puts it back.  The
functional endotype is therefore not a tone state but a CONTROLLER DEFECT,
and its magnitude is derivable rather than assumed: the arterioles behave
as though metabolic demand were 92% higher than it is.

FALSIFIABLE PREDICTION - needs coronary-sinus sampling, not a wire:
  resting extraction  control 0.689   functional 0.383   ratio 0.56
  Resting myocardial oxygen extraction in the functional endotype must be
  about 56% of normal, with a correspondingly RAISED coronary venous
  oxygen saturation (30% -> roughly 60% if arterial SO2 is 98%).
  If measured resting extraction is normal, the controller-offset reading
  is wrong and the low resting MR must instead be an artefact of the
  measurement condition (anxiety, contrast, supine catheter-laboratory
  haemodynamics).  Either way the model is refutable on one blood gas,
  which is the cheapest experiment it suggests.

The signs agree almost everywhere.  An earlier version of this section
claimed outright SIGN discordance between CFR and symptoms; that was an
artefact of bug B24 and the claim is withdrawn.  What survives is weaker and
more specific, and it is about RANKING - a trial picks one endpoint and ranks
drugs on it:

endotype      rho(dCFR,-dNOC) rho(dCFR,dEXD) best on CFR      best on symptoms
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
functional              0.60           0.54 ivabradine       ivabradine      
structural              0.94           0.96 ivabradine       ivabradine      
vasospastic             0.72           0.95 ivabradine       amlodipine      

The pattern in those three numbers is the point: CFR ranks treatments almost
perfectly in the structural endotype, where flow IS the disease, and much
less well in the two endotypes where it is not.

Read the functional-endotype rows again.  Aminophylline moves CFR by +0.012
- fifth of eleven arms, indistinguishable from placebo on a physiological
endpoint - and buys +147.5 s of Bruce time, twice the best vasoactive arm,
because in that endotype the afferent adenosine signal and not the flow is
the disease.  Elliott et al.
Heart 1997;77:523 (PMID 9227295) gave oral aminophylline to patients with
angina and normal arteriograms and improved exercise time; the model
reproduces that from the structure rather than from a fitted effect, and
predicts it should NOT work in the structural endotype, where blocking A2A
costs dilator reserve that is actually being used.

In the vasospastic endotype the two calcium antagonists and the Rho-kinase
inhibitor take the top three symptom places while their CFR changes are
mid-table, and nicorandil - a potent dilator - does nothing at all,
because between episodes there is nothing to dilate.

One negative result worth stating plainly: microvascular resistance reserve
does NOT rescue this.  MRR = (CFR/FFR)(Pa_rest/Pa_hyper), and a drug that
lowers blood pressure lowers it in BOTH states, so the correction cancels.
Here Pa_rest and Pa_hyper are equal by construction, which makes the
cancellation exact: MRR = CFR/FFR identically, so dMRR = dCFR/FFR to three
decimals and MRR adds no information about a treatment effect.  MRR earns its
keep against an EPICARDIAL stenosis, which is a different problem from a
drug's own haemodynamics, and the two are easily conflated.  The
index that stays interpretable under treatment is the hyperaemic RESISTANCE
itself: dMR_hyp above is near zero for every drug that has no true effect on
minimal resistance, whereas dCFR is not.  If a trial wants a physiological
endpoint that a blood-pressure-lowering drug cannot fake, it should
prespecify hyperaemic microvascular resistance, not a reserve ratio.

The model reproduces the trial's number, and the reason is not the one this
section was written expecting.  The three terms are:

  1. The microvascular gain is exactly zero.  Removing ETA-mediated tone
     helps only where there is tone to remove that is not already removed:
     in the functional endotype minimal resistance is normal, so there is
     nothing to gain, and in the structural endotype the ceiling is
     anatomical, so a receptor antagonist cannot lift it.  A drug can be
     perfectly on-target and still have no target left to hit.
  2. The blood-pressure fall is a small NET BENEFIT here, not a cost
     (+2.41 s).  Lowering systolic pressure removes wall-stress demand faster
     than it removes diastolic driving pressure, because demand carries the
     systolic pressure through the tension-time product while supply carries
     only the diastolic mean.  This section originally asserted the opposite
     and the arithmetic contradicted it.
  3. Fluid retention accounts for the ENTIRE null (-6.99 s).  LVEDP is
     subtracted from subendocardial driving pressure directly, so the
     trial's dominant adverse effect - present in 60% of treated periods -
     and its failure on the primary endpoint are the SAME event measured
     twice.

That is an actionable reading rather than a dead end.  It says the endothelin
hypothesis was not tested cleanly at 10 mg: the endpoint was moved by the
sodium retention and not by the receptor.  Two designs follow - co-administer
a diuretic or a lower dose and re-measure, or use a molecule that separates
coronary ETA blockade from renal sodium handling.  A larger trial of the same
10 mg would measure the same fluid.

Direction check on plasma ET-1 (bug B8): the model raises it on treatment,
as PRIZE measured, because ETA blockade also removes ETA-mediated clearance.
A model that lowered ET-1 would have predicted a win.

(p=0.014), SAQ angina frequency (p=0.027) and SAQ-7 (p=0.041) improved.

Composition from Rahman 2019: 47% CFR>=2.5; of the remaining 53%, 62%
functional and 38% structural -> 0.33 and 0.20.

stratum       weight   SAQ_pbo   SAQ_ran     dSAQ       dCFR
------------------------------------------------------------
control         0.47      98.7      98.7    +0.00     +0.243
functional      0.33      55.0      56.4    +1.41     +0.123
structural      0.20      60.8      64.6    +3.80     +0.152

CFR<2.5 stratum only (what the subgroup analysis saw):  dSAQ +2.31 U
whole randomised cohort (what the primary analysis saw): dSAQ +1.22 U
dilution: the non-CMD stratum carries 47% of the weight and contributes
  +0.00 U, so the observable effect is 53% of the mechanistic effect.

Taking 10 U as the clinically important SAQ difference (CorMicA's
stratified arm achieved +11.7 U, 95% CI 5.0-18.4):
  subgroup     +2.31 U -> below the MCID
  whole cohort +1.22 U -> below the MCID

Nothing about the drug changed between those two lines - only the
prevalence of the mechanism it targets.  Note also WHERE the effect sits:
it is largest in the structural stratum, i.e. in the patients whose minimal
resistance is actually abnormal.  A late-sodium-current inhibitor works by
lowering diastolic wall tension, which needs a filling-pressure problem to
act on; the functional endotype does not have one.  The argument is for an
interventional diagnostic procedure BEFORE randomisation, not after.

WHAT THE HYPERAEMIC-MR CUT-OFF ACTUALLY SEPARATES
A hyperaemic MR of 2.5 mmHg/(cm/s) is read as 'structural', but the model
says two different things raise it: inward remodelling and capillary
rarefaction, which fix the minimal radius, and constrictor tone that
adenosine does not fully reverse (88% of it is reversed here, calibrated,
so 12% survives).  The first is not drug-reversible on any useful
timescale; the second is reversible in weeks.  The label cannot tell them
apart, but a repeat measurement on a Rho-kinase inhibitor can:

  of 40 patients labelled structural, 0 (0%) fall below the cut-off
  after 21 days of a Rho-kinase inhibitor - i.e. their 'structural' label
  was constrictor tone, and the anatomy was never the problem.

  PREDICTION: the hyperaemic measurement should be repeated after acute
  Rho-kinase or ETA blockade before a patient is told their microvascular
  disease is structural.  It is a one-visit test and it changes the
  therapeutic class.

Ranolazine and ivabradine response by stratum (12 weeks, 20 per stratum):
  ranolazine   functional  dSAQ +1.82 +- 1.14   responders (>=10 U) 0% (n=20)
  ranolazine   structural  dSAQ +1.19 +- 0.69   responders (>=10 U) 0% (n=20)
  ivabradine   functional  dSAQ +3.68 +- 2.23   responders (>=10 U) 5% (n=20)
  ivabradine   structural  dSAQ +2.46 +- 1.35   responders (>=10 U) 0% (n=20)

Two things to read off honestly.  First, ranolazine's effect is small and
almost identical in the two strata, so the model does NOT support hyperaemic
MR as a ranolazine-responder test: it predicts that no clinically important
responder subgroup exists for this drug at this dose, and that RWISE's
subgroup signal is a real but sub-MCID effect reaching significance on a
frequency scale rather than a mechanism waiting to be exploited.  Second,
rate reduction outperforms it in both strata, which is the same ranking
section IV found and the opposite of the order in which these drugs are
usually tried in this population.

##############################################################################
SUMMARY OF THE QUANTITATIVE CLAIMS
##############################################################################
 1. Same number, different disease: functional CFR 1.83 vs structural 2.14,
    while their absolute hyperaemic velocities differ by 57% (2.41 vs
    1.54 mL/min/g).
 2. Resting MR is predicted, not fitted, wherever the controller is intact;
    the functional endotype needs an offset of 0.92, which forces resting
    oxygen extraction down to 56% of normal - refutable on one blood gas.
 3. Of the fall in subendocardial deficit from 68->55 bpm, 43% is the
    diastolic time window and 57% is lower demand.
 4. CFR ranks drugs badly even when its sign is right: rank correlation
    between dCFR and the symptom benefit is 0.60 (functional), 0.94
    (structural), 0.72 (vasospastic).  Hyperaemic MR is the index that a
    blood-pressure-lowering drug cannot fake.
 5. PRIZE: population-weighted model net -4.53 s vs observed -4.26 s
    (95% CI -19.60 to +11.06); microvascular gain alone +0.00 s, BP fall
    +2.41 s, fluid retention -6.99 s.
 6. RWISE: dSAQ +2.31 U in the CFR<2.5 stratum, +1.22 U in the whole
    cohort - 53% of the mechanistic effect survives the dilution.
 7. WARRIOR: model true hazard ratio 0.90; at 80% background contamination
    the observable ratio is 0.98. CFR +0.158 but SAQ +2.39 U over 2.5 y.
 8. CorMicA: stratified minus unstratified +5.38 U (observed +11.70 U).
 9. Virtual population: CFR<2.5 in 62% with a 49/51
    functional/structural split (observed 53%, 62/38).
10. Fixed point and fast-ODE formulations agree to 1e-6: yes
11. SAQ sensitivity is dominated by K_TTI, SAQ_SENS, K_ANG.
