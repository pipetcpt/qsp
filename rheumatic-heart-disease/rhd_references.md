# Rheumatic heart disease — references

Every PMID below was resolved against the PubMed E-utilities API rather than
written from memory, and the author, year and title shown are the ones the API
returned. This matters: the first draft of this file contained several PMIDs
recalled from memory, and checking them showed that a majority pointed at
unrelated papers.

Sections follow the structure of `rhd_qsp_model.dot` and
`rhd_mrgsolve_model.R`. Entries marked **[calibration]** supplied a number that
a parameter was fitted or anchored to; the rest are mechanism or context.

## What is NOT supported by a reference here

The following are **model outputs**, not literature findings, and are listed so
that they are not mistaken for established facts:

- The rank inversion between programme adherence and time-above-threshold.
- The 2.75 cm² crossover between the immune and autonomous arms of valve loss.
- The area-dependent optimal heart rate (108 bpm at 2.0 cm², 55 at 0.8 cm²).
- The steroid duration–response (14% of valve area saved at 6 weeks, 42% at 26).
- The claim that at the left-atrial-pressure ceiling, rate control raises
  cardiac output while leaving the measured mean gradient unchanged.
- The prediction that the size of the "nine-day window" is set entirely by how
  long the untreated host carries the organism.
