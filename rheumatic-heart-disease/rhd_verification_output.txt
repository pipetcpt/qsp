  THE MODEL'S MOST EXPOSED CLAIM.  The shear arm is calibrated to a
  MEAN progression of 0.09 cm2/yr at MVA 1.5 (Sagie 1996, Gordon 1992),
  and because the gradient rises as flow-squared-over-area-squared it
  then ACCELERATES as the valve narrows.  Sagie reported the opposite
  association -- progression was slower in the tighter valves -- so this
  is the assumption most likely to be wrong.  Sensitivity, same patient:
      KHALT  1.6  accelerating (base case)   5 y 1.58   10 y 0.81 cm2
      KHALT  6.0  strong rigidity brake      5 y 1.54   10 y 0.81 cm2
      KHALT 20.0  shear arm nearly flat      5 y 1.53   10 y 0.81 cm2
  The falsifiable statement is the SHAPE of the 5-to-10-year segment,
  which a restenosis cohort with serial echoes could settle directly.
