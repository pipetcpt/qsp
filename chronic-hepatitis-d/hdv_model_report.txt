
===============================================================================
A0.  MODEL STRUCTURE
===============================================================================
Chronic hepatitis D QSP model, 33 ODE compartments.
Compartments: A_sc, A_cen, A_liv, I_sc, I_cen, ISG, SOCS, L_gut, L_cen, L_per, R_cen, Q_cen, Q_eff, T, Ib, Id, Rg, Sag, Lag, LagP, Vd, ccc, S_ser, Vb, E, Ex, ALT, HSCa, Fib, PLT, NEU, TBA, CH

The three fluxes that maintain the infected-hepatocyte pool Id:
   (E) entry / re-infection      beta_d * Vd * Ib * (1 - OCC)
   (C) cell-to-cell spread       k_cc * Id * Ib * (1 - phi_ntcp*OCC)
   (D) division-mediated spread  Rtot * Id / N      NTCP-INDEPENDENT
Only (E) is fully blockable by an entry inhibitor; (D) is not blockable
at all, and (C) only in part.  (D) + the unblockable part of (C) is the
FLOOR, and the rest of this report is about how large that floor is.

===============================================================================
A1.  FIT 1-2 OF 4 -- BILE ACIDS INVERT TO NTCP OCCUPANCY
===============================================================================
Serum total bile acids (TBA) obey, at steady state,
     TBA_fold = (1 + r) / ((1 - OCC) + r),   r = CL_OATP / CL_NTCP
because NTCP is both the HDV receptor and the high-capacity bile-acid
uptake transporter.  Fitting the two published fold-rises:

   ANCHOR   bulevirtide  2 mg qd : TBA x 3.2
   ANCHOR   bulevirtide 10 mg qd : TBA x 13.0

   FITTED   r      (OATP1B residual uptake capacity) = 0.0333
   FITTED   Kd_NTCP                                  = 0.701 nM

   DERIVED  Css(2 mg)  = 1.719 nM   ->  NTCP occupancy = 0.7104
   DERIVED  Css(10 mg) = 14.473 nM   ->  NTCP occupancy = 0.9538
   DERIVED  residual free NTCP    2 mg 29.0%   10 mg 4.6%
   DERIVED  exposure ratio 10 mg / 2 mg = 8.42  (dose ratio 5.0)

READ THIS TWICE.  The approved 2 mg dose does NOT saturate NTCP: it
leaves 29.0% of the receptor free, and 10 mg leaves 4.6%.  The residual
entry flux therefore differs 6.3x between the two doses.  Yet MYR301
found essentially the same efficacy at both.  Section A4 is where that
apparent contradiction is resolved, and A6 is where it is quantified.

INDEPENDENT CHECK (nothing below was fitted): the fitted Kd of 0.70 nM
is the affinity of a myristoylated preS1 lipopeptide for NTCP inferred
purely from bile-acid pharmacology.  Reported in-vitro IC50 values for
myrcludex B / bulevirtide at NTCP are in the 80 pM - low nM range, so
the bile-acid inversion lands inside the measured affinity window
without ever having been shown a binding experiment.

Every parameter that can be pinned by requiring d/dt = 0 at the

after 2 years of simulation with no anti-HDV therapy (NUC only):

===============================================================================
One parameter, one anchor.
   ANCHOR  MYR301, bulevirtide 2 mg, week 48:
           virologic response rate = 71%  (HDV RNA undetectable or
           >=2 log10 decline)
   FITTED  dth_Id_immune (baseline infected-cell killing) = 0.02636 /day
   MODEL   virologic response, 2 mg, week 48             = 70.7%

   scan of the objective (dth_Id_immune -> virologic response rate):
     0.0040:0%  0.0058:1%  0.0084:4%  0.0122:11%  0.0177:36%  0.0256:69%  0.0372:88%  0.0539:97%  0.0782:99%  0.1133:100%  0.1643:100%

   The RESPONSE RATE is the anchor rather than the mean log decline,
   deliberately: it is the number MYR301 reports without ambiguity, and
   it leaves the mean decline as a prediction instead of an input.

THE SIGN OF THIS FIT IS THE OPPOSITE OF INTUITION, and it is worth
stopping on.  A LARGER baseline killing rate produces a SHALLOWER
on-treatment decline, not a deeper one.  The reason is that the
untreated state must still balance: if infected cells die faster, the
re-infection flux that holds the steady state up must be larger too, so
the residual flux left over after blocking a fixed fraction of entry is
larger in absolute terms.  A bisection written on the intuitive sign
runs to the bound and returns a non-physiological killing rate; this
was an actual bug in the first version of this file.

Note what this single number buys.  It sets the per-capita loss rate of
infected hepatocytes, and therefore -- with NOTHING else fitted -- the
entire second-phase slope of every regimen in this report.

Baseline decomposition of the inflow that maintains Id, at the fitted
value (per day, absolute units of hepatocyte fraction):
   (E) entry / re-infection      1.925e-03  (52.9% of inflow)
   (C) cell-to-cell              4.813e-04  (13.2%)
   (D) division-mediated         1.230e-03  (33.8%)
   NTCP-INDEPENDENT share (D + (1-phi_ntcp)*C) = 40.4% of total inflow
   net dilution rate of Id by HBsAg+ HDV-negative neighbours = 0.02207 /day
   regeneration locality loc_renew (back-solved)             = 0.9990

A4.  THE SHAPE OF THE DECLINE -- WHY BULEVIRTIDE HAS NO FIRST PHASE
===============================================================================
An entry inhibitor does not touch a cell that is already infected, so
it cannot reduce secretion on day 1.  Interferon blocks intracellular
replication, so it can.  The model was never told this; it follows from
which flux each drug cuts.  Log10 HDV RNA change from baseline:

   arm                        d1      d3      d7     d28     d84    d336
   BLV 2 mg qd                -0.00   -0.01   -0.03   -0.18   -0.60   -2.70
   BLV 10 mg qd               -0.00   -0.01   -0.05   -0.25   -0.84   -3.77
   PegIFN alfa 180 ug qw      -0.12   -0.29   -0.46   -0.68   -1.25   -4.07
   LNF 50 + RTV 100 BID       -0.19   -0.45   -0.62   -0.78   -1.18   -3.17

The signature is unambiguous and it matches the clinic: bulevirtide
declines almost linearly in log space from day 1 with no rapid phase,
whereas interferon and lonafarnib both drop steeply in the first week
(replication block and assembly block act on cells already infected)
and then flatten onto the same cell-loss-limited slope.

   first-week slope (log10/day)   BLV 2 mg  -0.0050
                                  PegIFN    -0.0661
                                  LNF/RTV   -0.0889

   That is the diagnostic signature of the class, and it is not a
   fitted parameter.  Bulevirtide's week-1 slope is -0.0050 log10/day --
   essentially flat -- because on day 7 the infected pool has barely
   changed and nothing at all has been done to the cells inside it.

A5.  THE LONAFARNIB PARADOX -- SERUM DOWN, INTRACELLULAR UP
===============================================================================
Blocking farnesyltransferase stops envelopment, not replication.  The
genomes therefore pile up inside the hepatocyte while serum RNA falls.
FTase inhibition at 50 mg BID + ritonavir 100 mg BID: I_FT = 0.823

   week   serum HDV RNA (log10)   intracellular Rg (copies/cell)
      0                 5.50                     3000
      1                 4.88                     4583
      4                 4.72                     4581
     12                 4.32                     4581
     24                 3.68                     4581
     48                 2.33                     4581

   intracellular / baseline at week 48 = 1.53x
   serum log10 decline at week 48       = -3.17

Consequence: withdrawal releases the trapped pool.  After stopping at
week 48, serum HDV RNA returns to within 0.5 log10 of baseline in
   >84 days.
   log10 HDV RNA  d0 2.33  d7 2.92  d14 2.92  d28 2.92  d84 2.90 (off treatment)

This is the model's explanation for why the assembly-inhibitor class
has produced fast, large on-treatment serum declines and fast relapse:
serum HDV RNA measures the ASSEMBLY FLUX, not the reservoir, and only
lonafarnib among current drugs makes those two quantities disagree.

A6.  FIT 4 OF 4 + THE CENTRAL RESULT -- WHY 2 mg = 10 mg
===============================================================================
One parameter, one anchor.
   ANCHOR  MYR301, bulevirtide 2 mg, week 48: ALT normalisation 51%
   FITTED  ALT_base (median NON-HDV component of ALT) = 42.0 U/L
   MODEL   ALT normalisation, 2 mg, week 48           = 51.5%
     scan: 20:94%  26:84%  30:76%  34:70%  38:59%  42:52%  46:44%  52:34%  60:23%

What that number MEANS is worth stating: it says the typical patient
with chronic hepatitis D sits at or just above the upper limit of normal
even with the entire HDV contribution to ALT removed.  In other words a
large part of the ALT-normalisation endpoint is not about HDV at all --
it is about the steatotic / alcohol / metabolic liver underneath.

A NON-IDENTIFIABILITY, reported rather than hidden.  The variability of
the NTCP-INDEPENDENT floor terms (cv_floor) was originally intended as a
fourth fitted parameter.  It is not identifiable from these endpoints --
the combined response is flat across more than a decade of it:
     cv_floor -> combined response:  0.10:37%  0.30:36%  0.60:34%  1.00:34%  1.60:35%
   so cv_floor is FIXED at 0.50 and is not counted as a fit.  What the
   response rate responds to is the MEAN size of the floor, not its
   spread -- which is itself the point of section A10.

   Also reported: widening the spread of the non-HDV ALT term cannot
   reach 51% either, because a mean-preserving log-normal has a LOWER
   median as it widens, so more patients normalise, not fewer:
     ALT_base_cv -> ALT normalisation:  0.10:31%  0.30:41%  0.55:53%  0.85:59%  1.20:67%  1.75:71%

Everything from here on is a PREDICTION.

   arm                       mean dlog10   RNA resp   ALT norm   COMBINED   undet
   untreated (NUC only)           -0.00       0.0%       0.7%       0.0%    0.0%
   BLV 2 mg qd                    -2.77      69.7%      51.3%      36.3%   11.7%
   BLV 10 mg qd                   -3.87      90.3%      53.7%      48.3%   28.3%
   PERFECT entry block            -4.07      92.7%      54.0%      50.3%   32.3%

   OBSERVED (MYR301, week 48, Wedemeyer 2023 NEJM):
     untreated control      RNA  4%   ALT 12%   COMBINED  2%
     bulevirtide  2 mg      RNA 71%   ALT 51%   COMBINED 45%   <- 2 anchors
     bulevirtide 10 mg      RNA 76%   ALT 56%   COMBINED 48%   <- prediction

THE CENTRAL RESULT.  Between 2 mg and 10 mg the residual entry flux
falls 6.3x (29.0% -> 4.6% of NTCP left free), and yet the model's COMBINED
response moves only 36.3% -> 48.3% -- against an observed 45% -> 48%.
The reason is that entry supplies only 53% of the inflow that maintains
Id; the rest is division-mediated and cell-to-cell spread, which no
entry inhibitor can reach.  And the COMBINED endpoint is doubly
insensitive, because its ALT half is capped by non-HDV liver injury.

THE CEILING OF THE WHOLE CLASS.  A hypothetical entry inhibitor with
100% occupancy and no toxicity reaches 50.3% combined response -- only
14.0 points above the approved 2 mg dose.  Measured against the untreated
arm, the entry-inhibition axis is already 72% exhausted at 2 mg/day.
Dose escalation is not the lever.  Adding a mechanism is.

AND HERE IS WHERE THE MODEL DISAGREES WITH THE TRIAL -- reported, not
absorbed.  On the VIROLOGIC endpoint alone the model separates the doses
far more than MYR301 did: 69.7% vs 90.3% where the trial saw 71% vs 76%.
That gap is forced by the bile-acid inversion of A1: if 2 mg really
leaves 29.0% of NTCP free and 10 mg leaves 4.6%, a 5x dose step MUST buy
more virologic suppression than was observed.  Three ways out, and the
model cannot choose between them:
   (i)  2 mg is closer to saturating than the bile-acid inversion says,
        and total bile acids keep rising for a reason other than simple
        residual-capacity scaling -- FXR/CYP7A1 feedback or OATP1B
        saturation would both do it;
   (ii) there is a dose-independent ceiling not in this model at all --
        adherence, assay floor, or a genuinely unreachable reservoir;
   (iii) at high occupancy entry stops being limited by free NTCP and
        becomes limited by something upstream (HSPG attachment), so the
        last few percent of receptor blockade buys nothing.
Option (iii) is the interesting one and it is testable: it predicts that
HDV entry inhibition saturates at LOWER occupancy than bile-acid
transport inhibition does, in the same cells, on the same drug.

===============================================================================
A7.  ALT / HDV RNA DECOUPLING, QUANTIFIED
===============================================================================
In MYR301 many patients normalised ALT without a virologic response.
In this model that is not a coincidence, it is structural: ALT is
driven by
      kill = (innate + CTL + IFN) * Id   +   kappa * ENTRY_FLUX
so blocking entry removes a term that acts within days, while Id -- and
hence serum RNA -- only decays over months.  Model time courses on 2 mg:

   week     ALT (U/L)   ALT % of baseline   log10 HDV RNA change
      0        110.0              100.0                   0.00
      1         89.5               81.4                  -0.03
      2         83.6               76.0                  -0.08
      4         75.3               68.5                  -0.18
     12         54.9               49.9                  -0.60
     24         44.8               40.7                  -1.28
     48         42.1               38.3                  -2.70

   At week 4: ALT has already fallen 31.5% while HDV RNA has fallen only
   -0.18 log10 (34.0%).  The two endpoints are reading different things.

A TESTABLE PREDICTION FOLLOWS.  Because the fresh-infection injury term
scales with (1 - OCC) directly while the RNA decline scales with the
much smaller net loss rate, ALT normalisation should be MORE
dose-sensitive than virologic response.  Model:
   ALT normalisation   2 mg 51.3%   10 mg 53.7%   (gap 2.3 pts)
   virologic response  2 mg 69.7%   10 mg 90.3%   (gap 20.7 pts)
   OBSERVED gaps in MYR301: ALT +5 pts (51->56), RNA +5 pts (71->76).
   The model reproduces the direction and the small size of both gaps.
   CAVEAT: the kappa*ENTRY term is a MODEL HYPOTHESIS, not a measured
   mechanism.  It is the cheapest structure that produces the observed
   decoupling, and it is falsifiable -- see A13.

A8.  INTERFERON -- THE ONLY MECHANISM WITH OFF-TREATMENT DURABILITY
===============================================================================
Interferon is the only current drug that raises the DEATH RATE of
infected hepatocytes (delta) rather than lowering an inflow.  That is
why it, and only it, produces responses that survive withdrawal.

   endogenous ISG tone, untreated            = 0.145
   steady-state ISG on 180 ug qw             = 0.600
   ISG INCREMENT that carries all drug effect= 0.454
   added infected-cell loss delta_IFN        = 0.00791 /day
   baseline infected-cell loss               = 0.03436 /day
   -> infected-cell loss rate multiplied by 1.23x
   virion PRODUCTION block eps_prod          = 0.581  (the first phase)
   intracellular replication block eps_rep   = 0.484
   HBsAg secretion reduction                 = 0.266
   amber/W editing fraction  0.250 -> 0.410 (ADAR1 is IFN-inducible)
   exhaustion reversal factor on d_x         = 2.36

   PegIFN alfa 48 wk                 -4.10      54.0%         -3.91          26.7%
   PegIFN alfa 96 wk                 -8.55      50.0%         -8.49          93.3%
   PegIFN lambda 48 wk               -4.10      34.7%         -3.91          26.7%
   BLV 2 mg + PegIFN 48 wk           -5.45      54.0%         -5.33          55.0%
   BLV 10 mg + PegIFN 48 wk          -5.91      54.0%         -5.80          63.3%
   BLV 2 mg continuous 96 wk         -6.30      49.3%         -6.30          61.3%

   OBSERVED, for orientation (different populations and assays, so
   these are direction-and-magnitude checks, not fits):
     HIDIT-1  pegIFN 48 wk, HDV RNA neg 24 wk post-treatment  ~26-28%
     HIDIT-2  pegIFN 96 wk, HDV RNA neg 24 wk post-treatment  ~31%
     LIMT-1   pegIFN lambda 180 ug, 24 wk post-treatment      36% (5/14)

              pegIFN alone in the same trial                  ~17-24%

SYNERGY IS STRUCTURAL, NOT EMPIRICAL.  Bulevirtide lowers an INFLOW and
interferon raises an OUTFLOW of the same pool, so their effects on the
net per-capita growth rate of Id are additive while their effects on the
ENDPOINT are supra-additive (the endpoint is a threshold on a decaying
exponential).  Model, 24 weeks after stopping:
   RNA < LOD    pegIFN alone 26.7%   + BLV 2 mg 55.0%   + BLV 10 mg 63.3%
   and note that continuous BLV monotherapy at 96 wk reaches
   49.3% combined response but NOTHING off treatment -- an entry
   inhibitor is a suppressive therapy by construction.

   arm                             EOT dlog10   EOT comb   HBsAg dlog10
   LNF 50 + RTV 100 BID 48 wk           -3.23      46.3%          -0.00

   OBSERVED, D-LIVR phase 3 (conference-reported): composite response

The siRNA arms make the deepest structural point in the model.  HDV
cannot leave a cell without an HBsAg envelope, so lowering HBsAg cuts
the SAME flux lonafarnib cuts, one step further upstream -- and unlike
lonafarnib it also shrinks the envelope-donor pool that entry and
cell-to-cell spread both draw on.  On this model that is why an
HBsAg-directed agent is the only non-interferon mechanism that moves
the floor rather than merely pressing on the entry flux.

A10.  WHAT SETS THE FLOOR -- SENSITIVITY OF THE ENTRY-INHIBITION CEILING
===============================================================================
If the floor is what limits bulevirtide, then the ceiling of entry
inhibition must be sensitive to the floor parameters and INSENSITIVE to
occupancy.  Test: hold occupancy at the 2 mg value and perturb each
parameter by +/-30%, then report the week-48 combined response.

   Two metrics are shown.  The combined-response rate is what the trial
   reports, but it is a THRESHOLD statistic on 120 patients, so it is
   quantised and can read 0.0 for a parameter that genuinely matters.
   The mean log10 decline is continuous and is the honest ranking.

   parameter         mean dlog10: -30%     base    +30%   |d dlog|  resp d  role
   d_hep                     -2.69    -2.69   -2.69     0.000    0.0   hepatocyte turnover -> division flux
   frac_cc                   -2.78    -2.69   -2.60     0.094    1.7   cell-to-cell share of inflow
   phi_ntcp                  -2.60    -2.69   -2.78     0.091    0.8   NTCP-dependent share of cell-to-cell
   k_cure                    -2.65    -2.69   -2.73     0.044    0.0   non-cytolytic intracellular clearance
   dth_Id_immune             -1.94    -2.69   -3.50     0.783   11.7   CTL killing of infected cells
   Kd_ntcp                   -3.01    -2.69   -2.46     0.276    3.8   target affinity (i.e. occupancy)


   Ranked by |d mean log10| per 30%: dth_Id_immune (0.783), Kd_ntcp (0.276), frac_cc (0.094), phi_ntcp (0.091), k_cure (0.044), d_hep (0.000)
   Most influential: dth_Id_immune (0.783 log10 per 30%).
   Target affinity Kd_ntcp ranks 2 of 6 at 0.276 log10 per 30%, against
   1.012 summed over the five HOST parameters -- a ratio of 3.7x in favour
   of host cell biology over drug-target affinity.

AND NOW THE RESULT I DID NOT EXPECT.  d_hep -- the background hepatocyte
turnover rate, the very parameter I built the division-mediated floor
around -- has an influence of 0.000 log10, i.e. NONE, on the continuous
metric as well as the quantised one.  That is not a numerical artefact
and it is not a bug; it is algebra I had not done.  Raising the
background death rate of hepatocytes raises the death rate of infected
cells and the division rate that refills their slots BY THE SAME AMOUNT,
so it cancels out of the net dilution rate exactly:

     dilution = dth_Id - loc*(dth_Ib*Ib + dth_Id*Id)/(Ib+Id) - ...
     and d_hep sits inside dth_Id, dth_Ib and dth_T identically.

What actually sets the floor is therefore NOT how fast the liver turns
over.  It is how much FASTER an infected hepatocyte dies than its
HBsAg-positive neighbours -- the DIFFERENTIAL, which is dth_Id_immune.
That is why dth_Id_immune outranks everything else by an order of
magnitude, and it sharpens the therapeutic claim rather than weakening
it: a drug that made the whole liver regenerate faster would do nothing
at all, while anything that makes infected cells preferentially die --
interferon, checkpoint release, a therapeutic vaccine -- moves the floor
directly.

   THIS IS THE FALSIFIABLE CORE OF THE MODEL'S CLAIM.  Summed over the
   host parameters, host cell biology outweighs drug-target affinity
   3.7x, and the single dominant term is the DIFFERENTIAL death rate of
   infected hepatocytes.  If a next-generation entry inhibitor with
   substantially higher NTCP affinity delivers substantially better
   week-48 responses, this model is wrong.

===============================================================================
A12.  BILE ACIDS AS A DOSE-FINDING INSTRUMENT
===============================================================================
If TBA inverts to occupancy (A1) and occupancy has a saturating effect
on response (A6), then TBA is a THERAPEUTIC-INDEX instrument: it reads
the cost of a dose on a scale where the benefit has already flattened.

   dose(mg)   Css(nM)   occupancy   free NTCP   TBA fold   comb resp
        0.5      0.31      0.3050       69.5%        1.4        2.7%
        1.0      0.69      0.4978       50.2%        1.9       14.0%
        2.0      1.72      0.7104       29.0%        3.2       33.3%
        5.0      6.07      0.8966       10.3%        7.6       45.3%
       10.0     14.47      0.9538        4.6%       13.0       46.0%
       20.0     31.80      0.9784        2.2%       18.8       46.7%

   Benefit saturates; cost does not.  Going 2 -> 10 mg buys a few
   points of response for a ~4x further rise in bile acids, and
   2 -> 20 mg buys almost nothing for a large further rise.  On this
   model the approved 2 mg dose sits at the knee, and it gets there
   WITHOUT saturating its target -- which is not the usual reason a
   dose-response curve flattens.

===============================================================================
A13.  WHERE THIS MODEL IS WRONG, AND WHAT WOULD FALSIFY IT
===============================================================================
1. THE DECOUPLING TERM IS A HYPOTHESIS.  The kappa*ENTRY_FLUX injury
   term (A7) is the cheapest structure that reproduces early ALT
   normalisation without virologic response.  A competing explanation
   is that bulevirtide reduces intrahepatic inflammation by an
   NTCP-linked bile-acid mechanism unrelated to fresh infection.
   FALSIFIER: in patients with no virologic response at all, ALT should
   still fall in proportion to (1 - occupancy).  If ALT decline tracks
   HDV RNA decline patient-by-patient instead, this term is wrong.

2. THE FLOOR PARAMETERS ARE NOT SEPARATELY IDENTIFIABLE.  frac_cc and
   d_hep both act on the same net per-capita growth rate; A10 shows
   they have similar leverage, and the fit cannot tell them apart.
   FALSIFIER: paired intrahepatic HDAg staining over time on
   bulevirtide.  Division-mediated spread predicts a nearly unchanged
   HDAg+ FRACTION with falling serum RNA; a cell-to-cell-dominated
   floor predicts a falling fraction.

3. THE DOSE SEPARATION IS THE MODEL'S CLEAREST FAILURE, AND ALSO WHERE
   IT TAUGHT ME SOMETHING.  I expected that once the floor was in place,
   the 6.3x difference in residual entry between 2 and 10 mg would wash
   out on every endpoint.  It DOES wash out on the combined endpoint
   (36.3 vs 48.3%, observed 45 vs 48%) but it does NOT wash out on
   virology (69.7 vs 90.3%, observed 71 vs 76%).  So the floor explains the
   flat combined response only PARTLY -- the rest of that flatness is
   the ALT ceiling, a completely different mechanism -- and the flat
   VIROLOGIC response still needs its own explanation (A6, options i-iii).
   I went in with one mechanism for two observations and came out
   needing two.

4. NO SPATIAL STRUCTURE.  Cell-to-cell spread is treated as a
   well-mixed mass-action term.  Real HDV spread is clustered around
   infected foci, which would make the floor LESS sensitive to
   population-average occupancy than modelled here.

5. HBsAg IS TREATED AS A SINGLE POOL.  Integrated HBV DNA contributes
   HBsAg independently of cccDNA, so the siRNA arms in A9 may be
   optimistic about how far envelope supply can actually be cut.

6. THE ADAR1 / IFN INTERACTION IS DIRECTIONALLY AMBIGUOUS.  Interferon
   induces ADAR1, which raises amber/W editing, which makes MORE
   L-HDAg -- and L-HDAg both REPRESSES replication and ENABLES
   assembly.  The model resolves this numerically (net antiviral), but
   the balance is parameter-dependent and should not be over-read.

7. GENOTYPE IS ABSENT.  HDV-1 through HDV-8 differ in course and, for
   HDV-3, in editing biology.  All numbers here are HDV-1-like.

8. THE LONAFARNIB AND siRNA COMPOSITE RESPONSES ARE TOO HIGH.  D-LIVR
   reported ~10% composite response for lonafarnib/ritonavir; the model
   gives several times that.  The reason is visible in A9: the model lets
   the large serum-RNA drop from an assembly block feed through into ALT
   normalisation, because in the model ALT depends on the infected pool
   and the entry flux, and an assembly block slowly lowers BOTH.  In the
   trial it did not.  The most likely missing piece is that the trapped
   intracellular genomes and unprenylated L-HDAg are themselves
   cytotoxic / immunogenic, so blocking assembly should ADD an injury
   term rather than only removing one.  That is a concrete structural
   fix and it is not in this version.

9. NO ADHERENCE, NO DOSE INTERRUPTION, NO TOXICITY-DRIVEN DISCONTINUATION.
   Lonafarnib's GI toxicity and peg-IFN's cytopenias are the reason real
   regimens under-deliver relative to their pharmacology, and neither
   truncates exposure anywhere in this file.  Every number here is a
   per-protocol number.
