A.  THE TWO THRESHOLDS THE WHOLE MODEL TURNS ON
==============================================================================
  measured inputs   doubling time (untreated)        1.20 d
                    decline half-life (on drug)      2.40 d
  derived           r0   = ln2/DBL0              = 0.5776 /d
                    DELI = ln2/THALF_TX          = 0.2888 /d
                    KPROD = r0 + DELI            = 0.8664 /d

  THRESHOLD 1 (drug)   e* = r0/KPROD          = 0.6667
     -> a regimen leaving e below 0.667 has a POSITIVE exponent and will
        break through no matter how the label is written.
  THRESHOLD 2 (immune) E8* = r0/KE8            =   4.81 cells/uL
     -> at or above this CMV-specific CD8 count the exponent is negative
        with NO drug present.  This is the endpoint of the illness.

E.  WHY A PERFECTLY WORKING TERMINASE INHIBITOR BARELY MOVES PLASMA DNA
==============================================================================
  measured DNAemia = encapsidated virion DNA (Vv) + DNA released from
  dying infected cells (Vl).  At untreated quasi-steady state the model's
  split is Vl:Vv = 2.0:1, i.e. 67% of what the PCR reports is not
  virion.  Polymerase inhibitors cut both terms.  Terminase / kinase
  inhibitors cut only Vv and, because unit-length genomes are no longer
  excised from the concatemer, RAISE the DNA per dying cell by AMPPK.

  instantaneous change in measured DNAemia at FIXED infected-cell pool:
  regimen                       e_pol   e_pack  Vmeas rel log10 drop
  no drug                       0.000    0.000      1.000      -0.00
  ganciclovir 900 BID           0.970    0.000      0.030       1.52
  foscarnet 90 q12h             0.719    0.000      0.281       0.55
  letermovir 480 od             0.000    0.969      1.000      -0.00
  maribavir 400 BID             0.000    0.792      1.000      -0.00

  AMPPK sensitivity (the one parameter here with no human measurement):
     AMPPK LTV Vmeas rel   log10 drop
      0.00        0.677         0.17
      0.25        0.838         0.08
      0.50        1.000        -0.00
      1.00        1.323        -0.12
  -> across the whole plausible range the immediate fall is <= 0.2 log10.
     The clinical corollary is not a nuance: on-letermovir DNAemia is not
     an early efficacy read-out, and letermovir is not a treatment drug.

  READ THIS CAREFULLY.  The rebound day tracks the STOP day exactly
  (60->78, 100->119, 150->169, 200->219 -- always 18-20 days later), so a longer course does NOT prevent
  the episode -- it postpones it.  The only reason P(disease) still falls
  is that the SAME rebound meets a less immunosuppressed host (ISI 1.91
  at day 60 vs 1.19 at day 300).  E8 at the moment of stopping barely
  moves (0.05 -> 0.12 against E8* = 4.81), because the drug removed the
  antigen that would have built it.
  HONEST MISS: IMPACT reported CMV disease 36.8% (100 d) vs 16.1%
  (200 d).  This model gives 42.2% vs 35.9% -- the DIRECTION is right and
  the MAGNITUDE is about a third of the observed effect.  The model is
  therefore missing something that makes a long course work, and the next
  section is the most plausible candidate it can test.

==============================================================================
J.  THE TRAINING-DOSE U-CURVE: IS FULLY SUPPRESSIVE PROPHYLAXIS OPTIMAL?
==============================================================================
  If E8 expansion is antigen-driven, then a prophylaxis regimen that
  suppresses COMPLETELY buys nothing durable, and one that suppresses NOT
  AT ALL causes disease.  The model can be asked where the optimum is.
  Adherence is used as the knob because it maps monotonically onto e.

   adhere    e(WT)     vs e* |   E8@100  Pdis100 |   E8@200  Pdis200 selWin200
     1.00    0.890     above |     0.07    0.422 |     0.09    0.359      200
     0.70    0.798     above |     0.07    0.422 |     0.09    0.359      199
     0.50    0.668     above |     0.07    0.419 |     0.10    0.347      110
     0.45    0.620     BELOW |     0.08    0.391 |     0.17    0.242       22
     0.40    0.563     BELOW |     0.27    0.231 |     0.38    0.163        2
     0.35    0.497     BELOW |     1.52    0.150 |     0.65    0.190        0
     0.30    0.420     BELOW |     2.06    0.296 |     1.14    0.305        0
     0.25    0.335     BELOW |     2.50    0.433 |     1.96    0.432        0
     0.20    0.244     BELOW |     2.94    0.510 |     3.00    0.507        0
     0.00    0.000     BELOW |     3.99    0.600 |     4.31    0.600        0

  The 200-day curve is NOT monotone.  Its minimum sits at adherence
  0.40, i.e. e = 0.563, which is BELOW the e* = 0.667 that a prophylaxis
  regimen is designed to clear: P(disease) 0.163 against 0.359 at full
  adherence.  The mechanism is the coupling and nothing else -- partial
  suppression leaves enough antigen to train E8 while keeping the load
  below the disease threshold.
  DO NOT READ THIS AS A DOSING RECOMMENDATION.  Three reasons.  (i) The
  selection-window column collapses to 2 d at the optimum, but that is a
  definitional artefact and not reassurance: selection requires the WILD
  TYPE to be suppressed, and below e* it is not -- so what a sub-threshold
  regimen actually buys is continuous wild-type replication, which is the
  substrate mutants arise FROM even though it is not the condition that
  selects them.  (ii) One deterministic patient; a real cohort spreads
  across this curve and some of it lands on the rising limb, where the
  disease rate is worse than full suppression.  (iii) The position of the
  minimum is set by KAG, the antigen half-saturation for CD8 expansion,
  which is the least well measured parameter in the model.  Sensitivity:
         KAG argmin adh Pdis at min Pdis at adh 1
        0.02       0.40      0.054      0.307
        0.05       0.40      0.163      0.359
        0.12       0.40      0.326      0.420
        0.30       0.40      0.524      0.526
  -> the U-shape survives the whole range, but the depth of the minimum
     does not: it is a qualitative prediction, not a quantitative one.

==============================================================================
H.  HONEST LIMITATIONS OF THIS RUN
==============================================================================
  * Single deterministic virtual patient per arm.  The model has no
    inter-individual variability, so every incidence quoted above is a
    hazard-function probability for one parameter set, NOT a trial rate.
  * AMPPK (concatemer amplification when packaging is blocked) has no direct
    human measurement.  Section E shows the whole plausible range; the
    conclusion (no early DNAemia drop on letermovir) is insensitive to it.
  * UL54 polymerase mutants are in the map and the cross-resistance table but
    are not a separate ODE strain.
  * The rejection and eGFR arms are reduced-form hazard surrogates, not a
    mechanistic alloimmune model; they are included because dose reduction for
    neutropenia is a real driver of rejection, not because they are calibrated.
  * Costs are order-of-magnitude list prices for illustration only.
