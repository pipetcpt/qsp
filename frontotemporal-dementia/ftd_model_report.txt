==============================================================================
FRONTOTEMPORAL DEMENTIA QSP MODEL — REFERENCE IMPLEMENTATION REPORT
49 ODE states | dependency-free RK4 | every number below is computed
==============================================================================

Trial scenarios do NOT start from hand-set 'symptomatic baseline'
initial conditions. Each genotype is run from a clinically silent
carrier at age 30 until its CDR plus NACC-FTLD SB reaches 5.5, and
that state becomes the trial baseline. Treated and untreated arms
therefore share one continuous trajectory, and every baseline
biomarker value is EARNED by the model rather than assumed.

==============================================================================
A. ONSET AGE AS A PREDICTION, NOT AN OFFSET
==============================================================================
A constant genetic deficit cannot by itself explain why a lifelong
carrier stays well for decades, so the model carries an explicit AGE
GATE (declining autophagic/lysosomal capacity and microglial priming,
(age/62)^5). With it, onset AGE becomes an output. Observed values are
cohort MEANS from Moore 2020 Lancet Neurol (PMID 31810826), n=3403
carriers from 1492 families; the sporadic figure is the usual ~58 y.

  genotype      predicted onset   observed   error
  ----------------------------------------------------
  MAPT                  50.5 y        49.5 y    +1.0 y
  C9orf72               54.3 y        58.2 y    -3.9 y
  GRN                   55.8 y        61.3 y    -5.5 y
  sporadic              59.2 y        58.0 y    +1.2 y

  MAPT lands within ~1 year of the reported mean and sporadic within
  ~1.5. C9orf72 is ~4 y early. GRN is ~5.5 y early, and there the
  reason is STRUCTURAL rather than a bad parameter: in this model PGRN
  haploinsufficiency is a damage driver acting from birth, so it can
  only ever move onset EARLIER than sporadic disease. The measured
  ordering is the opposite — GRN onset 61.3 y is LATER than sporadic
  bvFTD at ~58 y. NO monotone version of this model can produce that
  ordering. Two candidate resolutions:
    (i)  lifelong haploinsufficiency is developmentally compensated,
         so the deficit is not rate-limiting until a second hit —
         consistent with GRN's incomplete penetrance and its strong
         dependence on modifiers such as TMEM106B;
    (ii) the comparison is confounded by ascertainment: GRN carriers
         are found by GENOTYPE regardless of severity, while
         'sporadic bvFTD' is a clinically defined group enriched for
         aggressive pathology. The two cohorts are not exchangeable.

  The model does reproduce the modifier DIRECTION: the protective
  TMEM106B haplotype shifts predicted GRN onset from 55.8 y to 57.1 y (later),
  which is the direction reported for rs1990622. Note the model puts
  TMEM106B on the lysosomal/age-vulnerability axis and NOT on PGRN
  levels, which agrees with the 7071-person biofluid meta-analysis
  (PMID 38539243) finding no association between plasma PGRN and
  rs1990622 genotype. That was not fitted; it fell out of the wiring.

B. NATURAL HISTORY vs PUBLISHED ANCHORS
==============================================================================
Measured over the 2 years following each genotype's own onset state.

  genotype     slope/y   plasma NfL   CSF NfL   atrophy%/y   NPI
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  MAPT           1.58         42.7      2484        2.16     22
  C9orf72        1.67         59.5      3402        2.20     25
  GRN            1.76         57.5      3290        2.25     24
  sporadic       1.69         56.1      3214        2.18     23

  anchors: CDR+NACC-FTLD SB slope 1.5-2.5 /y symptomatic;
           plasma NfL 50-80 pg/mL (control ~10); CSF NfL 3000-5000
           (control ~700); frontal atrophy 2-3 %/y (control ~0.5);
           NPI total 20-40 in symptomatic bvFTD.

  Presymptomatic lead time — does NfL rise before the clinical score?

    threshold on plasma NfL   age crossed   lead time before CDR=3
    ------------------------------------------------------------
     1.5x control            38.9 y                15.3 y
     2.0x control            43.3 y                10.8 y
     3.0x control            48.2 y                 5.9 y
     4.0x control            51.7 y                 2.5 y
     5.0x control            54.7 y                -0.5 y
    CDR+NACC-FTLD SB reaches 3 at age 54.2.  Reported lead time ~1-2 y.

    THIS IS A FAILURE, and it is the SAME failure as the GRN onset
    error above. The model has no genuinely compensated latent phase:
    damage begins at t=0 and accumulates monotonically, so NfL starts
    drifting up decades early and only a very high threshold (~4-5x
    control) recovers a plausible 1-2 y lead time. Real presymptomatic
    carriers hold near-normal NfL and then rise steeply near
    conversion. Reproducing that needs a compensated regime with a
    threshold-crossing collapse, which this monotone structure lacks.

C. THE CENTRAL ANALYTIC RESULT AND ITS NUMERICAL VERIFICATION
==============================================================================
Sortilin share of PLASMA PGRN clearance    f_plasma = 0.6300
Sortilin share of CSF    PGRN clearance    f_CSF    = 0.7800
Sortilin share of LYSOSOMAL PGRN delivery  p        = 0.5000  <-- NOT MEASURED

Sortilin is BOTH the clearance route that sets extracellular PGRN AND
a delivery route filling the lysosomal pool where PGRN acts. Blocking
it raises the ligand and removes a delivery route simultaneously. At
steady state:
    CSF PGRN fold-rise       = 1 / (1 - f*theta)
    lysosomal delivery ratio = (1 - p*theta) / (1 - f*theta)

  latozinemab 60 mg/kg q4w, trough at 96 wk:
    plasma      2804 nM      CSF   5.77 nM   (CSF/plasma 0.206%, reported ~0.1-0.3%)
    sortilin occupancy: peripheral 0.9996   CNS 0.6579

  quantity                 numeric    algebraic   residual
  --------------------------------------------------------
  plasma PGRN fold-rise     2.7011      2.7011     0.0000
  CSF PGRN fold-rise        2.0758      2.0541     0.0217
  LYSOSOMAL PGRN ratio      1.4016      1.3784     0.0232

  Residuals are small and arise only because occupancy oscillates
  within the dosing interval while the algebra assumes it constant.
  The 49-state integration and the closed form agree, so what follows
  is a property of the STRUCTURE, not of one solver run.

D. THE BIOMARKER / TARGET DISSOCIATION
==============================================================================
Latozinemab 60 mg/kg IV q4w in a symptomatic GRN carrier, 96 weeks.

  compartment       baseline   on drug     fold
  ------------------------------------------------
  PLASMA PGRN          35.0%     94.5%    2.70x
  CSF    PGRN          35.0%     72.7%    2.08x
  LYSOSOMAL PGRN       35.0%     49.1%    1.40x
  (% of the wild-type level for that compartment)

  The registrational biomarker is restored essentially to the control
  range (95% of control). The pool where progranulin actually works
  reaches only 49% of wild type.

  96-week outcomes, treated vs untreated:

  endpoint                  untreated    treated   difference
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  CDR+NACC-FTLD SB              8.719      8.698      -0.021
  NPI total                    26.341     26.175      -0.166
  plasma NfL pg/mL             66.632     64.662      -1.970
  CSF NfL pg/mL              3790.303   3682.638    -107.665
  frontal volume %             73.360     73.371      +0.011
  synaptic density              0.621      0.626      +0.005
  CSF C1q au                    0.371      0.331      -0.039
  CSF C3 au                     0.370      0.330      -0.040
  CSF BMP au                   58.571     39.042     -19.529
  lysosomal function            0.515      0.717      +0.202

  Untreated 96-week CDR progression = +3.22 points.
  Treatment effect                  = -0.0210 points  (0.65% slowing).

  A fraction-of-one-percent effect on a 0-24 scale over 96 weeks is
  far below what any feasible trial can resolve. The model therefore
  PREDICTS a failed clinical endpoint sitting on top of a clean,
  unambiguous biomarker win — which is what the anti-sortilin phase 3
  programme reported. Note the C1q/C3 and BMP columns: the PD effects
  are real and measurable. Target engagement was never the problem.

E. THE SIGN FLIP: SWEEPING THE PARAMETER NOBODY HAS MEASURED
==============================================================================
p = sortilin share of lysosomal PGRN delivery. Everything else fixed.
Prediction: the sign flips exactly at p = f_CSF = 0.780, at ANY
dose, and the theta->1 ceiling is (1-p)/(1-f_CSF).

     p    LYSO pool  ratio  ceiling  plasma PGRN   dCDR@96wk  verdict
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  0.000     73.5%   2.100   4.545       94.5%    -0.0536  BENEFIT
  0.200     63.7%   1.820   3.636       94.5%    -0.0402  BENEFIT
  0.400     53.9%   1.541   2.727       94.5%    -0.0273  BENEFIT
  0.500     49.1%   1.402   2.273       94.5%    -0.0210  BENEFIT
  0.600     44.2%   1.262   1.818       94.5%    -0.0147  BENEFIT
  0.700     39.3%   1.122   1.364       94.5%    -0.0084  BENEFIT
  0.780     35.4%   1.011   1.000       94.5%    -0.0033  NEUTRAL  <== break-even
  0.850     32.0%   0.913   0.682       94.5%    +0.0012  HARM
  0.950     27.1%   0.773   0.227       94.5%    +0.0078  HARM

  Read the plasma-PGRN column: 94.5% at EVERY value of p. The drug
  looks identical on its registrational biomarker whether it is
  helping, doing nothing, or making the target pool worse. That is
  this model's sharpest claim — the biomarker is mathematically blind
  to the parameter that decides the sign of the effect, and no amount
  of dose escalation changes that, because raising occupancy raises
  the ligand and removes delivery capacity in lockstep.

==============================================================================
F. GENE THERAPY IS NOT SUBJECT TO THE CEILING
==============================================================================
AAV-GRN raises PRODUCTION, so lysosomal delivery scales with transgene
output and no receptor is removed. Same patient, same 96 weeks:

  therapy         plasma   CSF    LYSOSOMAL   dCDR      dBMP
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  untreated        35.0%   35.0%      35.0%   +0.000     +0.00
  latozinemab      94.5%   72.7%      49.1%   -0.021    -19.53
  AAV-GRN single   80.3%   80.3%      80.3%   -0.060    -26.96

  Gene therapy raises all three pools TOGETHER, because it adds
  production instead of blocking a receptor. Its lysosomal
  restoration is 1.64x the antibody's, and its predicted clinical
  effect 2.9x — from a LOWER plasma PGRN level. A plasma-PGRN
  biomarker would have RANKED THESE TWO THERAPIES BACKWARDS.

==============================================================================
G. WHAT TO MEASURE INSTEAD, AND THE RIVAL HYPOTHESIS
==============================================================================
CSF BMP (a lysosomal lipid) is in the model as the readout plasma
PGRN cannot be. Across the p sweep above it moves with the LYSOSOMAL
pool, not with the plasma pool — so it separates the cases plasma
PGRN cannot. It is attenuated, though, because lysosomal function in
this model also sees the extracellular pool (weight 0.2), so BMP is a
better discriminator than plasma PGRN without being a perfect one.

If progranulin acts mainly EXTRACELLULARLY rather than in the
lysosome, the elevated CSF pool IS the therapeutic signal and
blocking sortilin is straightforwardly good. Same drug, same doses,
action weights swapped:

  hypothesis                    dCDR@96wk    dNfL    dLYSO fn
  ----------------------------------------------------------
  lysosomal action (default)      -0.0210    -1.97    +0.2021
  extracellular action            -0.0232    -2.55    +0.2695

  The two hypotheses differ 1.1x in predicted clinical benefit and are
  INDISTINGUISHABLE by plasma PGRN. They are distinguishable by
  downstream lysosomal chemistry. That is a cheap, falsifiable
  experiment the model recommends running before the next trial.

H. SYMPTOMATIC PHARMACOLOGY, INCLUDING THE NULL/HARM ARM
==============================================================================
96 weeks in a symptomatic GRN carrier.

  arm                     NPI    dNPI     CDR    dCDR    ACh tone
  ----------------------------------------------------------------
  untreated               26.3    +0.0    8.72  +0.000     0.950
  SSRI 30 mg/d            20.2    -6.2    8.72  +0.000     0.950
  trazodone 150 mg/d      20.5    -5.8    8.72  +0.000     0.950
  SSRI + trazodone        15.1   -11.2    8.72  +0.000     0.950
  donepezil 10 mg/d       29.5    +3.2    8.72  +0.000     1.663

  trazodone dNPI = -5.8  (anchor: -6 to -10, Lebert 2004 crossover RCT)
  donepezil dNPI = +3.2  (anchor: behaviour WORSE, Mendez 2007)

  The AChEI null result is STRUCTURAL, not imposed. ACH_INTEGRITY is
  0.95 because the cholinergic system is largely PRESERVED in FTD, so
  donepezil does raise cholinergic tone (0.95 -> 1.66) —
  and the cognitive endpoint does not depend on it, so dCDR is
  EXACTLY zero while the agitation term makes behaviour worse. That
  is the mechanistic contrast with Alzheimer's disease, where the
  same drug acts on a genuinely depleted system.

  Note also that no symptomatic drug moves CDR at all: in this
  structure they act on neurotransmitter tone, which feeds the
  behavioural axis only. Benefit and harm both emerge from the
  wiring rather than from an imposed effect size.

==============================================================================
I. ASOs — TARGET ENGAGEMENT WITHOUT CLINICAL BENEFIT
==============================================================================
C9orf72 repeat-targeting ASO, intrathecal q12w x8:
  repeat RNA          -49.8%
  CSF poly-GP         -50.1%   (FOCUS-C9 reported up to ~50%)
  poly-GR (toxic)     -50.3%
  dCDR at 96 wk       -0.0351   (trial: no clinical benefit; discontinued)
  dplasma NfL         -3.17 pg/mL

  Why so little from ~50% target knockdown? In this structure the
  repeat products are one of several parallel inputs. The same
  genotype also carries a lysosomal penalty from C9orf72 protein
  HAPLOINSUFFICIENCY, which an RNA-targeting ASO cannot touch, and
  the TDP-43 loop is self-templating once started. Lowering poly-GP
  after that loop has closed removes an initiator, not the engine.

MAPT-lowering ASO, intrathecal q12w x8:
  MAPT mRNA           -45.6%
  soluble tau         -45.7%   (BIIB080 class: ~30-50%)
  AGGREGATED tau      -17.4%
  dCDR at 96 wk       -0.1387

  Aggregated tau falls roughly a third as much as soluble tau:
  blocking synthesis STARVES a self-templating deposit, it does not
  CLEAR one. The model's implication is that MAPT-ASO trials should
  be powered on presymptomatic or very early cohorts, while the
  deposit is still small — and that its effect, though the largest
  of any agent modelled here, is still ~1% of 96-week progression.

==============================================================================
J. WHERE THIS MODEL IS WEAK OR PROBABLY WRONG
==============================================================================
Stated rather than tuned away.

1. P_LYS_SORT (p) has no human measurement, and the SIGN of the
   anti-sortilin prediction depends entirely on it. The default 0.50
   sits below f_CSF=0.78 and so gives the drug the benefit of the
   doubt; a hostile choice would make it frankly harmful. What the
   model constrains is the RELATIONSHIP p vs f, not the value of p.

2. THE MODEL HAS NO COMPENSATED LATENT PHASE, and this single defect
   causes two separate disagreements: GRN onset lands ~5 y early
   (section A) and the presymptomatic NfL lead time is ~15 y instead
   of ~1-2 y (section B). Both follow from damage accumulating
   monotonically from t=0. The fix is not a parameter but a different
   structure: a compensated regime that holds biomarkers near normal
   until a threshold is crossed, then collapses. That is the single
   most valuable change anyone could make to this model.

3. KSYN_NMD=0.70 exists only to reconcile plasma PGRN at ~1/3 of
   control with an allele dose of 1/2. It is a fitted stand-in for
   nonsense-mediated decay plus lost autocrine recapture. Its
   consequence is real though: plasma falls to ~33% while CSF falls
   to ~50%, and no single gene-dose scalar fits both, which is itself
   evidence the two compartments have different clearance structures.

4. CNS antibody exposure is one well-stirred CSF compartment. Real
   parenchymal distribution is regionally heterogeneous, and the
   salience network is exactly where delivery matters. If cortical
   exposure is below the CSF surrogate, theta_CNS is overestimated
   and every anti-sortilin number here is optimistic.

5. Poly-GP is treated as a marker in equilibrium with repeat RNA and
   poly-GR as the toxic species. If the causal DPR is poly-GA instead
   (proteasome impairment), the ASO prediction changes and the CSF
   marker trials actually use would be tracking the wrong arm.

6. Two lumped regions cannot express what actually determines FTD
   phenotype, which is WHICH network fails first — a connectome
   problem. The svPPA scenario is a weighting shift, not a mechanism.

7. Sporadic FTLD gets the same TDP-43 machinery as GRN disease with
   no genetic driver, so its trajectory rests on the age gate alone.
   Real sporadic FTLD spans TDP-43 types A-C, FET and tau pathology;
   one parameterisation cannot represent that heterogeneity.

8. The age gate (age/62)^5 is phenomenological. It does real work —
   it turns onset age into a prediction — but its exponent is fitted
   to onset data, so the good agreement in section A is partly
   calibration rather than pure prediction. The genotype ORDERING
   and the TMEM106B direction are the parts that are not.
