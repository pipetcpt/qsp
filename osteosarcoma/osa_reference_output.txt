#  osa_reference_model.py    (numerical mirror of osa_mrgsolve_model.R)

#  Cure is a Poisson bet on lesions nobody can see, paid for out of an
#  exposure budget with three hard organ ceilings:
#
#      P(cure) = exp( -lambda0 * [1 - exp(-n0 * e^-K)] )
#      K       = kill integral, bounded by kidney / heart / cochlea

#  and the methotrexate ceiling is not a constant, because the drug is
#  cleared through the organ it destroys.

 SECTION 2 — THE METHOTREXATE RENAL FEEDBACK LOOP
====================================================================================================

 Methotrexate is cleared through the organ it destroys.  86% of the
 dose leaves by the kidney; the tubular concentration is

     C_tub = CL_ren(KID) * C_plasma / UrineFlow(KID)

 and everything above the pH-dependent solubility crystallises,
 obstructs, and lowers both CL_ren and UrineFlow — which RAISES
 C_tub.  Positive feedback with a loop gain, hence a bifurcation.

 2a. Solubility of methotrexate in tubular fluid
     S(pH) = 0.86 * 10^(0.682*(pH-5)) mM   — log-linear fit to the
     published table (0.39 / 1.55 / 9.04 mg/mL at pH 5 / 6 / 7)
      pH    S (mM)   S (mg/mL)
     5.0      0.86        0.39
     5.5      1.89        0.86
     6.0      4.14        1.88
     6.5      9.07        4.12
     7.0     19.88        9.04
     7.5     43.60       19.81
     8.0     95.61       43.45

 2b. A single 12 g/m2 course as a function of urine pH

     C24/C48/C72 are the monitoring concentrations in uM.  A course
     is 'delayed' if C48 > 1 uM or C72 > 0.1 uM (the thresholds that
     trigger rescue escalation in every modern protocol).

 2c. THE BIFURCATION
     urine pH at which tubular fluid first supersaturates   7.30
     urine pH below which elimination becomes delayed       7.15
     urine pH below which the course causes AKI (>25% GFR)  6.95

     The protocol instruction 'alkalinise the urine to pH >= 7.0 and
     hydrate at 3 L/m2/day' is not a convention.  It is where this
     loop changes sign.  Note that pH 7.0 sits +0.30 from the
     supersaturation boundary, i.e. AT the edge and not comfortably
     inside it — which is why 1-2% of courses still show delayed
     elimination under full protocol compliance, and why the
     escalation instruction on a rising creatinine is MORE
     bicarbonate, not less methotrexate.

 2d. EXCHANGE RATES.  C_tub scales as dose / urine-flow, while
     solubility scales as 10^(0.682*pH).  So any factor f applied to
     C_tub is worth exactly log10(f)/0.682 pH units:
       double the hydration           = 0.44 pH units
       quadruple the hydration        = 0.88 pH units
       halve the methotrexate dose    = 0.44 pH units
       quarter the methotrexate dose  = 0.88 pH units
     Read the other way: 1.00 pH unit = 4.81-fold change
     in C_tub.  ONE UNIT OF URINE pH IS WORTH 4.8x THE FLUID, OR A
     4.8-FOLD DOSE REDUCTION.  Alkalinisation is not adjunctive
     supportive care; arithmetically it is the largest single lever
     on high-dose methotrexate safety, larger than the dose itself.

 2e. The same statement, simulated (pH 6.6 held fixed):
  hydration (L/m2/d)   C48_uM   eGFR%  delayed
                 1.5   14.667    55.7     True
                 3.0    9.158    62.9     True
                 6.0    1.857    81.2     True
                12.0    0.556    99.8    False
     vs. pH 7.3 at the ORIGINAL 3 L/m2/day: C48 0.563 uM, eGFR 99.5%
     — 0.7 of a pH unit does what quadrupling the fluid does.

 2f. LOOP GAIN.  Identical course at pH 6.6, but starting from a
     kidney that a previous cisplatin cycle already shaved:
 start eGFR%    C/S   C48_uM  AUC mM*h  end eGFR%  GFR lost  delayed
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
         100   3.45    9.158      8.58       62.9      37.1     True
          90   3.55   11.884      9.30       60.9      29.1     True
          80   3.66   15.485     10.14       59.0      21.0     True
          70   3.77   20.244     11.12       57.2      12.8     True
          60   3.90   26.547     12.28       55.4       4.6     True
          50   4.03   34.919     13.66       53.7      -3.7     True

     Halving the starting GFR multiplies the methotrexate AUC of the
     SAME dose by 1.59x and C48 by 3.8x, and leaves the kidney at 53.7%
     instead of 62.9%.  (The absolute GFR LOST is smaller simply because
     there was less left to lose — the injury runs down toward a
     common floor, which is the point: the floor is where twelve
     courses take you.)  The loop gain rises as the kidney falls, so
     course n changes the
     pharmacokinetics of courses n+1 .. 12.  In a regimen that gives
     twelve courses of methotrexate AND four of cisplatin, the two
     nephrotoxins are not additive — they are multiplicative through
     a shared state variable.

 2g. THE RESCUE IS DIAGNOSTICALLY GATED TO ARRIVE AFTER THE
     EXPOSURE IT WOULD PREVENT.

     Glucarpidase is triggered by a measured concentration, and the
     concentration that triggers it is measured at 24, 48 and 72 h.
     But the exposure integral is front-loaded: by the time the
     trigger fires, most of the AUC has already been delivered.

rescue timing              C48_uM    C72_uM  AUC mM*h  AUC removed  end eGFR%
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
none                       12.073    1.8985      8.92            -       59.1
glucarpidase at 12 h        1.506    0.2513      6.34          29%       63.2
glucarpidase at 24 h        1.017    0.3672      7.99          10%       60.5
glucarpidase at 36 h        0.204    0.3428      8.55           4%       59.6
glucarpidase at 48 h       12.073    0.1771      8.78           2%       59.3
glucarpidase at 72 h       12.073    1.8985      8.90           0%       59.2

     Rescue at 24 h removes 10% of the exposure; at 48 h, 2%; at 72 h,
     0%.  The standard trigger is a 48-h or 72-h
     concentration, i.e. the drug is given at the point where it can
     still remove 2% of what is left and 98% has already landed.

     End-of-course GFR is 59.1% with no rescue and 59.3% with rescue
     at 48 h.  The tubular injury integral was paid in the first day,
     before any monitoring threshold had been crossed.  Nothing
     rescues the tubule retrospectively — which is why the only
     intervention with real leverage is the one applied BEFORE the
     infusion starts (2d), and why urine pH, not the rescue protocol,
     is where the safety of this regimen actually lives.

     Every kill term in the model carries the factor (1 - RES).  A
     'poor responder' is by definition a patient in whom that factor
     is small, so the escalation was tested in exactly the subgroup
     where any added cytotoxic is worth least — and it had to buy the
     ifosfamide/etoposide cycles by giving up one doxorubicin and one
     cisplatin cycle out of a budget that was already at its walls.
     The null result is the arithmetic of the design, not a surprise
     about the drugs.

====================================================================================================

     Reference MAP: mean log-kill 15.23 nats (6.61 log10)
     Between-patient CV of achieved log-kill: 25%  (SD 3.81 nats)
     Sources of that spread: SLC19A1 / ABCC2 transporter genotype,
     methotrexate clearance (CV ~30%), MTHFR, delivered dose
     intensity after the go/no-go gates, and intrinsic
     chemosensitivity.  None of them is randomised in any trial.

     Half a log of extra kill is worth +20 points of cure in a
     population with CV 2%, and only +4 points in one with CV 65% — a
     5.5-fold difference produced entirely by the spread and
     not at all by the drug.

     Note carefully what this does NOT say.  Narrowing the spread at
     constant mean barely moves the cure fraction itself (0.592 at
     CV 45% vs 0.659 at CV 5%): the patients who gain and the
     patients who lose very nearly cancel.  What the spread destroys
     is DETECTABILITY.  Any realistic intensification moves the mean
     by 0.1-0.3 log, and at CV 25% that is worth:

  delta log10   abs gain in cure     HR   n per arm for 80% power
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
         0.10             +0.019   0.94                     10827
         0.20             +0.037   0.88                      2764
         0.30             +0.054   0.83                      1256
         0.50             +0.087   0.73                       475

     EURAMOS-1 randomised 618 poor responders to MAPIE.  On this
     arithmetic that trial could only ever have detected a shift of
     roughly 0.50 log or larger, and the escalation delivers about
     0.2.  The trial was not underpowered by accident; it was
     underpowered by the shape of the dose-response surface it was
     built on top of.

 4b-ii. NOT THE MEAN, AND NOT THE TAIL EITHER — THE SHOULDER.

     Because P(cure) saturates at both ends, the marginal value of an
     extra log of kill is zero in patients who were already going to
     be cured AND zero in patients no achievable dose can rescue.
     Spend the same total extra log-kill on different parts of the
     distribution and the answers are not close:

     Achieved log-kill quartile bounds (log10): 0.74 / 5.49 / 6.60 / 7.72 / 12.52

who gets the extra kill                   each gets    cure    gain     HR
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
everyone (uniform intensification)            0.25L   0.649  +0.048   0.85
bottom quartile (worst responders)            1.00L   0.632  +0.030   0.90
SECOND quartile (the near-misses)             1.00L   0.717  +0.116   0.65
third quartile                                1.00L   0.632  +0.031   0.90
top quartile (already cured)                  1.00L   0.604  +0.002   0.99
bottom half                                   0.50L   0.672  +0.070   0.78
middle half (25-75th percentile)              0.50L   0.688  +0.087   0.74

     The same total drug is worth 2.4x more given to the
     SECOND quartile (the near-misses) than spread over everyone, and giving it
     to the WORST quartile is worth LESS than uniform (+0.030 vs
     +0.048) — a full extra log does not rescue a patient who is
     four logs short.

     The peak sits in decile 5 (K 6.18-6.60 log10) and the

     Now put that next to Section 5.  Necrosis correlates with
     achieved log-kill at r = 0.97, so the marker CAN identify this
     band — the near-misses at roughly 85-89% necrosis, not the 40%
     necrosis patients.  EURAMOS-1 used the marker correctly as a
     prognostic tool and then spent its escalation budget on the
     group the marker had identified as LEAST salvageable.  The band
     the arithmetic points at has never been randomised.

 4c. THE POISSON STRUCTURE.  Cure requires EVERY occult lesion to be
     sterilised, so the exposure enters as a double exponential:

       P(cure) = exp( -lambda0 * [1 - exp(-n0 * e^-K)] )

   lambda0   P(no lesion at all)   cure with MAP  cure, MAP +1 log
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
       0.5                 0.607           0.831             0.900
       1.0                 0.368           0.716             0.829
       1.8                 0.165           0.602             0.760
       3.0                 0.050           0.509             0.695
       6.0                 0.002           0.418             0.625
      15.0                 0.000           0.325             0.540

     lambda0 = 1.80 was calibrated on one number: cure after
     amputation ALONE is exp(-lambda0) = 0.165, and the historical
     figure is 0.15-0.20.  Everything downstream — including the
     6.5-log requirement on MAP — follows from that single anchor.

     Note the last column: in overt metastatic disease (lambda0 = 15)
     a full extra log of kill barely moves the cure fraction, because
     cure needs all fifteen lesions and the product of fifteen
     near-misses is still a miss.  Same drug, same log-kill,
     different arithmetic.

 SECTION 5 — HUVOS NECROSIS IS A READ-OUT, NOT A LEVER
====================================================================================================

     Necrosis at week 11 and micrometastatic log-kill are two
     integrals of the SAME sensitivity parameter over the SAME ten
     weeks.  That is why necrosis is the strongest prognostic factor
     in the disease — and also why changing therapy AFTER measuring
     it cannot recover what the parameter already decided.

 5b. THE SAME ESCALATION, MOVED EARLIER.  Poor-responder tumour
     (RES0 = 0.20); the ONLY difference is when the ifosfamide/
     etoposide cycles are given:
arm                                            Huvos%   log10    cure     HR
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MAP                                              77.1    4.09   0.185   1.00
MAPIE — escalation AFTER surgery (as randomised)     77.1    4.30   0.197   0.96
MAP + the same IE cycles given PRE-operatively     80.6    4.27   0.194   0.97

     Moving the identical drug from after surgery to before it changes
     the NECROSIS read-out by +3.5 points and the micrometastatic
     log-kill by -0.03 log10.

     That dissociation is the point, and it is worse for the surrogate
     than a weak correlation would be.  Across the resistance sweep
     above, necrosis tracks micrometastatic kill almost perfectly (r = 0.97),
     so it is an excellent PROGNOSTIC marker.  But necrosis is an
     integral over the primary, where drug penetration and matrix
     TGF-beta apply, and cure is an integral over the lung, where they
     do not.  Re-timing therapy moves the first without moving the
     second, i.e. the marker can be improved without improving the
     patient.  A marker that is prognostic but manipulable is exactly
     the wrong thing to build a treatment decision on — and
     response-adapted escalation is a design that reads the marker at
     week 11 and then spends its budget in the window where the
     integral has the least left to give.

 SECTION 6 — THE VICIOUS CYCLE IS A GROWTH TERM, NOT A SURVIVAL TERM
====================================================================================================

arm                                         OCLmax    ALP  Huvos%  log10   cure    HR

MAP — EURAMOS-1 arm A (reference standard     1.34  15.15    90.3   6.61  0.602  1.00

     Zoledronate cuts peak osteoclast drive by 25% and denosumab by 25%,
     while micrometastatic log-kill moves by -0.00 and -0.00 log10 respectively.

     The reason is structural rather than empirical.  In this model
     the RANKL -> osteoclast -> matrix-TGF-beta loop multiplies the
     tumour GROWTH rate (KG), whereas cure is decided by the KILL
     integral, and during MAP the kill term is one to two orders of
     magnitude larger than the growth term it would be modifying.
     Suppressing the loop therefore does exactly what the trials
     found it does: it changes osteolysis, ALP and radiographic
     appearance, and it does not change survival.  OS2006 randomised
     318 patients to zoledronate on top of chemotherapy and found no
     EFS benefit with worse local control.  Two readouts, two
     integrals; the marker moved and the disease did not.
