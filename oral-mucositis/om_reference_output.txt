2.  THE TWO CLOCKS -- the central claim, tested as a 2x2
==============================================================================

  CLAIM: onset belongs to the INSULT term, duration to the
  REGENERATION term.  If true, perturbing one term must move its own
  endpoint and leave the other's largely alone.

  The insult term is perturbed by scaling `sens` (which multiplies
  every kill term and nothing else).  The regeneration term is
  perturbed by scaling `lamS` (which enters only dS/dt renewal).

  HDM 200 mg/m2
    perturbation                  onset d     durn d
    baseline                         7.50       6.00
    insult  x0.70                     nan       0.00
    insult  x1.40                    6.70      11.20
    regen   x0.70                    7.20      11.40
    regen   x1.40                     nan       0.00
    relative swing over a 2-fold change in the term:
      insult      -> onset   nan%   duration 186.7%
      regeneration-> onset   nan%   duration 190.0%
      SEPARATION RATIO (onset: insult/regen) = nan
      SEPARATION RATIO (durn : regen/insult) = 1.02

  chemoRT 70 Gy + cis
    perturbation                  onset d     durn d
    baseline                        17.50      44.00
    insult  x0.70                   44.20       7.00
    insult  x1.40                   10.30      68.50
    regen   x0.70                   13.00      73.50
    regen   x1.40                   26.10       8.30
    relative swing over a 2-fold change in the term:
      insult      -> onset 193.7%   duration 139.8%
      regeneration-> onset  74.9%   duration 148.2%
      SEPARATION RATIO (onset: insult/regen) = 2.59
      SEPARATION RATIO (durn : regen/insult) = 1.06

  VERDICT -- AND THE CLAIM AS ORIGINALLY WRITTEN DOES NOT SURVIVE
  INTACT.  The ONSET half holds: in chemoRT a two-fold change in the
  insult term moves onset ~2.6x more than the same change in the
  regeneration term.  The DURATION half does NOT: the two terms move
  duration almost equally (separation ratio ~1.0-1.1).  That is not
  a numerical accident, it is obvious in hindsight -- duration is the
  time taken to climb back from a DEPTH, and the insult sets the
  depth.  The defensible statement is therefore the weaker one:

      ONSET is owned by the insult term.
      DURATION is shared, and only the regeneration term can
      shorten it WITHOUT also reducing the insult.

  Section 8 is where that weaker statement earns its keep: it is
  what separates an agent that can be given to a patient whose
  cytotoxic dose is fixed from one that cannot.

3.  THE LATENT PERIOD IS 1/k_shed, NOT THE TRANSIT TIME
==============================================================================

  For an IMPULSE insult (HDM) the barrier is left with no incoming
  flux, so it decays at k_shed and ulcerates when it crosses D_crit.
  The prediction is therefore  t_onset ~ ln(1/D_crit)/k_shed,
  and it should NOT scale with the transit parameter k_p.

  k_shed        onset d     pred d      ratio
  0.20             8.70       5.39       1.61
  0.25             8.00       4.32       1.85
  0.30             7.50       3.60       2.09
  0.40             7.00       2.70       2.60
  0.50             6.80       2.16       3.15

  k_p           onset d   (transit parameter -- should move onset much less)
  0.45             7.30
  0.62             7.50
  0.85             7.40
  1.10             7.20

  THE ANALYTIC PREDICTION IS WRONG, AND THE TABLE SHOWS BY HOW
  MUCH.  ln(1/D_crit)/k_shed under-predicts the onset by 1.6x at
  k_shed 0.20 and by 3.2x at 0.50, and the model's onset moves only
  ~22% while the prediction moves 2.5-fold.  The reason is that the
  closed-form ignores the transit-amplifying pool, which keeps
  feeding the barrier for several days after the insult and is NOT
  emptied instantaneously.  What survives is the QUALITATIVE claim,
  and it survives cleanly: onset tracks the BARRIER LIFETIME
  parameter and is nearly indifferent to the TRANSIT parameter
  (k_p 0.45 -> 1.10, a 2.4-fold change, moves onset by 0.3 d).

  For chemoRT the same two sweeps should behave DIFFERENTLY, because
  there the onset is set by how long the ramp takes to exhaust the
  clonogen pool, not by barrier lifetime:
  k_shed        onset d
  0.20            17.60
  0.30            17.50
  0.50            17.40

4.  THE CRYOTHERAPY CRITERION -- derived, not looked up
==============================================================================

  Ice acts only while it is in the mouth.  So the benefit must be

      benefit  =  (1 - f_cryo) x (fraction of the insult AUC that
                                  falls inside the ice window)

  and for a drug eliminated with half-life t_half, iced for T from
  the moment of dosing, that fraction is exactly  1 - 2^(-T/t_half).
  Everything below is the model computing that, with NO parameter
  fitted to any cryotherapy trial.

  (a) melphalan 200 mg/m2, t_half 1.2 h -- mucosal AUC vs ice window
      T_ice (h)       AUC ratio  1-2^(-T/th)   sev durn d
      none               1.0000            -         6.00
      0.25               0.9832       0.1345         5.60
      0.50               0.9560       0.2508         4.40
      1.00               0.9526       0.4388         0.00
      2.00               0.9757       0.6850         0.00
      4.00               0.9923       0.9008         0.00
      6.00               0.9964       0.9688         0.00

  (b) DECOMPOSITION: is cryotherapy a FLOW effect or a TEMPERATURE
      effect?  Run 6 h of ice with each arm disabled in turn.
                                    mucosal AUC   sev durn d
      no ice                             0.5192         6.00
      both arms                          0.5173         0.00
      flow arm only  (Q10 = 1)           0.5173         5.90
      temp arm only  (fQ  = 1)           0.5192         0.00

      A tissue that re-equilibrates with plasma in ~2 minutes cannot
      be protected by cutting its blood flow: the flow arm moves the
      RATE of equilibration, not the exposure.  Whatever cryotherapy
      does, the delivery story alone does not carry it.

==============================================================================

  block as a melphalan equivalent.  The palifermin arm below is a
  PREDICTION.

  peak WHO grade                               4.00       3.00   4 -> 3/4
  peak ulcer area                              0.84       0.67          -

  NOTE the SHAPE of the prediction, which is the two-clock claim
  again: palifermin is a REGENERATION-arm drug, so it should move
  DURATION much more than ONSET.  Check the two rows above.

  (b) THE SCHEDULING PARADOX -- WHERE IT IS NOT.
      Sweep the gap between the last pre-conditioning palifermin
      dose and the alkylator.  Negative gap = palifermin still on
      board when the cytotoxic lands.
      gap (d)            sev durn d    peak area   vs placebo
      no palifermin            9.00        0.836            -
      -1.0                     3.60        0.466       -60.0%
      -0.5                     3.70        0.471       -58.9%
      0.0                      3.80        0.482       -57.8%
      0.5                      4.10        0.500       -54.4%
      1.0                      4.50        0.521       -50.0%
      2.0                      5.10        0.568       -43.3%
      3.0                      5.70        0.610       -36.7%

      A PRIOR EXPECTATION OF THIS MODEL'S AUTHOR IS REFUTED HERE,
      AND THE SIGN IS THE OPPOSITE OF THE ONE EXPECTED.  The map and
      the model header both claimed the label's 24-hour separation
      is explained by KGF enlarging the cycling pool.  In THIS
      regimen it cannot be: neither an alkylator nor a photon
      carries a cycle-specific term (cycspec_mel = cycspec_cis = 0,
      and the LQ kill is first-order in S), so a larger cycling pool
      is a proportionally larger kill on a proportionally larger
      pool and the FRACTION killed does not change.  With the
      penalty structurally absent, all that is left is the KGF
      effect pool's 2-day half-life -- so giving palifermin CLOSER
      to the insult is strictly BETTER, monotonically, across the
      whole sweep.  The model therefore does not reproduce the
      label's separation requirement from mucosal kinetics, and it
      should not be read as supporting it: whatever justifies that
      requirement lies outside this model (formulation, direct
      interaction, or tumour safety), not in the epithelium.

  (c) THE SCHEDULING PARADOX -- WHERE IT IS.
      Repeat with a CYCLE-ACTIVE insult: bolus 5-FU 425 mg/m2 d1-5,
      for which cycspec = 1 and the kill scales with the cycling
      fraction.  Endpoint is ULCERATIVE (WHO>=2) stomatitis, the
      relevant grade for this regimen.
      gap (d)             durn>=2 d    peak area   fcyc_KGF = 0
      no palifermin            5.20        0.164              -
      -1.0                     4.80        0.177           0.00
      -0.5                     5.10        0.190           0.00
      0.0                      4.70        0.171           0.00
      1.0                      4.70        0.171           0.00
      2.0                      4.70        0.171           0.00
      3.0                      4.70        0.171           0.00

      THE LAST COLUMN IS THE RESULT.  Turning off the ONE parameter
      that couples KGF-driven proliferation to the cytotoxic target
      turns palifermin from a marginal drug into a curative one in
      this regimen.  The coupling eats almost the whole benefit.

      And the sweep is flat again -- but for the opposite reason.
      A 5-day bolus course cannot be separated from a growth factor
      whose effect pool has a 2-day half-life: there is no gap that
      empties the pool before the next dose lands.  That, not a
      24-hour rule, is the model's account of why palifermin is used
      in transplant conditioning and not with conventional
      chemotherapy: conditioning is an IMPULSE that a growth factor
      can be scheduled around, and a multi-day cytotoxic course is
      not.

7.  WHY WHO-GRADED TRIALS LOSE POWER WHERE THE DISEASE IS WORST
==============================================================================

  The WHO scale is ORDINAL and grade 4 is absorbing.  The same
  intervention is measured here on WHO grade and on ulcer area, at
  three baseline severities produced by scaling `sens` alone.

  intervention: 6 h oral cryotherapy with HDM
  sens             areaAUC0   areaAUC1   d(area)%   WHOdays0   d(WHOd)%
  0.70                 0.94       0.02     -97.8%       0.00       0.0%
  1.00                 4.42       0.13     -97.0%       6.00    -100.0%
  1.30                 8.56       0.52     -93.9%      10.10    -100.0%
  1.70                13.00       1.96     -84.9%      14.20     -97.9%

  THE ARTEFACT IS REAL BUT IT IS A FLOOR, NOT THE CEILING THIS
  SECTION WAS WRITTEN TO LOOK FOR.  The WHO-derived effect reads
  -100% at every severity -- the ordinal endpoint cannot express
  anything between 'severe' and 'not severe', so an intervention
  that drops the patient just below threshold and one that abolishes
  the disease score identically.  The continuous endpoint separates
  them cleanly and shows the effect DECAYING with severity
  (-97.8% -> -84.9%), which is the clinically meaningful fact and
  the one the ordinal scale destroys.  Either way the conclusion
  for trial design is the same and it is not the one asserted
  earlier: WHO grade is not a low-powered version of ulcer area, it
  is a DIFFERENT measurement, and a trial powered on it is blind to
  the size of the effect it is measuring.

8.  AN AGENT CANNOT BUY THE ENDPOINT IT DOES NOT OWN
==============================================================================

  Every available intervention, HDM 200 mg/m2, same patient.
  intervention                      onset     durn    peakA  painAUC
  none                               7.50     6.00    0.677     50.9
  cryotherapy 6 h  [INSULT]           nan     0.00    0.020      4.4
  dose 140 mg/m2   [INSULT]           nan     0.00    0.163     24.5
  palifermin       [REGEN ]          8.90     1.80    0.385     36.3
  photobiomodulation [REGEN]          nan     0.00    0.090     10.8
  glutamine        [REGEN ]           nan     0.00    0.323     28.6
  benzydamine      [INFLAM]          7.80     5.00    0.599     45.2
  cryo + palifermin                   nan     0.00    0.017      2.1

  READ THE AREA COLUMN, NOT THE DURATION COLUMN.  Section 7's floor
  effect reappears here unbidden: the calibrated patient sits just
  above the WHO>=3 threshold (peak area 0.68 against a threshold of
  0.35), so every effective agent reads as 100% prevention on the
  ordinal endpoint and 'nan' onset.  That is an artefact of the
  instrument and of a single deterministic patient -- NOT a claim
  that six-hour ice abolishes mucositis, which no trial reports.
  On the continuous endpoint the agents separate and rank sensibly,
  and the INSULT-arm agents (ice, dose reduction) beat the
  REGENERATION-arm agents (palifermin, glutamine) on peak area,
  which is what a depth-vs-recovery-rate reading predicts.
  The virtual population in om_calibrate.py, not this table, is
  where incidence-type claims belong.

10.  STRUCTURAL CHECKS
==============================================================================

  (a) The TNF -> NF-kB positive feedback.  The loop gain at the
      operating point must be < 1 or the inflammation block
      diverges.  Gain = gTNF * d(hill)/d(TNF) * pTNF/kTNF.
      NFkB=0.20 -> TNF=0.200  loop gain 0.2917  OK
      NFkB=0.50 -> TNF=0.500  loop gain 0.1867  OK
      NFkB=1.00 -> TNF=1.000  loop gain 0.1050  OK
      NFkB=1.50 -> TNF=1.500  loop gain 0.0672  OK

  (b) Dose delivery is exact (the breakpoint integrator).
      70 Gy/35 fx mucosal BED (Gy10)            84.0000  expected  84.0000
      TBI 12 Gy/8 fx mucosal BED (Gy10)         13.8000  expected  13.8000

  (c) Integrator convergence: halve the tolerances and compare.
      max |A(t)| difference rtol 1e-7 vs 1e-9 : 4.002e-06
      relative BED difference                  : 1.987e-07

  (d) Drug-free steady state is a genuine attractor.
      D drift over 60 drug-free days: 1.300e-06
      max ulcer area with no insult : 6.144e-06
