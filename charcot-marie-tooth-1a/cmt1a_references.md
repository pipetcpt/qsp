# Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 1A (CMT1A) — annotated references

Every PMID in this file was resolved through the NCBI E-utilities API
(`esearch` → `esummary`) and the returned first author, year, journal and
title are reproduced below as they came back. Nothing here was written
from memory.

That mattered. Four citations in the first draft of this model were
recalled rather than looked up, and all four pointed at real papers that
say something else:

the "unsourced" section at the bottom rather than given a plausible-looking

## Claims in the model with NO verified source

These are honest gaps. Each is used in the model and each is flagged as an
estimate in `cmt1a_mrgsolve_model.R` rather than attributed to a paper:

- **"~80% of newly synthesised PMP22 is degraded before reaching myelin."**
  This is the quantitative basis of structural commitment S1 and it is
  widely repeated, but the pulse-chase paper that establishes the exact
  fraction could not be pinned to a verified record in this session.
  Refs 19-24 establish the *qualitative* claim (PMP22 is inefficiently
  folded, aggregates, and saturates the proteasome); the specific number
  0.80 is a model assumption. **If it is wrong, S1 is wrong**, so it is
  the single most important thing to check before using this model.
- **The childhood NCV plateau (velocity fixed by age ~2-5 y).** Krajewski
  2000 (ref 51) and Birouk 1997 (ref 3) support the decoupling of
  conduction velocity from disability and from age, but a longitudinal
  paediatric series demonstrating the plateau itself is not cited here
  because no specific record was verified.
- **Plasma NfL is roughly doubled in CMT1A and correlates with CMTNS.**
  Refs 81-82 cover NfL in peripheral neuropathy and in CMT2A; a CMT1A
  cohort value was not verified, so the model's NfL scaling (ANFL) is an
  estimate.
- **PLEO-CMT high-dose ONLS difference of -0.37 at 15 months.** The trial
  itself is verified (ref 101); the specific effect size quoted in the
  model's diagnostic D07 is taken from the published abstract as recalled
  and should be re-read against the paper before being relied on.
- **PREMIER (the second phase 3 of PXT3003).** Referred to on the
  mechanistic map. No PubMed record was found for it in this session, so
  it is drawn on the map without a citation.
- **Reported PMP22 copy-number-4 (homozygous duplication) phenotypes.**
  Refs 10-11 describe severe homozygous/biallelic PMP22 phenotypes but
  not the homozygous 1.4-Mb duplication specifically.
