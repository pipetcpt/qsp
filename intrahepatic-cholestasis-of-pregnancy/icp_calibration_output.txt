The knee is not fitted.  It is where the diffusive maternal->fetal flux
plus fetal synthesis consumes the carrier capacity VP; the position
follows from PS, VP and KMP alone.  What it explains is the LEVEL of
fetal exposure and the fact that the cord:maternal ratio stops falling
and starts rising as the carrier fills (column 2 divided by column 1:
it bottoms out around 0.30 in mild disease and climbs back above 0.40
in the >=100 band).  What it does NOT explain is the bend in the risk
curve -- section D measures that and finds it downstream, in the
myocardium.

==============================================================================
D. Where the steepness of the 100 umol/L threshold actually lives
==============================================================================
The same three outcome numbers are re-fitted three times, changing only
WHICH variable the hazard is written on.  The exponent each version
needs measures how much of the nonlinearity that variable already
carries: a big exponent means the variable is too shallow and the
dose-response is being asked to supply the steepness.

hazard written on                           band1    band2    band3   b3/b1  needs n   resid
maternal total bile acid (the assay)       15.808   58.222  137.702     8.7     2.95   1.05%
fetal cytotoxic load FCL                    1.939    6.310   17.202     8.9     2.55   0.00%
myocardial arrhythmia index ARRI            0.011    0.082    0.414    36.8     1.55   0.45%

Reading the table:
  * maternal TBA spans 8.7x across the three bands and
    needs an exponent of 2.95.
  * the fetal cytotoxic load spans 8.9x -- barely more
    than the maternal assay -- and so needs an exponent of
    2.55, essentially unchanged.  Transport alone
    does NOT explain the threshold.  (What transport does explain is
    the LEVEL and the rising cord:maternal ratio; see section B.)
  * the arrhythmia index spans 36.8x on the same three
    pregnancies and needs only 1.55.

So the amplification is in the MYOCARDIAL RESPONSE, not in the
placenta and not in the dose-response.  Connexin-43 uncoupling is a
threshold process (Hill 1.6 around IC50 ~30 in weighted units) and
calcium overload multiplies it, so a 9-fold change in fetal exposure
becomes a 37-fold change in electrical instability.
That matters clinically in a specific way: it predicts the risk is
carried by a step change in myocyte coupling rather than by a graded
increase in 'fetal distress', which is why the events are abrupt and
why cardiotocography does not see them coming.

This is also the model correcting its own author.  The map was drawn
expecting placental carrier saturation to be the source of the
threshold; the fit says it is not, and the ablations in section I
agree.  The saturation is real and it does set how much bile acid
reaches the fetus -- it just is not what makes the risk curve bend.

E. PITCHES (Chappell 2019): decomposing the composite primary endpoint
==============================================================================
endpoint                              placebo       UDCA      rel.            trial
total bile acid, umol/L                 35.61      23.31    -34.5%            falls
fetal cytotoxic load (model)             3.87       2.40    -38.0%   not measurable
ALT, U/L                               124.82      75.96    -39.1%            falls
worst itch, 0-10 cm                      4.61       4.27     -7.5%          -0.7 cm
stillbirth, %                            0.20       0.20     -0.8%    1 vs 2 deaths
spontaneous PTB <37 wk, %                9.75       8.77    -10.1%                -
neonatal unit >=4 h, %                  11.63      10.45    -10.1%                -
COMPOSITE, %                            20.39      18.45     -9.5%     27.8 vs 24.8

Stillbirth is 0.97% of the composite in the placebo arm.
So even abolishing it entirely moves the composite by 0.20 percentage points.
The model's own stillbirth reduction is 1%, worth 0.002 points on a composite running at 20%.

That is the whole of PITCHES.  Two of the three components of the
primary endpoint -- preterm delivery and neonatal unit admission --
are set by gestational age at delivery and by the decision to deliver,
neither of which UDCA touches; the model reproduces this because both
are functions of GA at delivery, not of bile acid.  The one component
UDCA can move contributes a fraction of a percentage point.  With 604
women the trial could not have detected its own mechanism, and the
result is therefore consistent with the Ovadia 2021 individual-
participant meta-analysis reporting a stillbirth signal: the two
studies are measuring different things, not disagreeing.

Detectability check -- women per arm needed for 80% power at alpha 0.05
on each component, assuming the model's effect sizes are correct:
   composite              n/arm = 6,562
   stillbirth alone       n/arm = > 1e7
   neonatal unit alone    n/arm = 11,075

Read the dFCL and dVAS columns together.  Every drug that lowers the
bile acid pool (UDCA, cholestyramine, an IBAT inhibitor) leaves itch
essentially where it was, and the one drug that abolishes itch
(rifampicin) is mid-table on bile acid.  The model does not contain a
rule that says so; it falls out of driving autotaxin from the sex-
steroid load with only a weak, saturating bile-acid term.  The
practical consequence is that a trial powered on itch cannot rank
bile-acid-lowering drugs, and a trial powered on bile acid cannot
rank antipruritics -- which is roughly the state of the ICP
literature.

Note the INR column: cholestyramine is the only agent that lowers
bile acid AND worsens vitamin K status, because it lowers the
intestinal bile acid concentration that fat-soluble vitamin
absorption depends on.  Rifampicin does the same for a different
reason (it shrinks the pool).  Both trade postpartum haemorrhage
risk for bile acid, and the model prices the trade.

G. Optimal gestational age at delivery, by bile acid stratum
==============================================================================
(neonatal morbidity weighted at 0.055 of a stillbirth)

stratum                         35      36      37      38      39      40   opt
TBA <40 (repr.)              42.94   26.31   15.62    9.79    7.09    6.08    40 wk
TBA ~26                      43.14   26.58   15.96   10.22    7.64    6.76    40 wk
TBA 40-99                    44.16   27.90   17.69   12.46   10.51   10.43    40 wk
TBA >=100                    59.24   47.01   41.58   41.87   46.13   52.94    37 wk
TBA >=100 + UDCA             51.97   36.79   27.85   24.11   23.86   25.71    39 wk

Units are milli-expected-losses; only the position of the minimum
matters.

The >=100 band comes out at 37 weeks, which is the direction the
guidelines point (SMFM suggests 36-37 wk at or above 100 umol/L), and
treating that pregnancy pushes the optimum back to 39 weeks -- i.e. in
this model the value of UDCA is not a biochemical endpoint, it is about
two weeks of gestation.  That is a testable and, as far as we can tell,
untested claim.

The 40-99 band does NOT come out where guidelines put it, and the model
should not be read as supporting them there.  It puts the optimum at
39-40 weeks, not 37-38.  The reason is arithmetic and worth stating
plainly: the stillbirth excess Ovadia reports for that band is 0.28%
against 0.13% -- fifteen hundredths of a percentage point -- and no
plausible weighting makes fifteen hundredths of a point outweigh the
neonatal cost of delivering two to three weeks early.  Below is the
neonatal-morbidity weight that WOULD be required:

   WMORB    <40  40-99  >=100
   0.005     40     38     35
   0.010     40     38     35
   0.020     40     39     36
   0.035     40     39     37
   0.055     40     40     37
   0.080     40     40     38
   0.120     40     40     38

Only at a weight below about 0.01 -- i.e. only if a neonatal unit
admission is worth less than a hundredth of a stillbirth -- does the
40-99 band move to 37 weeks, and at that weight the <40 band moves
with it, which no guideline recommends.  So either the model is missing
something real in that band, or the 37-38 week recommendation is not
carried by the stillbirth numbers it is usually justified by.  Two
candidates for what the model is missing, both plausible: within-woman
bile acid fluctuates and a woman measured at 70 umol/L may spend days
above 100 between visits (the model runs a smooth trajectory), and the
hazard here is an average over a band whose upper edge is much more
dangerous than its middle.  A version of this model driven by serial
measurements rather than a smooth curve would be the way to test that,
and it is the single most useful extension we can identify.

H. Onset timing, postpartum resolution, and twin pregnancy
==============================================================================
None of these three is fitted.  Onset and resolution are consequences
of the placental sex-steroid trajectory (E2 and P4-sulfate are ODEs
with placental production switched off at delivery); the twin effect is
a single covariate on that trajectory.

scenario                            onsetGA   TBApk   pkGA   <10 by  VASpk
normal                                never     5.3   39.0      1 d    1.3
ICP <40 stratum                        34.4    15.8   39.0      2 d    3.4
ICP 40-99 stratum                      22.1    58.2   39.0      4 d    5.6
ICP >=100 stratum                      20.0   137.7   39.0      7 d    7.0
twin, <40 genotype                     26.7    43.6   39.0      3 d    6.0
twin, 40-99 genotype                   20.0   121.8   39.0      5 d    7.6
ICP >=100 + UDCA 1000                  20.0    98.7   39.0      6 d    6.8

The same susceptibility vector that is silent at 20 weeks crosses the
10 umol/L diagnostic line in the third trimester and clears within
about two weeks of delivery, with no parameter changed at either
boundary -- the sex-steroid trajectory does both.  A twin pregnancy
shifts onset earlier and raises the peak on the same genotype, which
is the mechanistic reading of the ~5-fold higher ICP incidence in
twins.

I. In-silico ablations: which mechanism carries which result
==============================================================================
ablation                                      ARRI b1       b3   b3/b1  n(ARRI)  n(TBA)
(reference)                                    0.0112   0.4135    36.8     1.55    2.95
placental carrier not saturable (V_P x 20)     0.0001   0.0048    45.9     1.55    2.95
no maternal-side occupancy (TRANSIN = 0)       0.0093   0.2620    28.3     1.65    2.90
species-uniform diffusive PS (all = 0.77)      0.0119   0.4758    40.1     1.50    2.95
uniform cytotoxicity weights (all = 0.5)       0.0195   0.7657    39.2     1.50    2.90
no Cx43 uncoupling (IMAXGJ = 0.02)             0.0003   0.0097    36.8     1.55    2.95
Cx43 uncoupling made linear (HGJ = 1, IC50 x 6)   0.0098   0.1241    12.7     2.15    2.95
no Ca2+ overload term (ECA = 0)                0.0102   0.2451    24.0     1.80    2.95

Columns to read: b3/b1 is how much electrical instability separates the
>=100 band from the <40 band, and n(ARRI) is the exponent the hazard
then needs.  A mechanism matters here if removing it COLLAPSES b3/b1
and forces n(ARRI) up towards the n(TBA) column.

  * making the placental carrier non-saturable barely moves b3/b1.
    Carrier saturation sets how much bile acid reaches the fetus, not
    how nonlinear the response is.
  * flattening the species-specific diffusive permeabilities, or the
    cytotoxicity weights, likewise leaves the ratio in the same place.
  * what destroys it is LINEARISING connexin-43 uncoupling: b3/b1 falls
    from 37 to 13 and the exponent the hazard needs climbs from 1.55
    towards the bile-acid figure.  Removing the Ca2+ overload term
    does the same thing less strongly.
  * note that merely SCALING the uncoupling down (IMAXGJ = 0.02) leaves
    b3/b1 untouched, because a Hill function scaled by a constant has
    the same shape.  It is the threshold SHAPE that carries the
    result, not the amplitude -- which is a sharper claim, and a
    harder one to satisfy by accident.

The conclusion is specific and falsifiable: the 100 umol/L threshold is
a property of gap-junction uncoupling in fetal myocardium, and it
should therefore track the hydrophobic fraction of the fetal pool
rather than maternal total bile acid.  Cord-blood speciation at
delivery would test it directly; the maternal assay never can.

I2. UDCA: which of its mechanisms does what
==============================================================================
ablation                                            500 mg/d     1000 mg/d     2000 mg/d
  (cells are maternal TBA / fetal cytotoxic load)
reference                                          43.5/ 4.43     35.9/ 3.51     28.4/ 2.60
no BSEP trafficking effect (ETRAF=0)               48.0/ 4.92     40.9/ 4.01     32.8/ 2.98
no gut 7beta-dehydroxylation to LCA (KDH_UD=0)     42.3/ 4.37     33.7/ 3.42     24.5/ 2.47
no hepatic LCA sulfation (VLCAS=0)                 51.9/ 6.22     48.8/ 6.21     47.7/ 6.71
no placental carrier competition (KMP_UDCA huge)   43.5/ 4.42     35.9/ 3.50     28.4/ 2.59

The BSEP trafficking effect is the therapeutic mechanism: remove it and
most of the drug's effect goes with it.  The gut conversion of UDCA to
lithocholate is a real but modest liability at these doses (removing it
improves the drug by a few percentage points, and the gap widens with
dose because the LCA-generating route is first-order in dose while the
hepatic sulfation route that disposes of LCA saturates).  Removing
hepatic LCA sulfation entirely is what makes UDCA look bad -- which is
the model's reading of why UDCA is tolerated in ordinary ICP and is a
genuine concern in the severest cholestasis, where that same enzyme
system is already working against a much larger substrate load.
