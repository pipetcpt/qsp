   LAMP arm       dAL obs  dSER obs  AL-implied  implied lens
   placebo           0.41     -0.81       -1.15          0.34
   0.01%             0.36     -0.59       -1.01          0.42
   0.025%            0.29     -0.46       -0.82          0.36
   0.05%             0.20     -0.27       -0.56          0.29
   implied lens compensation across the four arms: +0.353 +/- 0.053 D/yr
   -> it IS treatment-independent (CV 15%), which is the internal
      consistency check LAMP's two endpoints have to pass, and they do.
   the model's own physiological lens term over the same year: -0.270 D/yr
   published childhood lens-power loss: -0.20 to -0.45 D/yr

   CROSS-TRIAL TEST -- ATOM1 placebo vs ATOM2 0.01%% (2-yr values):
     ATOM1 placebo    dAL 0.38 -> -1.06 D expected, -1.20 D reported, implied lens -0.14 D/2yr
     ATOM2 0.01%      dAL 0.41 -> -1.14 D expected, -0.49 D reported, implied lens +0.65 D/2yr
     The two cohorts have the SAME axial elongation (0.41 vs 0.38 mm) yet
     differ by 0.71 D in reported refraction.  Reconciling them requires
     the 0.01% arm's lens to have lost +0.40 D/yr MORE power than the
     placebo arm's.  Atropine 0.01%% costs only 0.8 D of accommodative
     amplitude, so a lens-power change of that size is not available.
     VERDICT: the ATOM1/ATOM2 refractive comparison is not internally
     consistent, and the axial endpoints -- which agree -- should be
     believed instead.
