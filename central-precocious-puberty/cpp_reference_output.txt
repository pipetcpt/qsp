3b. ACROSS SUBJECTS: the model REFUTES the stronger form of this claim
====================================================================================
Hypothesis under test: if growth velocity is a misleading monitor, then
across subjects a LOWER on-therapy velocity should go with a LARGER
adult-height gain.  The model says NO, and the reason is instructive:
between subjects, both quantities are driven by the SAME third variable
(remaining growth-plate reserve at the start of therapy), so they move
TOGETHER.  The monitoring trap is therefore purely WITHIN-subject -- the
step change at the moment therapy starts -- and not a between-subject
ranking.  This is reported rather than tuned away.
n = 185 virtual subjects with thelarche < 8.0 y
