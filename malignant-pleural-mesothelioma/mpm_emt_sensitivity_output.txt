MPM QSP model -- post-hoc sensitivity on the failed subgroup prediction

==============================================================================
 WHAT WOULD MAKE THE CHECKMATE 743 SUBGROUP SPLIT COME OUT?
==============================================================================

 Baseline EMT_CHEMO is 0.55 (chemotherapy retains 45% of its kill in
 fully sarcomatoid disease), taken from the EMT-chemoresistance
 literature rather than from any mesothelioma trial.

 EMT_CHEMO     epi ratio     non-epi   epi OS c/IO non-epi OS c/IO
  0.55              0.71        0.88    15.5 /  21.8   5.0 /   5.7
  0.70              0.70        0.84    15.2 /  21.8   4.8 /   5.7
  0.80              0.69        0.82    15.1 /  21.8   4.7 /   5.7
  0.88              0.69        0.81    14.9 /  21.8   4.6 /   5.7
  0.94              0.68        0.80    14.8 /  21.8   4.5 /   5.7
  0.97              0.68        0.79    14.8 /  21.8   4.5 /   5.7

 observed:      epithelioid 0.86      non-epithelioid 0.46

 No value of EMT_CHEMO in the scanned range reproduces the ORDER
 CheckMate 743 reported.  Chemoresistance alone cannot do it, and
 the missing mechanism is somewhere else.

==============================================================================
 THE OTHER LEVER: how much brake is there for blockade to release?
==============================================================================

 PD-L1 positivity is reported at roughly 20% in epithelioid and 60-70%
 in sarcomatoid mesothelioma, which is where EMT_PDL1 = 2.0 comes from.

 EMT_PDL1     epi ratio non-epi ratio
  2.00             0.71        0.88
  3.00             0.67        0.80
  3.50             0.65        0.77
  3.75             0.64        0.75

 PD-L1 alone is bounded: PD-L1 is a fraction, so EMT_PDL1 above 4.0
 puts the sarcomatoid tumour past 100% positivity.  Whatever produces
 the CheckMate 743 split, it cannot be PD-L1 expression by itself.

==============================================================================
 READING
==============================================================================
 Neither lever reproduces the trial.  Even at EMT_CHEMO = 0.97 --
 chemotherapy retaining 3% of its kill in fully sarcomatoid disease --
 the non-epithelioid ratio only reaches 0.79 against an epithelioid
 0.68, so the two subgroups stay in the WRONG ORDER.  Pushing PD-L1 to
 the edge of what a fraction allows does the same thing to both
 subgroups and never separates them either.

 That is a more useful failure than a near miss, because it says the
 discrepancy is not a mis-set slope.  Look at the survivals rather than
 the ratios: at x = 0.85 BOTH arms have collapsed to 4.5 and 5.7
 months, against best supportive care's 7.0 at the reference histology.
 In this model a sarcomatoid tumour is so aggressive that NO treatment
 has room to work, and a ratio between two small numbers cannot be far
 from one.  CheckMate 743 says something quite different: its
 non-epithelioid patients on nivolumab + ipilimumab lived 18.1 months,
 which is what its EPITHELIOID patients achieved (18.7).  Checkpoint
 blockade did not lose efficacy with sarcomatoid histology at all --
 only chemotherapy did, falling to 8.8 months.

 So the located error is structural, not numerical: this model applies
 the histology penalty to BOTH arms, through three channels that do not
 distinguish them --
   HZ_EMT      raises the baseline hazard for everybody;
   EMT_KCOL    raises collagen, which shortens lambda for the T cell
               exactly as it does for the platinum;
   EMT_KG      raises proliferation against both.
 To reproduce CheckMate 743 the model would need checkpoint blockade to
 be SPARED that penalty -- an immune effector whose reach does not
 degrade with desmoplasia, or a sarcomatoid-specific antigenicity gain
 large enough to cancel it.  Which of those is true is a question about
 mesothelioma, not about this code, and the model is left disagreeing
 with the trial rather than tuned into agreement with it.
