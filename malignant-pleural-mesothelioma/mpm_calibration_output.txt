CHECK 1  GEOMETRY: what a 30% response actually means
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  parietal area (anatomic x coverage) = 450 cm2
  dV/dh on the parietal leaf          = 45 mL per mm of rind
  dV/dh over all three leaves         = 96 mL per mm

  RIND   : V = A h            ->  30% thickness drop = 30.0% volume kill
  SPHERE : V = 4/3 pi r^3     ->  30% diameter drop = 65.7% volume kill
  ratio of implied cytoreduction (sphere / rind) = 2.19 x

  So 'partial response' in a mesothelioma trial and 'partial response'
  in the RECIST tumour it was designed for differ by a factor of 2.2 in
  the cell kill they imply.  Reading across the two is not valid.

CHECK 6  SURGERY: setting h without resetting phi or reaching the fissure
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  pemcis           median OS  12.1 mo   mRECIST d90  24.7 mm   FVC d180 2.08 L
  surgery_chemo    median OS  26.5 mo   mRECIST d90   4.9 mm   FVC d180 3.13 L
  epp_chemo        median OS  19.4 mo   mRECIST d90   4.9 mm   FVC d180 2.04 L
  surg_hithoc      median OS  26.8 mo   mRECIST d90   4.8 mm   FVC d180 3.13 L

  MARS2 (Lancet Respir Med 2024) randomised extended pleurectomy/
  decortication plus chemotherapy against chemotherapy alone and found
  an OS hazard ratio of 1.28 AGAINST surgery.

  THE MODEL DOES NOT REPRODUCE THAT DIRECTION.  It predicts a large
  benefit from cytoreduction, because in this model an operation is a
  clean reset of rind THICKNESS and nothing else.  What the model is
  missing is everything the trial says the harm comes from:
    - no peri-operative mortality (MARS2: 3.4% at 30 days);
    - no months-long performance-status and FVC debt from a thoracotomy;
    - residual microscopic disease is modelled as a THIN RIND over the
      SAME 45% surface coverage, when 'macroscopic complete resection'
      in fact leaves tumour cells over essentially the WHOLE pleural
      surface -- the coverage fraction should go to 1 as the thickness
      goes to 0.1 mm, and in this model it does not.

  This is reported rather than fixed: tuning a surgical penalty until
  the model agreed with MARS2 would make the agreement an input.
