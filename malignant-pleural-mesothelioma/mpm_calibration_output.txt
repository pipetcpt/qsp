A.  RK4 STEP-SIZE CONVERGENCE
==============================================================================
   The integrator uses a two-phase step: dt = 0.02 d while any drug
   depot is switching (the fastest rate in the system is cisplatin's
   k_el + k_bind = 33.3 /d) and dt = 0.06 d thereafter.
   dt =  0.040 / 0.120 d   V(400 d) =  618.8529 cm3   CumHaz =  0.617138
   dt =  0.020 / 0.060 d   V(400 d) =  618.8378 cm3   CumHaz =  0.617106
   dt =  0.010 / 0.030 d   V(400 d) =  618.8365 cm3   CumHaz =  0.617103
   dt =  0.005 / 0.015 d   V(400 d) =  618.8366 cm3   CumHaz =  0.617103
   -> the step actually used differs from dt = 0.005 d by 0.000 %.

H.  THE MATRIX THE DRUG MAKES IS THE BARRIER THE DRUG THEN MEETS
==============================================================================
    cycle   day  h_meas cm  matrix cm3   f_exp  viable change this cycle %
        1     0      0.800       144.0   0.095                       -16.9
        2    21      0.732       168.3   0.104                       -18.7
        3    42      0.664       184.7   0.116                       -20.3
        4    63      0.597       193.7   0.129                       -22.1
        5    84      0.532       196.5   0.146                       -24.2
        6   105      0.470       194.1   0.165                       -26.5
   Matrix clears with t1/2 = 87 d while cycles are 21 d apart, so the debris of cycle 1 is still in the diffusion
   path at cycle 4, and the matrix pool GROWS from 144 to ~196 cm3.

   THE AUTHOR EXPECTED THIS TO PROTECT THE LATER CYCLES.  It does not.
   The exposed fraction RISES over the six cycles (0.095 -> 0.165) and the per-cycle kill
   ACCELERATES, because the rind thins faster than the matrix
   accumulates.  Matrix only RETARDS the improvement.  Running the
   same regimen with PHIM = 0 (killed cells vanish instead of
   fibrosing) isolates how much:
      with matrix (PHIM = 0.70):  h at day 105 = 0.470 cm, f_exp = 0.165
      without matrix (PHIM = 0):  h at day 105 = 0.142 cm, f_exp = 0.470
   So the debris costs 65 % of the exposed fraction that would otherwise have been
   recovered by cycle 6 -- a real penalty, but not the reversal that
   was expected.  Where the matrix DOES dominate is the MEASUREMENT:
   at day 105 it is 62 % of what the scanner is measuring.

==============================================================================
   regimen                         viable nadir %  peak symphysis  median OS mo
   systemic chemo only                      -76.8            0.66          12.0
   intrapleural only                        -82.1            0.66          11.7
   systemic + intrapleural                  -99.5            0.66          14.0
   talc first, then chemo                   -76.9            0.82          12.3
   talc first, then both                    -98.4            0.82          13.9
   The two routes attack opposite faces, so their coverage adds rather
   than overlapping -- and talc closes the very route it was given to
   make usable, which is why 'talc first, then both' is worse than
   'systemic + intrapleural' even though the effusion is better
   controlled.

   CAVEAT ON THE INTRAPLEURAL ARMS.  The model likes them more than
   the literature does, and the reason is identifiable: an instillate
   reaches the ADVANCING MARGIN, which is only 0.6 mm deep, from the
   free face, so f = 0.24 there against 0.021 in the bulk.
   That rests on assuming a contact fraction of 0.55 -- i.e. that an
   instillate bathes just over half the involved surface.  No trial has
   compared intrapleural chemotherapy against systemic chemotherapy in
   MPM, so this is a PREDICTION and a fragile one: it is roughly linear
   in a contact fraction nobody has measured.

L.  HISTOLOGY: WHY CHEMOTHERAPY AND IMMUNOTHERAPY SWAP PLACES
==============================================================================
   histology       chemo mo  nivo+ipi mo  HR 12 mo  HR 24 mo  CM743 HR
   epithelioid         13.7          9.8      1.98      0.78      0.86
   biphasic             8.8          8.1      0.82      0.43         -
   sarcomatoid          5.6          6.1      0.55      0.43      0.46
   CHEMOS and IMMINF/VISTA_S are set from pathology, not from outcome.
   The direction of the crossing is an output.

   TWO FINDINGS FALL OUT OF THIS TABLE.

   (a) THE EPITHELIOID RESULT SITS ON A THRESHOLD, AND IS FRAGILE.
       Sweeping the maximal immune kill rate over a 2.4-fold range,
       with everything else held fixed:
         KKILL /d   kill at the margin /d    epithelioid HR 24 mo
             0.22                  0.0296                    1.11
             0.33                  0.0429                    0.78
             0.44                  0.0552                    0.52
             0.53                  0.0645                    0.38
       An earlier parameterisation of this model appeared to show that
       the epithelioid hazard ratio was INSENSITIVE to this rate.  That
       was an artefact of moving the infiltration and VISTA parameters
       to compensate; with everything else held fixed the table above
       shows the opposite, and the claim is withdrawn.

       What the sweep actually shows is a THRESHOLD.  The quantity that
       matters is the immune kill AT THE THIN ADVANCING MARGIN against
       the margin's own net proliferation, 0.070 /d.  Below it the
       front keeps advancing and immunotherapy loses to six cycles of
       chemotherapy; approaching it, the front stops for as long as the
       drug is given -- two years rather than five months -- and
       immunotherapy wins by DURATION.  The calibrated value sits at
       0.043 /d, below the threshold and on the steep part of the
       curve.  So the model's epithelioid prediction is genuinely
       ill-conditioned, which is worth saying plainly: a 30 % error in
       one rate constant moves the epithelioid HR from 1.1 to 0.5.

   (b) THE COMPARATOR'S CROSSOVER IS NEEDED TO REPRODUCE CM743.
       Roughly 44 % of the CheckMate 743 chemotherapy arm went on to
       subsequent systemic therapy.  Mixing that fraction of the
       chemo-then-nivolumab arm into the comparator moves the model:
            0 % crossover in the comparator -> HR 0.78   (CheckMate 743: 0.74)
           25 % crossover in the comparator -> HR 0.80   (CheckMate 743: 0.74)
           45 % crossover in the comparator -> HR 0.82   (CheckMate 743: 0.74)
           60 % crossover in the comparator -> HR 0.84   (CheckMate 743: 0.74)
          epithelioid  HR 0.78 without crossover, 0.83 with 45 % -> trial 0.86
          sarcomatoid  HR 0.43 without crossover, 0.46 with 45 % -> trial 0.46
       The sarcomatoid subgroup then lands on the trial value exactly.
       The epithelioid subgroup does not -- see section S.

Q.  PREDICTIONS THE MODEL MAKES THAT NOBODY HAS MEASURED
==============================================================================
   (i) mRECIST CANNOT SEE A TWELVE-FOLD DIFFERENCE IN DRUG ACCESS.
       The author expected the partial-response rate to FALL with
       baseline rind thickness, since the exposed fraction does.  The
       table refutes that and says something sharper: with the involved
       AREA held fixed and only the thickness varied, the measured
       response is flat while the true kill is not.
     baseline h cm   f_exp  mRECIST best %  viable nadir %  category
              0.25   0.333           -63.1           -96.2        PR
              0.50   0.160           -62.2           -89.2        PR
              0.80   0.095           -59.1           -76.8        PR
              1.20   0.061           -59.0           -64.4        PR
              1.80   0.039           -61.6           -53.9        PR
              2.50   0.028           -63.8           -47.5        PR
       Every one of them is a partial response, over a 10-fold range of
       baseline thickness and a 12-fold range of exposed fraction.  The
       reason is that measured thickness is dominated by matrix
       clearance and by the sheet spreading, neither of which tracks
       how many tumour cells the drug reached.  THE TESTABLE CLAIM is
       therefore the opposite of the one expected: mRECIST response
       depth should NOT stratify by baseline rind thickness even though
       survival does (15.3 vs 4.4 months in section G).

   (ii) The fibrotic fraction of the MEASURED rind under treatment.
        day    0:  28.0 % of what the scanner measures is matrix, not tumour
        day   30:  41.4 % of what the scanner measures is matrix, not tumour
        day   60:  49.2 % of what the scanner measures is matrix, not tumour
        day  120:  68.5 % of what the scanner measures is matrix, not tumour
        day  180:  52.1 % of what the scanner measures is matrix, not tumour
        day  300:  16.0 % of what the scanner measures is matrix, not tumour
        day  500:  10.4 % of what the scanner measures is matrix, not tumour

   (iii) Serum mesothelin should be re-scaled by measured GFR before it
        is read as a response biomarker; the correction is worth
        +16 % at cycle 6 of cisplatin.

   (iv) Intrapleural chemotherapy and talc pleurodesis are mutually
        exclusive by geometry, not by pharmacology.

==============================================================================
S.  WHERE THIS MODEL DISAGREES WITH THE DATA
==============================================================================
   Listed rather than tuned away.

   1. THE HISTOLOGY x IMMUNOTHERAPY INTERACTION IS TOO FLAT.
      CheckMate 743 split 0.86 (epithelioid) / 0.46 (non-epithelioid).
      The model gives 0.78 / 0.43.  Differences in immune
      infiltration, VISTA-type suppression and chemosensitivity, set at
      literature magnitudes, do NOT generate a split that wide.  Either
      one of those differences is larger than assumed, or the trial's
      subgroup estimate is unstable (it rests on ~140 patients).

   2. THE IMMUNOTHERAPY CURVES CROSS TOO LATE.  The model's 12-month HR
      for nivo+ipi is 1.52 against 0.78 at 24 months.
      CheckMate 743's curves cross at about 8 months; the model's cross
      later, so it understates early immunotherapy benefit and the
      median lands on the wrong side of the crossing.

   3. ABSOLUTE MEDIANS FOR THE MODERN ARMS ARE LOW.  The cohort is
      anchored to EMPHACIS-era survival; MAPS, CheckMate 743 and MARS2
      enrolled fitter patients and their control arms run 16-25 months.
      Only the hazard ratios are comparable, and those are what was fit.

   4. THE SUPPLEMENTATION SURVIVAL EFFECT IS SMALLER THAN THE
      EMPHACIS SUBGROUP.  The trial's fully-supplemented subset showed
      13.3 vs 10.0 months; the model produces the toxicity difference
      but a much smaller survival difference, because in the equations
      folate reaches survival only through dose intensity and febrile
      neutropenia.  The abstract itself says supplementation reduced
      toxicity 'without adversely affecting survival time', so the
      13.3/10.0 contrast is confounded by era; the model sides with the
      smaller effect.
