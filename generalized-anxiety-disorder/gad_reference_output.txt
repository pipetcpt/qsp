     drug-placebo     -1.10 -> -2.45 -> -4.23 -> -5.33
     Doubling 10 -> 20 mg buys 0.109 occupancy and -1.78 HAM-A points.

     A CLEAN MISS, REPORTED.  The tidy version of this argument is that
     the flat clinical SSRI dose-response IS the occupancy hyperbola.
     The model does not support that.  Occupancy does flatten (0.744 ->
     0.853 from 10 to 20 mg, a 15% relative gain), but the HAM-A delta
     still grows by 73% (-2.45 -> -4.23), because in this model raised
     occupancy is followed by a STEEP rise in extracellular 5-HT (1.98 ->
     2.62) as the reuptake term approaches its floor, and that undoes the
     flattening.  Baldwin 2006 (PMID 16946363) found 20 mg NOT superior to
     10 mg; the model predicts about 1.8 points of superiority.  Either the
     5-HT rise saturates far earlier than the reuptake algebra implies, or
     the flatness comes from somewhere this model does not contain (most
     likely dropout at 20 mg, which Baldwin also reports).  Stated, not fixed.

  VENLAFAXINE ER 75 -> 150 -> 225 mg: SERT is already flat (0.752 -> 0.901)
     but NET is NOT (0.208 -> 0.344 -> 0.440; Arakawa 2019 measured 8-61%),

     drug-placebo -2.44 -> -4.24 -> -5.34

  it -- the same number with opposite sign, which is a testable coupling.

  HOW BIG IS THAT, HONESTLY?  Small.  The model returns the patient to

  literature describes discontinuation-emergent anxiety that clearly
  EXCEEDS the pre-treatment state for weeks.  Two readings are available

  would over-predict tolerance), or discontinuation anxiety is not the
  same object as loss of I_gaba at all -- it may be a distinct withdrawal
  syndrome that this one-state adaptation model does not contain.  The
  SHAPE is right (abrupt worse than tapered, decaying over ~3 weeks); the
  MAGNITUDE is under-predicted, and that is reported rather than tuned.

  TWO RESULTS, ONE STRONG AND ONE AGAINST EXPECTATION.
  (1) Phi is MULTIPLICATIVE to within a few percent in all three pairs
      (errors -0.4%, -1.2%, -0.7%).  That is the strong result: two drugs that divide
      different factors of the same ratio compose exactly, with no
      pharmacological interaction term anywhere in the model.
  (2) On HAM-A the combinations come out at 107%, 115%, 112% of the additive
      expectation -- i.e. SUPER-additive, which is the OPPOSITE of what
      was expected going in.  The expectation was sub-additivity from a
      saturating readout.  The readout does saturate, but not here: at
      effect sizes of 3-4 HAM-A points the arms sit on the CONVEX part
      of the link, where each further reduction in Phi buys MORE score
      than the last.  Sub-additivity is what the same link gives once
      the effects are large enough to approach the floor, and GAD trials
      never get there.  The prior expectation is recorded as refuted,
      and it inverts the clinical prediction: combination arms in GAD
      should BEAT the sum of their monotherapy deltas, not fall short.

  depend on a drug being present, so it does not unwind when dosing stops.

  too weak to overturn a monotone pharmacological effect, and either the
  AE weights or the mechanism of the reported 300 mg failure is wrong.

  SEVERITY DEPENDENCE (escitalopram 10 mg minus placebo, by baseline
  tertile).  The expectation going in was that the delta would GROW with
  baseline severity, because Phi is a ratio and the same fractional change
  buys more score further up the curve.  The tertiles are printed without
  comment so the reader can see whether that happened:
     mild      baseline ~18.8 : placebo -8.21, escitalopram -9.89, delta -1.68
     moderate  baseline ~23.1 : placebo -9.84, escitalopram -11.60, delta -1.76
     severe    baseline ~28.4 : placebo -12.34, escitalopram -13.80, delta -1.46

  IT DID NOT.  The difference is -1.68 in the mild tertile and -1.46 in the
  severe one -- flat, and non-monotone in between.  The reason is that
  two effects cancel: severe patients sit higher on the readout, where a
  given fractional change in Phi is worth more score, but they also have
  a LARGER placebo response (-12.34 versus -8.21), which is subtracted.
  The severity-enrichment argument therefore does NOT follow from this
  model, and the prior expectation is recorded as refuted rather than
  quietly dropped.

====================================================================================================
Design reproduced exactly: 12 weeks open-label escitalopram 20 mg/d;
responders (HAM-A <= 10) randomised to continue 20 mg or to placebo;
followed to week 76; relapse = HAM-A >= 15 at any scheduled visit.
