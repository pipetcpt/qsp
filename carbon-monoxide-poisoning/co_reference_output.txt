  COHb (blood, measured)       peak    0.3712  t(50%)   172.9 min  t(10%)    388.9 min
  brain tissue CO              peak     660.2  t(50%)   172.9 min  t(10%)    388.9 min
  cardiac myoglobin-CO         peak    0.4364  t(50%)   259.3 min  t(10%)    561.8 min
  skeletal muscle CO           peak     197.2  t(50%)   648.2 min  t(10%)   6093.2 min
  brain cytochrome c oxidase   peak    0.2204  t(50%)   777.9 min  t(10%)   7519.3 min
  -> COHb reaches the 'normal' 5% at 346 min (5.8 h).  At that moment brain
     cytochrome c oxidase is still 19.9% CO-inhibited, and it does not
     fall to 5% of its own peak until nan min (nan h) -- nanx later.
     The instrument that decides disposition is measuring the fast pool.

================================================================================
RESULT B.  DNS is a bistable switch, and its threshold is computable
================================================================================
  clone expands iff Tprol*H(MBPad) > Tdeath  =>  H > 0.4750
  MBPad_crit = theta*(H/(1-H))^(1/n) = 0.45*(0.4750/0.5250)^(1/6) = 0.44256
  and demyelination feeds antigen back (epitope spreading), so above the threshold
  the loop LATCHES: that is what supplies the weeks-long latency and the
  all-or-none character, neither of which was written into the model.
