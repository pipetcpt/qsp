==========================================================================================================================
ACUTE TYPE B AORTIC DISSECTION - QSP REFERENCE MODEL  (Python/scipy, LSODA)
53 ODEs, 22 scenarios.  Every number below is integrated, none is asserted.
==========================================================================================================================

The model is built around one product:

    ASI = (sigma_FL_sys / sigma_ref) x (dP/dt_max / dPdt_ref)

sigma carries the PRESSURE, dP/dt carries the IMPULSE, and the two are set by
different physiology.  Sections 1-3 test whether a drug that lowers only the
first can be worse than no drug at all.

scenario               SNA     HR     PP  PP_FL P_FLsys   TL-FL sig_flp   mobil

S21-vd-forced        1.446   84.8   55.1   34.2   102.7    15.8    75.3    1.06
S05-snp-only         1.355   80.3   45.8   29.0   109.1    13.1    61.1    1.04
S03-bb-only          1.384   57.0   40.3   29.9   102.3     9.0    40.0    1.00
S04-bb-then-vd       1.332   58.7   41.8   30.6   103.9     9.4    42.2    1.00

  A vasodilator raises heart rate, which shortens the cardiac cycle relative to
  the false lumen's RC time constant (tau_FL = 0.14 s here), so MORE of the
  pulse is filtered out of the false lumen and the systolic pressure DIFFERENCE
  across the intimal flap grows even as the pressure itself falls.  Tachycardia
  is not merely a cosmetic side effect of vasodilatation in a dissected aorta;
  it is a second, independent mechanism of flap loading.

  Nothing in the code says partial thrombosis is dangerous.  Two anatomical
  numbers were changed - the entry tear area and the distal re-entry
  conductance - and the rest is the divider:

    large re-entry  -> brisk through-flow, no thrombus, FL diastolic 65 mmHg,
                       growth 3.96 mm/y, 5-y survival 0.813
    no re-entry     -> blind sac: no outflow so no pressure drop, the pulse is
                       filtered out so FL DIASTOLIC pressure rises to 68 mmHg,
                       the stagnant distal sac clots to 47% while the entry tear
                       stays wide open, growth 6.68 mm/y, 5-y survival 0.613
    small entry     -> the whole sac clots, the entry seals, the false lumen
                       depressurises to 9 mmHg, growth -0.23 mm/y,
                       5-y survival 0.890

  Implied hazard ratio, partial vs patent = 2.36.  The reported adjusted
  hazard ratio in the IRAD cohort that first described this (Tsai 2007) is 2.69.

### 5. VERIFICATION - the growth maximum has a closed form

  Growth ∝ (1 + A*THR)^n * (1 - s*THR) with n = EXP_GROW = 1.9,
  s = THR_SUPPORT = 0.85, A = KTHR_H = 1.0 (thrombus thins the wall it
  supports).  dg/dTHR = 0 gives THR* = (n*A - s) / ((1+n)*s*A).

         THR   rel. growth
       0.000         1.000
       0.100         1.097
       0.200         1.174
       0.300         1.226
       0.400         1.251
       0.426         1.252
       0.500         1.242
       0.600         1.197
       0.700         1.110
       0.850         0.893
       1.000         0.560

  numerical argmax = 0.425   analytic optimum = 0.426   agreement 99.8%
  The interior maximum is the model's explanation of the paradox: a
  thrombus supports the wall LINEARLY and thins it under a Laplace
  exponent, so the worst possible false lumen is a half-clotted one.

S13-snp-toxic         134.1    45.00   0.494   2.52   2.12   2.28    0.633    71.7

  The same regimen at the same target reaches SBP 140 in the patient whose
  renal artery is fed by the false lumen and SBP 113 in the one whose is not.
  The vasodilator is already at its ceiling (32.0 vs 0.01 mg/h): renal
  hypoperfusion (Q_ren 0.48) drives renin to 2.6x normal, and the
  angiotensin II it makes puts the resistance back.  Refractory hypertension in
  aortic dissection is a diagnosis, not a dosing failure - and the model's
  advice is to open the kidney, not to add a fourth vasodilator.

  Nitroprusside at 45 mg/h (10.0 ug/kg/min) for 72 h with that same kidney
  reaches CN- 0.63 mg/L (toxic threshold 1.0) and SCN- 72 mg/L, and the
  lactate it produces (2.28 mmol/L) is indistinguishable at the bedside from
  the mesenteric ischaemia everyone is watching for.

S22-tevar-map          79.2   0.849    0.57     0.37    0.020    0.860

  Covering the entry tear costs 30% of the segmental supply and thrombosing the
  false lumen costs more, so the cord depends on a collateral network that has to
  be RECRUITED.  Sustained pressure augmentation halves the ischaemic burden
  (0.74 -> 0.37 index.h) but does not improve 5-year survival (0.878 ->
  0.860), because the pressure that perfuses the cord also loads the aorta.
  The model's reading: augmentation belongs to the coverage window, not to the
  discharge prescription.

scenario               SBP   MMP9 AT1blk    PRA   mm/yr  T55(y)   sig/S  surv5y
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
S14-fl-patent        110.1   1.74   0.91   1.54    3.96     nan    0.41   0.813
S20-nonadherent      132.6   2.04   0.71   1.42    7.94    3.21    0.86   0.275
S18-marfan-los       115.9   4.52   0.91   1.55    8.28    1.95    1.33   0.000
S19-marfan-nolos     119.6   4.57   0.00   0.73    8.29    2.00    1.34   0.000

  Adding losartan to a beta-blocked patient lowers systolic pressure by
  3.7 mmHg and blocks 91% of AT1, yet moves the time to 55 mm from
  2.00 y to 1.95 y - a difference of 17 days, in the WRONG direction.
  The model's arithmetic: beta-blockade has already suppressed renin (PRA 1.55),
  so there is little angiotensin II left for the ARB to act on, and what AT1
  blockade does reach the wall removes TGF-beta-driven adventitial collagen -
  the only negative feedback the aorta has - as well as Ang II-driven MMP-9.
  The two effects cancel.  This is a PREDICTION, and it is the model's most
  exposed one; it happens to agree with the randomised trials that failed to
  show losartan superior to a beta-blocker on aortic growth, but the model was
  not fitted to them and the cancellation could be an artefact of the
  TGF-beta/collagen coupling.  Flagged accordingly.
