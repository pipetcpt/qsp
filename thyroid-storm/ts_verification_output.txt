  HONEST LIMITATIONS
   L1  Treated-arm mortality is UNDER-predicted.  Modern series report
       ~8-25% with full treatment; this model gives well under 1% for
       the bundle, because it contains no comorbidity, no multi-organ
       failure, no thromboembolism, no ventilator or line complications
       and no drug-toxicity deaths (agranulocytosis, PTU hepatic
       failure).  Only the UNTREATED figure was calibrated; the treated
       figures should be read as 'the storm physiology was controlled',
       not as a survival estimate.
   L2  The runaway is capped at Tc = 43.0 degC by a numerical ceiling.
       Temperatures at the ceiling mean 'lethal hyperthermia', not a
       predicted measurement.
   L3  The fast/slow split is a modelling choice.  Rb (36 h) and the
       cardiac reserve are treated as slow; over a 7-day simulation both
       move, and the storm verdict in [H]/[S] freezes them.
   L4  Only ONE precipitant axis is modelled (a scalar Prec plus its
       PGE2 fraction).  Real precipitants differ qualitatively -- an
       iodine load acts through the gland, DKA through volume, surgery
       through catecholamines -- and the map shows those separately.
   L5  Amiodarone-induced thyrotoxicosis (types 1 and 2), for which
       iodide is contraindicated, is drawn on the map but is not a
       simulated scenario here.
   L6  Burch-Wartofsky is computed from simulated signs, so it inherits
       every simplification above; it is not an independent measurement.
   L7  There is no plasma / extravascular split, so plasma exchange can
       only be represented as its net WHOLE-BODY removal (~20% of T4,
       ~5% of T3).  The large immediate post-exchange dip in plasma
       concentration, and the rebound that follows it, are invisible
       here.  TPE is therefore under-represented acutely.
   L8  Clinical Wolff-Chaikoff escape is NOT reproduced within 21 days
       at SSKI doses (section [W]).  The mechanism (NIS down-regulation)
       is present and quantitatively large, but the intrathyroidal pool
       stays far above the Wolff-Chaikoff IC50, so the release block
       persists.  Either IC_WC is too low or a second escape mechanism
       (e.g. recovery of organification once the pool falls) is missing.
   L9  Ithy is a lumped, uncapped intrathyroidal pool.  Its ABSOLUTE
       value under a pharmacologic iodide load (hundreds of umol) is not
       physiological; only the saturating Wolff-Chaikoff function of it
       enters the model, so the conclusions do not depend on it.
