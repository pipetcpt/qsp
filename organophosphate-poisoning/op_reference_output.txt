2. CRITICAL INGESTED VOLUME (the dose at which the oxime runs out of ceiling)
==============================================================================
CYP desulfuration is saturable, so above C_thion >> Km the oxon plateau
is dose-INDEPENDENT at Vmax/CL_oxon.  The oxime ceiling therefore becomes
a CONSTANT for every large ingestion, no matter how large.

'ceiling' is the best any dose of that oxime could do; 'achieved' is what
the clinically attainable concentration (2-PAM 130 uM, obidoxime 20 uM)
actually delivers.  The gap between the two columns is the reason a
higher oxime dose is not the answer.

==============================================================================
3. THE AGING RATCHET  phi = k_a/(k_a + k_s + k_r)
==============================================================================
phi is the probability that ONE inhibition event ends irreversibly.
Dimethyl OPs age 9x faster than diethyl but ALSO reactivate spontaneously
110x faster -- the classic teaching that 'dimethyl OPs are hopeless' is
therefore NOT a statement about phi, it is a statement about how long the
enzyme stays inhibited, i.e. about C_oxon.

OP                 k_a h-1   k_s h-1  phi no oxime   phi 2-PAM 130uM  phi obi 20uM
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
chlorpyrifos        0.0210    0.0090         0.700             0.001         0.001
parathion           0.0210    0.0090         0.700             0.001         0.001
dimethoate          0.1873    0.9902         0.159             0.033         0.030
fenthion            0.1873    0.7702         0.196             0.034         0.031

Cumulative irreversible loss is an INTEGRAL, not an event:
      aged(T) = 1 - exp(-k_a * INTEGRAL f_EP dt)
   chlorpyrifos   f_EP=1 for   6 h  ->  aged =  11.8 %
   chlorpyrifos   f_EP=1 for  12 h  ->  aged =  22.3 %
   chlorpyrifos   f_EP=1 for  24 h  ->  aged =  39.6 %
   chlorpyrifos   f_EP=1 for  72 h  ->  aged =  78.0 %
   dimethoate     f_EP=1 for   6 h  ->  aged =  67.5 %
   dimethoate     f_EP=1 for  12 h  ->  aged =  89.4 %
   dimethoate     f_EP=1 for  24 h  ->  aged =  98.9 %

5. THE ATROPINE REQUIREMENT IS A HYPERBOLA IN ENZYME ACTIVITY
==============================================================================
Cleft ACh at steady state = (1+leak)/(E+leak); competitive antagonism then
makes the atropine concentration needed to restore baseline occupancy
        Ce* = KB * (ACh/ACh_0 - 1)
so the atropine requirement scales as 1/E, not as (1-E).

6. STOICHIOMETRIC BIOSCAVENGER: AN ARITHMETIC IMPOSSIBILITY FOR INSECTICIDES
==============================================================================
Plasma-derived human BChE binds oxon 1:1.  Nerve-agent prophylaxis works
because the agent dose is micromoles; an insecticide ingestion is
millimoles of PARENT that keeps generating oxon for days.

   chlorpyrifos  50 mL of 20% EC =    28.5 mmol parent;     200 mg BChE =   2.35 umol sites  ->  shortfall    12122-fold
   chlorpyrifos  50 mL of 20% EC =    28.5 mmol parent;    1000 mg BChE =  11.76 umol sites  ->  shortfall     2424-fold
   dimethoate    50 mL of 40% EC =    87.2 mmol parent;     200 mg BChE =   2.35 umol sites  ->  shortfall    37069-fold
   dimethoate    50 mL of 40% EC =    87.2 mmol parent;    1000 mg BChE =  11.76 umol sites  ->  shortfall     7414-fold

8. CONTRASTS THE MODEL PRODUCES WITHOUT BEING TOLD TO
==============================================================================
(a) Oxime benefit is a function of INGESTED DOSE, not of the drug:
    50 mL chlorpyrifos     AChE72h   0.0% ->  34.6% | mortality  27.3% ->  26.1%
    10 mL chlorpyrifos     AChE72h   0.1% ->  67.2% | mortality  18.3% ->   7.2%
    15 mL parathion        AChE72h   0.1% ->  45.5% | mortality  20.8% ->  10.8%
    50 mL dimethoate       AChE72h   0.1% ->   1.0% | mortality  13.3% ->  13.1%

(b) Route of oxime administration matters more than the choice of oxime
    2-PAM infusion (S02)  Css 292 uM  AChE72h 34.6%  mort 26.1%
    2-PAM q6h bolus (S03) Cmax 159 uM AChE72h 11.0%  mort 27.8%
    obidoxime      (S04)  Cmax 37 uM  AChE72h 28.4%  mort 28.5%

(c) The ventilator is worth more than every drug in the list:
    with ventilator (S02)  mortality 26.1%
    without         (S13)  mortality 52.3%

(d) Atropine titration speed, same total pharmacology:
    rapid doubling  (S02)  atropine 33 mg/24h, mortality 26.1%
    slow ad-hoc     (S14)  atropine 16 mg/24h, mortality 39.0%

(e) A peripherally restricted antimuscarinic cannot protect the brain:
    atropine        (S02)  peak seizure index 0.00, mortality 26.1%
    glycopyrrolate  (S15)  peak seizure index 0.21, mortality 44.5%

(f) Decontamination is a race against a first-order absorption constant:
    charcoal at 1 h (S17)  AChE nadir 0.0%, mortality 15.1%
    charcoal at 6 h (S18)  AChE nadir 0.0%, mortality 23.4%

(g) PON1 genotype scales the oxon exposure, hence the aged fraction:
    PON1 R192R (S02)  oxon24h 63 nM, aged72h 47.0%, mort 26.1%
    PON1 Q192Q (S20)  oxon24h 181 nM, aged72h 62.3%, mort 34.6%

(h) The bioscavenger changes nothing, as section 6 predicts:
    no scavenger (S02) AChE nadir 0.03%
    1 g BChE     (S19) AChE nadir 0.03%

(i) Lipophilic dimethyl OP (fenthion): late, nicotinic, ventilator-hungry
    S21 vent hours 64, peak nAChR block 0.55, IMS True, mortality 24.3%
    S02 vent hours 85, peak nAChR block 0.52, IMS True

(j) RBC AChE is a bad recovery marker because red cells cannot resynthesise:
    S02: day-7 RBC AChE 28.7% vs day-7 NMJ AChE 37.0% (muscle enzyme is resynthesised, red cells are not)
    S05: day-7 RBC AChE 64.6% vs day-7 NMJ AChE 70.3% (muscle enzyme is resynthesised, red cells are not)

10. VIRTUAL TRIAL: what a randomised oxime trial would have measured
==============================================================================
A pragmatic trial enrols whoever arrives.  Ingested volume is log-normal
and OP class is mixed; the model is run patient by patient and the two
arms are compared on the endpoint the real trials used (death).

   n = 60;  55% chlorpyrifos / 45% dimethoate;  median ingestion 31 mL
   mortality  placebo 18.9%   pralidoxime 17.2%   RR 0.91   ARR 1.7 points
   subgroup <= 15 mL  n= 11  placebo  11.6%  2-PAM   7.7%  RR 0.67
   subgroup > 15 mL   n= 49  placebo  20.5%  2-PAM  19.3%  RR 0.94
   subgroup chlorpyrifos n= 30  placebo  25.9%  2-PAM  22.5%  RR 0.87
   subgroup dimethoate   n= 30  placebo  11.9%  2-PAM  11.8%  RR 0.99

   The whole-trial RR sits close to 1 while a real, large effect exists in
   the low-dose diethyl stratum.  Dilution by patients above the oxime
   ceiling is sufficient to produce a null trial: no assumption about the
   oxime being inactive is required.
