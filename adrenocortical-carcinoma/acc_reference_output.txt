 0. THE DEPOT IS REAL: where does the mitotane actually sit?
==========================================================================
Apparent Vss                 : 5966 L   (reported ~6,086 L, Arshad 2018)
  of which adipose-dependent : 4466 L  (75%)
Terminal t1/2, uninduced     : 86 d
Terminal t1/2, fully induced : 42 d   (reported range 18-160 d)
Fraction of body burden in the depot at day 180: 93.6%

-> 94% of the drug in the body is in the depot. The measured plasma
   level is a thin overflow film on top of a lipid reservoir, which is
   why it responds to the reservoir SIZE and not just to the dose.

 A. FAT SETS *WHEN*, NOT *WHERE*  (identical 6/4/3 g regimen)
==========================================================================
fat (kg)      Vss (L)  t->14mg/L      peak   Cp d360   Cp d480    d >20
10               3530         20      21.6      15.8      15.8       51
22               5966         34      18.1      15.8      15.8        0
45              10635        110      15.7      15.6      15.7        0

Same dose, same destination (Cp d480 within 0.3 mg/L across a 4.5-fold
range of adipose mass) but a 5-fold spread in time-to-target -- and the
LEAN patient is the one who overshoots 20 mg/L.

Closed-form check (Css = D*F/CL, tau = Vss/CL):
  fat 10 kg: Vss=  3530  Css= 16.7 mg/L  tau= 35.7 d  t1/2= 24.8 d  t->14=  65.1 d
  fat 22 kg: Vss=  5966  Css= 16.7 mg/L  tau= 60.4 d  t1/2= 41.9 d  t->14= 110.0 d
  fat 45 kg: Vss= 10635  Css= 16.7 mg/L  tau=107.6 d  t1/2= 74.6 d  t->14= 196.1 d
Css is IDENTICAL across the three; only tau moves. That is the whole point.

 B. WHAT THE DOSE-REGIMEN TRIAL WAS UP AGAINST
==========================================================================
High-dose start (6 g x 6wk -> 4 -> 3) vs low-dose ramp (1 -> 2 -> 3),
time to first reach 14 mg/L, at three fixed adipose masses:

 high  regimen:  fat 10 kg ->     20 d  fat 22 kg ->     34 d  fat 45 kg ->    110 d
 low   regimen:  fat 10 kg ->     86 d  fat 22 kg ->    138 d  fat 45 kg ->    236 d

Virtual population (n = 240 per arm; adipose mass lognormal around
22 kg, clearance lognormal, together reproducing the reported ~80% CV
on apparent volume):

 high : ever reached target 195/240 (81%) | by day 90 76% | median t->14  35.0 d | Cp90  16.0 mg/L
 low  : ever reached target 150/240 (62%) | by day 90 15% | median t->14 121.0 d | Cp90  10.5 mg/L

HOW BIG IS EACH LEVER? Using Cp at day 90 -- a continuous outcome that
every simulated patient has, so nothing is lost to censoring:

  regimen      variance explained  37.7%
  fat          variance explained  24.7%
  clmult       variance explained  26.9%
  Residuals    variance explained  10.7%

  effect of regimen on median Cp90 : 5.5 mg/L
  effect of fat P10->P90 within arm: 9.1 mg/L
  correlation of Cp90 with adipose mass : r = -0.59

-> Two separate readings, both true, and they must not be conflated:
   VARIANCE SHARE. The regimen is the largest single term (37.7%), but the
     two unmeasured PATIENT factors -- adipose mass 24.7% and clearance
     26.9% -- together explain 51.6%, i.e. more than the dose does.
   CONTRAST SIZE. Comparing like with like, switching regimen moves median
     Cp90 by 5.5 mg/L, while moving adipose mass from its 10th to its 90th
     percentile moves it by 9.1 mg/L -- a LARGER contrast than the dose
     intervention produces, on a variable nobody randomised or measured.

   So the trial did randomise the smaller of the two levers, but not
   because the dose does not matter -- it does, and starting high is
   right. It is because the dose contrast it tested is smaller than the
   patient-to-patient contrast it left in the residual. Add the censoring
   problem on top: the reported endpoint -- time to reach target -- drops
   exactly the patients the covariate hurts most (the high-fat patients
   who never get there at all), so the covariate is invisible in the very
   analysis that would reveal it.

   That reconciles a popPK model recommending the high-dose start with a
   small RCT of the same comparison reporting no significant difference.
   Actionable version: stratify on body composition, and read the trough
   as a continuous exposure rather than a pass/fail at 14 mg/L.

 C. HYPERCORTISOLISM DIGS THE HOLE IT SITS IN -- BEFORE TREATMENT STARTS
==========================================================================
First, the untreated secreting tumour. Cortisol excess adds adipose:

day        freeCort   fat (kg)    Vss (L)  tumour mL
0              0.23       22.0       5966        122
90             2.28       25.2       6623        640
180            2.55       26.5       6871       2047
270            2.63       26.9       6959       4177

So a patient who spent ~9 months Cushingoid before diagnosis arrives
with 26.9 kg of fat instead of 22.0 -- a 993 L larger depot.
Now give both patients the SAME high-dose regimen:

                                         t->14 mg/L     Cp d90    Cp d480
no pre-treatment Cushing (22.0 kg)               35       17.6       15.8
Cushingoid before diagnosis (26.9 kg)            41       16.6       15.8

And the feedback DURING treatment, for completeness:
  fat at day 180: 22.1 kg (feedback on) vs 22.0 kg (off) -- only 0.1 kg

-> The feedback is essentially SPENT by the time therapy begins: once
   mitotane controls the cortisol, the fat stops accumulating, so during
   treatment the loop contributes almost nothing. Its whole effect is
   front-loaded into the pre-diagnosis period -- which is precisely when
   nobody is measuring it. The disease lengthens its own treatment delay
   before the first tablet is swallowed. The dose is right; the clock is
   wrong, and it was set wrong before the patient arrived.

Etoposide AUC computed on a fine grid (delta 0.02 d) over one cycle.

                               etoCLfold    AUC/cyc   best %   d180 %   resp    regain

-> Induction costs ~half the etoposide exposure and a chunk of the depth
   of response. EDP-M still beats Sz-M, reproducing FIRM-ACT's ordering,

   NOTE the RECIST artefact: a DEEPER nadir lowers the absolute bar for
   +20%/+5 mm progression, so nadir-referenced PFS can look SHORTER for a
   better responder. Time-to-regain-baseline is the fairer control metric.

 E. TOTAL CORTISOL LIES: two independent errors, same direction
==========================================================================
Adrenalectomised / adrenolysed patient on replacement, day 300-360 mean:

                                               CBG   total     FREE  %normal total/free
HC 20 mg/d, NO mitotane (control)             1.00    8.47    0.501      100%      16.9
HC 20 mg/d + mitotane                         1.81    6.53    0.175       35%      37.2
HC 50 mg/d + mitotane                         1.81   11.64    0.422       84%      27.6

-> The two errors are of DIFFERENT KINDS, and the decomposition below
   separates them cleanly:
     * INDUCTION is the error in the PATIENT. It doubles hydrocortisone
       clearance and takes free cortisol to 35% of control. This is a
       real deficit and it is the one that does the physiological harm.
     * The CBG RISE is the error in the ASSAY. At steady state it barely
       moves the free level (83% of control on its own), but it inflates
       the total/free ratio from 16.9 to 37.2 -- so the TOTAL cortisol a
       clinician actually orders reads reassuringly while free cortisol
       has halved.
   One error hides the other, and both point the clinician the same wrong
   way. Doubling the dose to 50 mg/day restores free cortisol to 84%.
   Measure free or salivary cortisol, not total.

Decomposition -- which of the two errors does the damage?
  both             free cortisol 0.175 ug/dL ( 35% of control)
  CBG rise only    free cortisol 0.417 ug/dL ( 83% of control)
  induction only   free cortisol 0.169 ug/dL ( 34% of control)
  neither          free cortisol 0.411 ug/dL ( 82% of control)

                                             freeCort   TEFF   best %   d180 %   resp
Pembrolizumab, NON-secreting ACC                 0.23   3.20    -43.8    -28.0     PR
Pembrolizumab, cortisol-SECRETING ACC            2.45   1.28     +0.0    +93.6     PD

No treatment, secreting (reference)              2.59   0.39     +0.0   +156.0     PD

PD-1 receptor occupancy is >99% in ALL of these runs -- target engagement
is never the problem. The effector cells the antibody would unleash have
already been suppressed by the tumour's own steroid output. Controlling
the steroid is a PREREQUISITE for immunotherapy, not an alternative.
