# Retinopathy of Prematurity — annotated references

Every PMID in this file was resolved through the NCBI E-utilities API during
the session that built the model; titles, journals and years are as returned by
PubMed. Where a number from a paper is used to calibrate or test the model, the
number is quoted here with the entry, and the abstract was read. Entries without
quoted numbers are bibliography — the title and journal were verified, the full
text was not read.

Papers marked **[CAL]** were used to fit a parameter; papers marked **[TEST]**
were used to test a model output that nothing was fitted to; **[FAIL]** marks a
paper the model does not reproduce.

## 2 · Supplemental oxygen as therapy — the phase 2 experiment

**[TEST] [FAIL] Supplemental Therapeutic Oxygen for Prethreshold Retinopathy Of Prematurity (STOP-ROP), a randomized, controlled trial. I: primary outcomes.**
*Pediatrics* 2000;105:295–310. [PMID 10654946](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10654946/)
> 649 infants with prethreshold ROP and median SpO2 <94%, randomised to a
> conventional arm targeting 89–94% or a supplemental arm targeting **96–99%**.
> Progression to threshold **48% vs 41%**; adjusted OR 0.72 (95% CI 0.52–1.01).
> Retinal detachment or fold 4.4% vs 4.1%. **Post hoc: infants without plus
> disease 46% → 32%, infants with plus disease 52% → 57%.** Pneumonia or
> exacerbation of chronic lung disease 8.5% vs 13.2%.
> Axis 2 explains the near miss: at PMA 35 wk the model needs PaO2 ≈ 95 mmHg to
> re-oxygenate the full retinal thickness, and 96–99% SpO2 in HbF-rich blood
> spans PaO2 61.7–104.2 mmHg — the prescribed band straddles the requirement.
> The subgroup contrast, however, is a reported model **failure** (F4): at the
> model's enrolment trigger only 25 of 621 infants yet have plus disease, so the
> split cannot be evaluated, and with a type-1 endpoint the model gives a larger
> benefit (23.2% → 13.6%) than the trial measured.

## 8 · Anti-VEGF treatment trials

**[TEST] [FAIL] Efficacy of intravitreal bevacizumab for stage 3+ retinopathy of prematurity** (BEAT-ROP).
*N Engl J Med* 2011;364:603–15. [PMID 21323540](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21323540/)
> 150 infants, bevacizumab **0.625 mg** bilaterally vs conventional laser.
> Recurrence requiring retreatment before 54 weeks PMA in **6 of 140 eyes (4%)
> with bevacizumab vs 32 of 146 eyes (22%) with laser** (P=0.002); significant
> for zone I (P=0.003) but not zone II (P=0.27). Peripheral vascularisation
> continued after bevacizumab; laser destroyed the peripheral retina
> permanently.
> **This is the model's headline failure (F1).** The model gives laser 0%
> recurrence at every ablation completeness from 95% down to 45% and anti-VEGF
> 100% — the ordering is inverted. The diagnosis is structural: laser failure is
> spatially focal (a skipped sector stays locally hypoxic) and a single
> well-mixed avascular compartment cannot represent a sector.

> success 56/70 (80%) vs 57/76 (75%) vs 45/68 (66%); ranibizumab 0.2 mg vs laser
