C. DISCRIMINATOR #1 — ZINC PROTOPORPHYRIN: same overload, opposite metal
==============================================================================
  Normal (FECH 100%)         free PPIX    0.13  Zn-PP   0.85  total EP    0.98  metal-free  13.3%
  EPP typical   (FECH 15%)   free PPIX   21.42  Zn-PP   1.42  total EP   22.83  metal-free  93.8%
  XLP (ALAS2 GOF x3.5)       free PPIX   17.49  Zn-PP   8.13  total EP   25.62  metal-free  68.3%

  free PPIX vs normal:  EPP x164   XLP x134    (both enormously up — the same overload)
  Zn-PP    vs normal:   EPP x1.7    XLP x9.6     (a 5.7-fold DISPROPORTION)
  metal-free fraction:  normal 13%  ->  EPP 94%  vs  XLP 68%

  Mechanism: FECH is the enzyme that inserts ZINC as well as iron.
  EPP breaks that enzyme, so the substrate piles up and the metal
  cannot go in either — free PPIX rises 160-fold while Zn-PP barely
  moves. XLP leaves FECH intact and merely floods it, so zinc
  insertion rises with the substrate. The clinically used metal-free
  fraction therefore separates the two diseases, and it does so here
  with nothing in the code special-casing them: only FRES and GOF
  differ between the two runs.

  (Contrast: iron deficiency and lead poisoning raise Zn-PP with a
   NORMAL metal-free PPIX — FECH is intact, the substrate is normal,
   only the metal is wrong. Set FEDOSE < 0 in section D to see the
   Zn-PP arm move on its own.)

  => In XLP, iron loading is STRONGLY beneficial: FECH is intact, so
     the extra iron feeds the disposal arm and it wins outright.
     In EPP the SAME intervention is essentially inert (a few percent
     the wrong way at modest doses): the disposal enzyme is broken and
     cannot use the iron, so all that is left is the IRE/IRP supply
     arm pushing ALAS2 up. Identical equations, identical parameters —
     only FRES and GOF differ between the two columns.
     This reproduces the clinical asymmetry: iron repletion has
     repeatedly helped in X-linked protoporphyria while trials and
     case series in EPP have been inconsistent and at best neutral.
     Note also the non-monotonicity in the XLP column at 0.5-0.75x:
     severe iron deficiency suppresses ALAS2 translation through the
     very same IRE, so the supply arm re-emerges at the bottom end.

E-G. THERAPY — two orthogonal axes, and why their effects MULTIPLY
==============================================================================
  Axis A (shielding)      MC1R agonists raise epidermal melanin OD
  Axis B (source)         bitopertin lowers PPIX via glycine limitation
  Photodynamic dose  D = E x T(melanin) x [PPIX]skin x t
  therefore tolerance time  t_tol = D_pro / (E x T x PPIX)  -- a RATIO
  of PRODUCTS, so the two axes compose multiplicatively.

  Endpoints are averaged over the whole 180-day season, because the
  afamelanotide implant produces a deliberate sawtooth (dosed q60d)

H. HEPATIC BISTABILITY — protoporphyric hepatopathy as a SADDLE-NODE
==============================================================================
  The only exit for PPIX is bile; PPIX crystals reduce bile flow;
  reduced bile flow raises hepatic PPIX. Positive feedback on a
  single-exit system produces MORE THAN ONE steady state.

  BISTABLE WINDOW in residual ferrochelatase activity:
    upper fold  FECH = 16.06%  (RBC PPIX 20.3 umol/L)  <- below this a second, cholestatic
                attractor APPEARS alongside the healthy one
    lower fold  FECH = 7.43%  (RBC PPIX 29.5 umol/L)  <- below this the HEALTHY branch
                CEASES TO EXIST and hepatopathy is obligatory
  Between the folds the patient is bistable: biochemically stable for
  decades, yet one sufficiently large transient (intercurrent illness,
  fasting, alcohol, a haemolytic episode) pushes the state past the
  saddle and the system falls onto the cholestatic branch and stays
  there. That is why protoporphyric liver failure presents abruptly.

  Worked example, FECH residual 10%:

    disease branch      hepatic PPIX   5.32  cholestasis 0.714
    margin to the saddle: 67% above the resting hepatic PPIX

  A three-microsecond species (singlet oxygen) has produced a
  three-day illness. The persistence is not in the photon and not in
  the porphyrin — it is in the slow inflammatory state variables the
  photon switched on, which is why the pain is opioid-refractory and

  wanted. The disease burden IS the avoidance.

  Two things to read off this table.
  (i)  THE STEP. Between a 20-minute and a 40-minute delay the peak
       pain roughly doubles and reaction-days go from ~0 to real:
       the reaction is all-or-nothing, not graded, because the
       mast-cell/complement arm becomes self-amplifying above DCRIT.
       Clinically this is why patients describe either 'a warning'
       or 'three days of boiling oil', and very little in between.
  (ii) THE SECOND BENEFIT OF SHIELDING. At every delay the
       afamelanotide column accrues LESS dose and MORE sun. By
       slowing the rate at which dose accumulates, the drug makes the
       SAME human reaction time non-limiting. That benefit is
       invisible to an 'hours in sunlight' endpoint, which is one
       reason patient-reported outcomes in this disease can move
       further than the primary endpoint does.

  A caveat the model produces on its own: with the nociceptor
  time constant set to ~50 h, DAILY exposure stacks even when every
  prodrome is obeyed. Fourteen consecutive days of compliant outdoor
  time at a 2-minute delay reach a plateau pain of:
    untreated        NRS 6.4 (cumulative pain-free sun 10.0 h)
    afamelanotide    NRS 6.2 (cumulative pain-free sun 16.3 h)
  i.e. the chronic low-grade burning reported by patients who try to
  live a normal outdoor life is a predicted consequence of a slow
  nociceptor state, not of any single reaction.
