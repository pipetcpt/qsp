1. THE GEOMETRY KERNEL, CHECKED AGAINST PUBLISHED NUMBERS
==============================================================================
   A red cell of membrane area S and volume V can be squeezed into a
   cylinder no narrower than D_c, the smallest positive root of
        V = S*D/4 - pi*D^3/12
   which has the closed form  D_c = 2*sqrt(S/pi)*cos(arccos(-s)/3 - 2pi/3)
   with s = V/V_sph and V_sph = S^1.5/(6 sqrt(pi)).  Nothing here is fitted.

   (S um^2, V um^3)                s       SI   D_c um   V_sph/V  OF %NaCl       EMA
   normal young 140, 90       0.5778   0.6937    2.722     1.731     0.418     1.000
   normal old   125, 82       0.6248   0.7308    2.817     1.601     0.452     0.895
   mild HS      128, 89       0.6536   0.7531    3.003     1.530     0.483     0.914
   moderate     118, 86       0.7135   0.7985    3.208     1.402     0.537     0.843
   severe       106, 80       0.7796   0.8470    3.411     1.283     0.600     0.757
   cordal cell   98, 78       0.8550   0.9009    3.745     1.170     0.679     0.700

   Published anchors, none of them used to fit anything:
     minimum cylindrical diameter of a normal red cell  ~2.8 um
       -> the formula gives 2.72-3.06 across the measured spread of
          normal (S, V); 2.72 at S=140/V=90, 3.06 at S=138/V=98.
     50%% osmotic lysis, normal                          0.40-0.45 %%NaCl
       -> derived 0.418 at S=140, V=90, MCHC 33.3
     50%% osmotic lysis, HS                              0.55-0.65 %%NaCl
       -> derived 0.537 at S=118, V=86
     EMA reduction diagnostic cut-off                   16-21%%
       -> a 118 um^2 membrane is a 15.7% reduction

   THE POINT THAT IS NOT IN THE TEXTBOOKS.  Waugh 1992 measured a
   50-day-old red cell losing 10.5%% of its AREA and 8.4%% of its
   VOLUME, and concluded there is 'little change in sphericity'.
   Run the same area loss with and without the matching volume loss:
     start                     s = 0.5778  D_c = 2.722 um
     -10.5% area, -8.4% volume  s = 0.6251  D_c = 2.819 um   (ageing)
     -10.5% area, volume kept   s = 0.6824  D_c = 3.129 um   (HS)
   The SAME membrane loss is 2.21x more spherocytising when the
   volume underneath it is not lost with it.  What makes an HS
   vesicle pathogenic is not that there are more of them; it is
   that they are haemoglobin-free.

5. THE AMPLIFIER HYPOTHESIS, TESTED AND NOT ADOPTED
==============================================================================
   The model was built around an idea: that the splenic cords do not
   merely EAT spherocytes, they MAKE them -- hypoxic, acidotic,
   glucose-poor, and the place a rigid cell spends the most time, so the
   place it should shed the most membrane.  That closes a loop:

        area loss -> D_c up -> longer cordal residence -> area loss

   whose gain is the parameter cordamp: membrane shedding inside the cord
   as a multiple of the circulating rate.  cordamp = 1 means off.

   cordamp          Hb     ret%     life     area      D_c  splenic %
   1.00          10.50     8.75    17.48    110.7    3.316        0.0
   1.50          10.03    10.68    15.47    110.5    3.335       14.1
   2.00           9.54    13.22    13.64    110.4    3.357       27.1
   3.00           8.53    19.32    10.56    110.1    3.408       48.7
   5.00           6.64    26.62     6.97    109.7    3.524       70.6
   10.00          5.00    36.35     4.43    109.0    3.706       84.4
   20.00          3.82    48.01     2.74    107.8    3.899       91.9

   THE HYPOTHESIS IS NOT REFUTED -- IT IS REDUNDANT.  Turning the
   gain up moves the phenotype smoothly and monotonically toward
   severity, which is exactly what turning fdef up does.  Inside
   this model the two are degenerate: any severity the amplifier
   produces can be produced by a slightly larger membrane defect,
   and there is only ONE HS number available to fit (and it is
   already spent on nothing -- see section 2).  So cordamp = 1 in
   the base model, and the HS phenotype in section 3 owes nothing
   to splenic conditioning.

   A REAL DEFECT WAS FOUND HERE AND IS WORTH RECORDING.  While this
   experiment was being built the same parameter set produced a mean
   red cell lifespan of 15.5, 18.6 or 30.8 days depending only on
   the ODE solver's maximum step size.  The cause was not cordamp:
   it was splenic hypertrophy.  Splenic blood flow had been written
   as proportional to splenic mass, which closes a SECOND loop --
   hazard -> erythrophagocytic workload -> splenic mass -> flow ->
   hazard -- with a gain above one, so the spleen ran to its ceiling
   and the steady state stopped being unique.  Flow per gram falls
   in a big spleen; writing flow ~ mass^0.35 with a 1.6x ceiling
   drops the gain below one and the model now gives identical
   answers at max_step 4, 6 and 8 (checked in section 18).

   WHAT THE SPLEEN IS, THEN.  Switch off the two splenic functions
   separately on the same moderate HS patient:
   spleen                            Hb    ret%    life    area    TBIL
   A intact                       10.50    8.75    17.5   110.7    3.30
   B eats but cannot retain       12.45    3.83    31.7   108.4    2.23
   C retains but cannot eat       13.89    1.88    59.1   104.3    1.34
   D splenectomised               12.45    3.83    31.7   108.4    2.23

   Total splenectomy benefit                  +1.95 g/dL
   B eats but cannot retain                  +1.95 g/dL  (100%)
   C retains but cannot eat                  +3.39 g/dL  (174%)

   Both single knock-outs recover most of the benefit, because the
   geometric hazard is a PRODUCT of retention and phagocytosis:
   f_pass x p_slow(D_c) x (1 - exp(-k_ph*MAC*tau_c*visc)).  Remove
   either factor and the term goes.  That is the honest answer to
   'sink or amplifier': in this model the spleen is a sink whose two
   halves are multiplicative, and no experiment inside the model
   assigns credit between them.

7. GENOTYPE x SPLENECTOMY: A PREDICTION THAT FAILS, AND BY HOW MUCH
==============================================================================
   Reliene 2002 (Blood 100:2208) reported two things.  First, that
   band 3-deficient red cells carry up to 140 IgG molecules per cell while
   spectrin/ankyrin-deficient cells and controls carry <= 60.  Second, that
   splenectomy helps spectrin/ankyrin-deficient cells MORE than band
   3-deficient ones.  In this model one parameter separates the genotypes:
   whether band 3 leaves the cell inside the shed vesicle.  Only the first
   of the two findings was used in calibration (stage 3).

                          IgG pre  IgG post   Hb pre  Hb post      dHb life pre life post
   spectrin/ankyrin            45        45    10.50    12.45     1.95     17.5     31.7
   band 3                     151       161     8.79    12.27     3.48     11.2     29.7

   THE FIRST FINDING IS REPRODUCED: 45 IgG per cell in the
   spectrin/ankyrin arm (Reliene: control level, <=60) against
   151 pre- and 161 post-splenectomy in the band 3 arm (Reliene:
   up to 140).  The mechanism is the accounting rule alone: if band
   3 leaves with the vesicle then band-3 SURFACE DENSITY is
   conserved as area falls, and the clusters that low-affinity
   natural antibody needs never form.

   THE SECOND FINDING IS NOT REPRODUCED.  The model has the band 3
   arm gaining MORE from splenectomy (+3.48 g/dL) than the
   spectrin/ankyrin arm (+1.95), the opposite of Reliene's
   direction.  The reason is a single number that was assumed rather
   than measured: the model gives the spleen 72%% of opsonic
   clearance and the liver 28%%, so removing the spleen removes most
   of the opsonic arm too.  Because the spectrin/ankyrin arm has NO
   opsonic clearance at all (IgG below threshold), that number acts
   only on the band 3 arm, and it can be solved for.

   hepatic share   dHb sp/ank  dHb band 3 dLife sp/ank dLife band3
   0.28                  1.95        3.48        14.2        18.6
   0.50                  1.95        2.96        14.2        16.0
   0.65                  1.95        2.59        14.2        14.1
   0.80                  1.95        2.21        14.2        12.3
   0.90                  1.95        1.98        14.2        11.1
   0.97                  1.95        1.83        14.2        10.3

   The ordering inverts once the LIVER carries at least 97% of
   opsonic clearance.  That is the model's quantitative reading
   of Reliene: their result is not evidence about spherocytes,
   it is evidence that clearance of heavily IgG-coated red cells
   is mostly hepatic.  The base model keeps the assumed 28%% and
   reports the failure rather than tuning to it.

9. PARVOVIRUS B19: THE ARITHMETIC OF AN APLASTIC CRISIS
==============================================================================
   A production arrest of D days costs a fraction D/lifespan of the
   red cell mass, because that is how much of it would have been
   replaced.  Nothing else is needed to explain why the same virus
   is trivial in a normal child and life-threatening in HS.

   vesicle haemoglobin                 Hb    life    area     MCV    MCHC     D_c

12. BILIRUBIN AND GILBERT SYNDROME: A PREDICTION THAT MISSES
==============================================================================
   ugt_f = 0.287 is NOT free: it is the UGT1A1*28 homozygote
   activity that reproduces an isolated Gilbert bilirubin of
   2.2 mg/dL at a normal haemolytic load.

                                      BR prod mg/d       TBIL   x normal
   normal                                    213       0.72       1.00
   Gilbert alone                             213       2.06       2.86
   moderate HS                               932       3.30       4.57
   moderate HS + Gilbert                     932      11.18      15.50
   severe HS + Gilbert                      1237      16.57      22.96

   HONEST MISS.  Published HS+Gilbert bilirubins cluster around
   4-7 mg/dL, not the ~10 the model gives.  The model combines the
   two insults MULTIPLICATIVELY because conjugation is the only
   disposal step it has; the data say they combine
   SUB-multiplicatively.  That is a structural statement, not a
   tuning error: bilirubin disposal must have a load-inducible
   component (uptake, MRP2 export, alternative conjugates) that
   UGT1A1 kinetics alone do not provide.  It is left in.

   mitapivat 100 mg alone                      10.90    +0.40
   both                                        12.58    +2.08

   The two act on DIFFERENT terms.  Mitapivat lowers the
   vesiculation rate through ATP, so it changes the cell's geometry;
   splenectomy leaves the geometry alone and removes the hazard that
   reads it.  The model therefore expects them to be roughly
   additive in Hb, and the drug to do little in an already
   splenectomised patient, because the hazard whose input it
   improves has already been removed.
