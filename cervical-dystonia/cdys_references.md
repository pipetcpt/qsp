## A note on what the references do and do not establish

The literature above supports the **structure** of the model — which
species exist, which interactions are real, and roughly how fast each
process runs. It does **not** supply most parameter values. Seven
parameters were fitted to seven reported clinical quantities
(`cdys_reference_check.py`, analysis A1); the rest are literature-shaped
order-of-magnitude choices. The five places where the model and the
literature disagree are enumerated in A13 rather than smoothed over, and
the reader should treat the four muscle torque shares `w_m` as the
weakest link in the whole construction: they set the factorisation of
φ = ρ·Σw, which A13(1) explains is *not* identified by the data.
