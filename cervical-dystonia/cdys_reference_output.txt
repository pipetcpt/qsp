A2  *** THE BOUND ***  the plateau is not saturation, it is a measurement
==============================================================================
  Chemodenervation can at best abolish transmission in the part of
  the neck the injectate actually reached.  Write that share as

      phi = rho * SUM(w_m over injected muscles)
             ^         ^
        how much of    which muscles
        each muscle    were on the list
        the needle
        reached

  Then for ANY toxin -- ANY dose, product, potency, dilution or
  interval -- the dystonic torque load obeys

      L(t)  >=  L_min  =  (1 - phi) * Dcen

  There is no drug parameter on the right-hand side.  Dose does not
  appear.  Potency does not appear.  Serotype does not appear.

  ---- phi is OBSERVABLE, and the observation is the plateau ----
  A dose-response curve that flattens while adverse effects keep
  climbing is not pharmacological saturation -- the toxin has not run
  out of anything.  It is the needle running out of DISEASE TO REACH.
  So the plateau measures phi.  Fitting it (A1 stage 1) gives:

      rho              = 0.5463
      SUM w (standard) = 0.6500   ->  phi(standard) = 0.3551
      SUM w (extended) = 0.8700   ->  phi(extended) = 0.4753

  Read plainly: about 64 % of the dystonic drive in
  cervical dystonia lies outside what a standard surface injection
  affects at all.  That number was not assumed from anatomy -- it was
  recovered from the shape of the published dose-response curve.

       Sprouting is worth nothing AT the bound, because sprouts draw
       on the same cytosolic SNARE pool the toxin has destroyed --
       under permanent blockade they carry nothing.  On a real
       wearing-off regimen they matter only during the recovery limb,
       and there they are worth little too.  That is a genuine
       finding and it CONTRADICTS the common claim that sprouting is
       what ends the clinical effect: in this model the effect ends
       because the light chain decays and SNAP-25 is resynthesised.
       Sprouting merely rides along.  A5 tests this directly.

    more or better toxin, at the current phi     4.37 pts    16.0 %

    perfect, permanent, side-effect-free chemodenervation of the
    motor units it currently reaches could ever deliver.  A 60-fold
    dose increase reaches 99.8 % of it -- i.e. essentially all of a
    small thing -- at P(dysphagia) 21 % instead of 13 %.

  Three consequences, in descending order of how uncomfortable they
  are for the way this disease is currently treated.

  (i)  THE DOSE AXIS IS THE WORST-VALUE AXIS AND IT CARRIES ALL THE
       HARM.  It is not merely inefficient: it is BOUNDED, and the
       bound is the smallest of the four.

  (ii) PLACEMENT AND TARGET LIST ARE NOT SUBSTITUTES FOR DOSE, THEY
       ARE PREREQUISITES FOR IT.  Applied at constant 240 U they buy
       little on their own -- extended list +2.95, perfect placement +12.85,
       both together +17.11 -- because they
       RELOCATE the ceiling rather than climb toward it.  Dose climbs;
       phi relocates.  A trial that improves guidance while holding
       total Units fixed is testing the wrong combination, and this is
       a concrete prediction about why such trials read as modest.

  (iii) THE BOUND IS NOT CLINICALLY ATTAINABLE ANYWAY.  Perfect
       blockade carries P(neck weakness) 10 % as-is and 93 % for the
       extended plan at perfect placement, because the posterior
       muscles that generate the dystonic torque are the same ones
       that hold the head up.  The antagonist of the disease is the
       agonist of the posture.  That is a geometric conflict, and no
       improvement in any toxin resolves it.

  The single largest prize in the table is the one nothing is aimed
  at: the motor nerve terminal's own capacity to repair itself.

A3  DOSE -> DURATION IS LOGARITHMIC; DOSE -> SPREAD IS LINEAR
==============================================================================
  E(S) is a threshold function (A0), so duration is the time S needs
  to climb back across the threshold.  S recovers at a rate set by
  k_syn and by the decay of the light chain -- NOT by how deep it was
  driven -- so each doubling of dose adds a roughly FIXED number of
  days.  Spread is different: the toxin arriving at the pharynx is a
  fixed fraction of whatever was injected, so it scales LINEARLY.

A5  SPROUTING IS NOT WHY THE EFFECT WEARS OFF
==============================================================================
  The received account of wearing-off is nerve-terminal sprouting:
  the terminal grows collateral sprouts, the sprouts form new
  functional contacts, transmission returns.  The sprouting is real --
  it is documented, it peaks at about the right time, and this model
  reproduces it (A4).  The claim tested here is the CAUSAL one.

  Sprouts grow from the same axon and load their vesicles using the
  same cytosolic SNARE pool the light chain has been destroying.  So
  sprout release must be gated by S as well.  If it is, sprouting
  cannot restore transmission while the toxin is still working -- and
  by the time the toxin has decayed enough for sprouts to function,
  the parent terminal is functioning too.  Sprouting then predicts
  almost nothing about duration.  Test it:

  The two strongest single operators are the two that are not oral
  drugs: raising phi and lowering central drive.  No licensed oral
  agent in this model reaches either with useful potency -- the same
  structural gap the influenza model finds for infected-cell death.

A11 A LONGER-ACTING TOXIN DOES NOT MOVE THE CEILING
==============================================================================
  daxibotulinumtoxinA is represented phenomenologically as a 2-fold
  slower loss of active light chain (kLC x 0.50).  A13 explains why
  that is a stand-in and not a mechanism.

   product              LC t1/2   nadir dTW  t_nadir  duration(d)  P(dysph)  AUC/injection
   incobotulinumtoxinA    28.1 d     -10.88      38d       109.0     11.0%         1529
   onabotulinumtoxinA     28.1 d     -10.88      38d       109.0     11.0%         1529
   abobotulinumtoxinA     28.1 d     -10.88      38d       109.0     11.0%         1529
   daxibotulinumtoxinA    56.2 d     -12.72      64d       263.0     11.8%         2795
   rimabotulinumtoxinB    19.4 d      -9.84      29d        73.0     10.4%         1074

  The three conventional serotype-A products are IDENTICAL once their
  label Units are converted, as they must be: in this model they differ
  only in unit scale and in protein load, and protein load acts on the
  antibody pool, not on the muscle.

  daxibotulinumtoxinA is BOTH deeper and longer, and that is worth
  stating precisely rather than glossing: depth and duration are not
  independent here, because both are governed by light-chain
  persistence.  Slower loss means the quasi-steady SNAP-25 level sits
  lower AND stays low for longer.  What matters is the EXCHANGE RATE:
      nadir    -10.88 -> -12.72   (+16.9 %)
      duration 109 d -> 263 d   (+141.3 %)
      i.e. 8.4 times as much duration as depth.
  Reported: daxibotulinumtoxinA gave roughly 24 weeks of benefit in
  cervical dystonia against about 12 for the conventional products, at
  a broadly comparable peak TWSTRS change -- the same lopsided exchange
  rate, and the model's ratio (2.4x duration) lands on the reported one
  (2x) without having been fitted to it.

  BUT THE CEILING HAS NOT MOVED.  Every product above remains far from
  the phi bound of A2:
      incobotulinumtoxinA  nadir  -10.88
      daxibotulinumtoxinA  nadir  -12.72
      BOUND at phi = 0.355   -13.24  (asymptotic)
  A longer-acting toxin is therefore a CONVENIENCE improvement -- fewer
  visits for the same control -- and only marginally an efficacy one.
  It buys benefit per INJECTION, not the benefit per cycle that the
  geometry levers of A2 buy.

  READ THE TOP OF THAT TABLE CAREFULLY, because it is not the answer
  one would want.  The two parameters that own the DURATION read-out
  are L50 and kp_off -- the position of the clinical severity curve and
  the pain time constant.  Neither is toxin biology.  They dominate
  because 'duration' is defined here as the time until the gain falls
  back through a FIXED THRESHOLD (the MCID), and anything that shifts
  the whole curve up or down moves that crossing a long way.

  So the headline is a caveat: a large part of what trials report as
  'duration of effect' is a property of WHERE THE THRESHOLD SITS
  relative to the response, not of how long the drug acts.  That is the
  same measurement problem A13(2) runs into from the other side.

  Among the parameters that ARE mechanism, the ordering is clean and it
  agrees with A4 and A5:
    k_LC   (light-chain persistence)  E_dur = -0.84   <- the real clock
    S50, k_cl, k_syn (SNARE turnover) E_dur ~ +/-0.5
    k_sp, k_rg (SPROUTING)            E_dur ~  0.04   <- essentially nil
  Sprouting has the smallest duration elasticity of any dynamic
  parameter in the model.  A5 reached that by knocking it out; this
  reaches it by perturbing it.  Two routes, same answer.

  Peak benefit belongs to L50, kp_off and hs -- again the clinical map,
  not the toxin.  Spread risk belongs to theta_scale, k_diff0, mass_sw,
  S50 and k_cl.  The three read-outs have almost disjoint owners, which
  is precisely why one dose knob cannot trade among them (A3).

A13 DISCREPANCIES -- five places this model does not fit, stated plainly
==============================================================================

  (1) THE CEILING IS TOO GENEROUS.
      phi = 0.65: asymptotic severity floor  6.98/35 (from 20.0), TWSTRS total 14.89
      phi = 0.87: asymptotic severity floor  1.70/35 (from 20.0), TWSTRS total  3.72
      Best reported severity outcomes with extended EMG- or
      ultrasound-guided patterns sit near 10-13/35, not 3-7.  Either
      the deep muscles carry less of the drive than the anatomical
      weights assume, or Dcen RISES to defend the posture when the
      periphery is silenced.  The model contains no such
      compensation and it probably should.  Largest open item.

  (2) THE EFFECT-TIME CURVE IS TOO SQUARE.
      model: 10 % of peak benefit by day 3.0, 50 % by day 7.5, 90 % by day 21.5;
      benefit stays above the 4.5-point MCID until day 109 (15.6 weeks).
      reported: first benefit at a median of about 7 days, and
      patient-perceived waning at 10-12 weeks.
      So the model rises too fast AND falls too slowly, even though
      it lands on both fitted TWSTRS time points.  That is not two
      errors, it is one: matching a RATING SCALE at two visits does
      not reproduce PERCEIVED onset and waning, so perceived benefit
      cannot be an affine function of the TWSTRS total.  A model that
      forced both would need a separate perception read-out with its
      own threshold and hysteresis; none is included here.

  (3) ONE HILL CANNOT HOLD BOTH ENDS OF THE DOSE-RESPONSE.
      60 U   week-4 dTW =  -3.48   nadir dTW =  -3.61
      120 U  week-4 dTW =  -7.58   nadir dTW =  -7.59
      180 U  week-4 dTW =  -9.58   nadir dTW =  -9.67
      240 U  week-4 dTW = -10.50   nadir dTW = -10.88
      480 U  week-4 dTW = -11.45   nadir dTW = -13.00
      The model reproduces 240 U > 120 U with 480 U barely better
      than 240 U.  What it cannot also reproduce with a single L50 is
      the near-linear 60-180 U range some series report.  That is
      evidence about the SHAPE of sev_ss(L), not about potency, and a
      model fitted on k_cl alone would have quietly absorbed a shape
      error into a toxin parameter.

  (4) PAIN IMPROVES MORE THAN TORQUE REDUCTION EXPLAINS.
      model pain change at the nadir: -2.42 / 20
      reported TWSTRS-pain change with 240 U: about -2.5 to -3.5.
      closing that gap requires a DIRECT antinociceptive term of
      magnitude pain_direct = 0.30, i.e. BoNT must be
      blocking CGRP and substance-P release from nociceptor terminals,
      not merely unloading the muscle.  That is a falsifiable claim,
      and pain_direct is left at 0 in the base model so the gap shows.

  (5) ONE k_b CANNOT SPAN BOTH FORMULATION ERAS.
      modern      5.0 ng/100 U   ->  5.10 % with clinically relevant antibody
      historical 25.0 ng/100 U   -> 39.46 % with clinically relevant antibody
      Reported: about 1-3 % for the modern formulation and up to
      ~9.5 % for the pre-1998 one.
      The model gets the ORDERING right and everything else wrong, and
      it is wrong in two distinguishable ways:
        (a) it is too HIGH in absolute terms even at the formulation it
            was calibrated on -- the median patient's Nab is on target
            by construction, but the recall term (1 + beta*Bm) makes the
            cohort tail heavier than a lognormal k_b alone would, so the
            5 % event rate overshoots the reported 1-3 %;
        (b) it is too STEEP in protein load -- a 5-fold load increase
            multiplies the event rate about 7.7-fold here, against
            roughly 4-fold in the historical comparison.
      (b) matters more than it looks: a pure antigen-DOSE model with a
      single saturation constant cannot be this steep AND match the
      absolute rate.  That argues antigen processing saturates harder
      than Ka_ag allows, or that individual variation sits on more than
      one axis (HLA type, prior exposure) rather than on k_b alone.
      Neither is included, so this row is a GAP, not a fit -- and note
      that it is the only anchor in A1 the model misses badly.

  ALSO NOT MODELLED: primary non-response from mis-targeting as
  distinct from a low phi; needle-EMG accuracy as a distribution
  rather than a binary; phasic versus tonic dystonia; head tremor;
  sensory-trick (geste antagoniste) physiology; and the
  anticholinergic cognitive burden that in practice limits
  trihexyphenidyl far more than its efficacy does.  The abo- and
  rima- Unit conversions are clinical rules of thumb and must not be
  read as equipotency claims.
