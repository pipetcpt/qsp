in this model reads the O2.- concentration; everything reads the HOCl flux.

SECTION B — THE CATALASE DOGMA, DONE AS ARITHMETIC
==============================================================================

The textbook says CGD patients are infected by CATALASE-POSITIVE organisms,
because catalase-negative organisms donate the H2O2 that the CGD phagosome
cannot make.  That is two claims, about two different properties, and the
model separates them.

CLAIM 1 — catalase protects the organism by destroying phagosomal H2O2.
Pseudo-first-order competition for H2O2 inside the phagosome (/s):

    organism                            k_MPO  k_catalase  catalase share
    Staphylococcus aureus             2.3e+04         708           2.9%
    Aspergillus fumigatus             2.3e+04         992           4.0%
    Escherichia coli                  2.3e+04         425           1.8%
    Streptococcus pneumoniae          2.3e+04           0           0.0%

    An organism would need 1.62e-03 M of intracellular
    catalase haem — 32x what S. aureus carries — merely to take
    HALF the phagosomal H2O2 away from MPO.  Catalase loses this race.

And the deeper problem with Claim 1: it is a claim about the NORMAL
phagosome.  In CGD there is no H2O2 for catalase to destroy.  Deleting
catalase from S. aureus in the model:
    normal          killed with catalase 0.9500 -> without catalase 0.9541 (delta +0.0041)
    phi = 0 (CGD)   killed with catalase 0.4000 -> without catalase 0.4000 (delta +0.0000)
    Messina 2002 made exactly this mutant and found it fully virulent in
    CGD mice.  The model says so too, and says why: nothing was removed.

CLAIM 2 — the organism's own H2O2 arms the CGD phagosome.  THIS one is
quantitatively enormous, and it is a DIFFERENT column of the table:

    organism                        catalase  self-arming index  CGD pathogen?
    Staphylococcus aureus                pos               0.00            YES
    Serratia marcescens                  pos               0.00            YES
    Burkholderia cepacia                 pos               0.00            YES
    Nocardia spp.                        pos               0.00            YES
    Aspergillus fumigatus                pos               0.00            YES
    Salmonella spp.                      pos               0.00            YES
    Granulibacter bethesdensis           pos               0.00            YES
    Escherichia coli                     pos               0.00             no
    Candida albicans                     pos               0.00             no
    Streptococcus pneumoniae             NEG               4.60             no
    Streptococcus pyogenes               NEG               1.50             no
    Enterococcus faecalis                NEG               0.80             no
    Lactobacillus spp.                   NEG               3.00             no
    Haemophilus influenzae               pos               0.00             no

    A self-arming index of 1.0 means the organism hands the phagosome as
    much H2O2 as a HEALTHY NADPH oxidase would.  Every peroxigenic organism
    in the table clears that bar, and not one of them causes CGD disease.

    BUT THE TABLE CANNOT ADJUDICATE BETWEEN THE TWO CLAIMS, AND SAYING SO
    IS THE POINT.  Score both rules as classifiers of CGD pathogenicity:
        'catalase-positive -> CGD pathogen':  TP 7 FP 3 FN 0 TN 4   accuracy 78.6%
        'cannot self-arm   -> CGD pathogen':  TP 7 FP 3 FN 0 TN 4   accuracy 78.6%

    IDENTICAL.  In this organism set catalase status and peroxigenicity are
    almost perfectly confounded, so the epidemiology cannot separate the
    hypotheses, and the decades of textbooks that cite the organism list as
    evidence for the catalase mechanism were citing evidence that does not
    discriminate.  Note also that BOTH rules mis-classify the same three
    organisms — E. coli, Candida albicans, Haemophilus influenzae are
    catalase-positive, cannot self-arm, and are still not CGD pathogens —
    which is where the third axis, how oxidant-dependent the killing of
    that organism is at all, has to do the work.

    WHAT DOES DISCRIMINATE is the arithmetic, and it goes against catalase
    on both counts: catalase captures under 5% of phagosomal H2O2 even in a
    normal phagosome, and in the CGD phagosome it captures 5% of nothing.
    Deleting it changes the modelled kill by 0.0025 and 0.0000 respectively,
    which is what Messina 2002 found in mice.

    CATALASE-NEGATIVITY IS A MARKER.  PEROXIGENICITY IS THE MECHANISM.
    The discriminating experiment the confounding hides: a catalase-NEGATIVE
    but non-peroxigenic organism should behave as a CGD pathogen, and a
    catalase-POSITIVE peroxigenic organism should be spared.  The dogma
    predicts the opposite for both.

SECTION C — K(phi): THE SHAPE OF THE DOSE-RESPONSE TO OXIDASE ACTIVITY
==============================================================================

Hill fit to K(phi) = log10 kill of one ingested S. aureus in 60 min:
    K0     = 0.22185
    Kmax   = 1.301
    phi50  = 0.54482
    hill   = 2.7351
    r2     = 0.96379
    rmse   = 0.061674

    phi50 = 0.545 with a Hill coefficient of 2.74.


    The Hill form fits only moderately (r2 0.964) and it fits BADLY at the
    ends, because the true curve has no upper plateau.  Read the raw curve
    instead, in 0.2 steps of phi, and the shape is unmistakable:
        phi 0.0 -> 0.2:  K gains 0.138 log
        phi 0.2 -> 0.4:  K gains 0.193 log
        phi 0.4 -> 0.6:  K gains 0.216 log
        phi 0.6 -> 0.8:  K gains 0.246 log
        phi 0.8 -> 1.0:  K gains 0.286 log

    Each successive fifth of oxidase activity is worth MORE than the one
    before it, all the way to phi = 1.  A power law K = K0 + A*phi^n fits
    with n = 1.33, i.e. convex and still accelerating at full
    activity.  There is NO THRESHOLD inside the phagosome, and the first
    20% of oxidase activity is the LEAST valuable 20% there is.
    This matters clinically because it is the opposite of the intuition
    behind gene therapy and mixed chimerism, where 10-20% correction is
    known to be enough.  If the phagosome has no threshold, the clinical
    threshold has to be somewhere else.  It is: see Sections D and F.

Where the phagosome DOES have a threshold is its pH:
    phi = 0.30  peak pH 7.200  peak K+ 0.248 M
    phi = 0.50  peak pH 7.200  peak K+ 0.320 M
    phi = 0.55  peak pH 7.200  peak K+ 0.338 M
    phi = 0.60  peak pH 7.205  peak K+ 0.356 M
    phi = 0.70  peak pH 7.324  peak K+ 0.392 M
    phi = 0.90  peak pH 7.635  peak K+ 0.464 M
    phi = 1.00  peak pH 7.800  peak K+ 0.500 M

    Alkalinisation is a race between the oxidase's proton consumption and
    the V-ATPase, and below ~57% of normal flux the V-ATPase simply wins.
    So a hypomorphic patient with 30% residual oxidase has a phagosome that
    is, in pH and therefore in alkaline-protease terms, indistinguishable
    from a null patient's — even though the DHR assay separates them
    cleanly.  The location of that threshold is set by pH_rest and by the
    V-ATPase rate, neither of which is well measured; the EXISTENCE of the
    threshold is structural.

         0.20       0.4949        0.5161    0.959  MOSAIC
         0.30       0.4423        0.3996    1.107  uniform
         0.40       0.3897        0.2844    1.370  uniform
         0.50       0.3372        0.2003    1.684  uniform
         0.70       0.2320        0.1150    2.018  uniform
         0.90       0.1269        0.0826    1.536  uniform

    THE ORDERING CHANGES SIGN AT A DHR MEAN OF 0.238.
    Below it, concentrating the oxidase into a few fully competent cells
    beats spreading it thinly over all of them; above it, the reverse.
    The crossover is the inflection of K(phi) and nothing else.

    The crossover matters because of WHERE the clinic operates.  X-linked
    carriers, mixed chimerism after reduced-intensity conditioning, and
    gene-therapy marking all sit in the 5-25% band, which is below the
    crossover, so mosaicism is the favourable arrangement there.  A
    hypomorphic patient reported as '20% of normal' on DHR is NOT the same
    patient as a carrier reported as '20% of normal', and the flow report
    that produced both numbers cannot say which one it is looking at unless
    the HISTOGRAM — bimodal versus uniformly shifted — is read as well.
    Above the crossover the ordering reverses, so this is not a general
    claim that mosaicism is better; it is a claim that the sufficient
    statistic is the distribution and not its mean.

At the level of the whole focus the same comparison is much sharper,
because the sheltered compartment Bi only exists in cells that cannot kill:

    CAVEAT ON THE LOCATION.  The mortality column is dominated by the
    fungal term, because invasive aspergillosis carries a 20x higher
    case-fatality than a bacterial infection.  So the position of the
    threshold is mostly the position of the FUNGAL critical inoculum, and
    the fungal arm is the less well constrained of the two.  The bacterial
    column, which is better constrained, shows the same convexity but a
    gentler one: 20% correction halves it, 50% quarters it again.

    NOTE WHERE THE THRESHOLD COMES FROM.  It is NOT in K(phi), which has no
    threshold at all (Section C).  It is manufactured downstream, by the
    sheltered compartment: correcting a cell does not merely add a killer,
    it removes a sanctuary, and the sanctuary term is what is convex.

SECTION H — GRANULOMA WITHOUT AN ORGANISM
==============================================================================

The claim: CGD's inflammatory disease is not caused by the infections and
does not answer to antimicrobials.  The test: run patients who are never
inoculated with anything at all, for a year.


    arm                              IL-1b  IL-10  apoptotic  granuloma  colitis    CRP
    healthy control                   2.35   4.46        9.2      0.079     0.45   1.06
    X-CGD null, no therapy            7.21   4.46       36.7      0.998     5.58   1.12
    X-CGD, co-trimox+itra             7.21   4.46       36.7      0.998     5.58   1.12
    X-CGD, prednisolone 1 mg/kg       6.46   4.46       36.7      0.236     1.09   1.04
    X-CGD, anakinra 2 mg/kg           7.21   4.46       36.7      0.039     0.17   1.06
    X-CGD, HSCT 95% chimerism         2.44   4.46        9.5      0.097     0.55   1.06
    X-CGD, gene therapy 20%           4.94   4.46       23.0      0.603     3.41   1.09

    Antimicrobial prophylaxis does not touch the granuloma, because there
    was never an organism in it.  Anakinra and transplant both abolish it,
    by opposite routes — one blocks the receptor, the other restores the
    oxidase that would have restrained the inflammasome and permitted
    efferocytosis in the first place.  Prednisolone lands in between and
    buys its effect with an infection penalty the other two do not pay.

    The mechanism is a LOOP, not a level: the CGD neutrophil dies without
    externalising the oxidised phosphatidylserine that marks it for
    clearance, so the corpse persists, and the corpse is simultaneously an
    IL-1beta stimulus AND the missing source of the IL-10 that would have
    switched the lesion off.  Note the IL-10 column: it does not fall in
    CGD, it RISES, because there are so many more corpses; what fails is
    IL-10 per corpse, and the model shows that failing quantity is not
    enough on its own.

    A DHR-MEAN CHECK.  The mosaic and uniform patients of Section D, whose
    infection risks differ, have IDENTICAL inflammatory phenotypes:
        X-CGD carrier 20%      IL-1b   4.93  granuloma  0.600  colitis  3.40
        X-CGD hypomorph 20%    IL-1b   4.93  granuloma  0.600  colitis  3.40
        The DHR mean IS a sufficient statistic for the inflammatory
        phenotype and is NOT one for the infectious phenotype, because
        efferocytosis and inflammasome restraint average over the
        population while killing happens one phagosome at a time.

SECTION L — WHAT THIS MODEL GETS WRONG, AND WHAT IT NEVER TESTED
==============================================================================

 1. PEAK SUPEROXIDE is 17x below Winterbourn's own estimate (Section A).  The
    two models disagree about whether MPO or spontaneous dismutation is the
    dominant superoxide sink.  Nothing downstream reads [O2.-], so the error is
    contained, but it is a real disagreement and not a rounding difference.

 2. THE ALKALINISATION THRESHOLD at phi ~ 0.57 (Section C) depends on two
    numbers that are barely measured: the resting phagosomal pH and the
    V-ATPase rate constant.  Its EXISTENCE is structural; its LOCATION is not,
    and no clinical claim in this file rests on the location.

 3. SEGAL vs JANKOWSKI/GRINSTEIN on whether the CGD phagosome really fails to
    alkalinise is unresolved in the literature and is unresolved here.  The
    model follows Segal and prices the alternative (Section I) rather than
    pretending the question is closed.

 4. THE ITRACONAZOLE PREDICTION UNDERSHOOTS BADLY: 53% against the trial's 86%.
    Of the four predicted trials this is the poor one, and the direction is
    informative — the model has itraconazole acting only on hyphal extension,
    whereas azoles also block germination, which is the step the model gives
    entirely to the (uncorrectable) conidial survival term.  Adding a
    germination-block arm would very likely fix it and was not added, because
    it would then be a fitted arm rather than a predicted one.

 5. THE CO-TRIMOXAZOLE PREDICTION IS GOOD (61% vs 56%) BUT FLATTERS ITSELF: the
    untreated arm of that comparison is one of the two numbers the exposure
    model was calibrated on, so only the treated arm is a prediction.

 6. THE KUHNS GRADIENT IS UNDER-PREDICTED TWOFOLD (Section K).  Either the
    real residual-ROS distribution reaches higher than the one drawn here, or
    K(phi) is too flat at low phi, or residual ROS carries prognostic
    information this model does not represent at all.  The model cannot
    distinguish those three, and does not pretend to.

 7. omega, the oxidant-dependence of each organism, is ASSUMED.  It is the
    softest input in the whole model and it drives the entire organism
    spectrum.  The self-arming index of Section B does not depend on it; the
    Winkelstein ordering does.

 8. NOT TESTED AT ALL: age.  Every patient in this file is a 30 kg child with
    fixed pharmacokinetics.  CGD presents from infancy to the sixth decade and
    the drugs, the marrow and the exposure all change across that range.

 9. NOT TESTED: the autosomal-recessive genotypes as distinct entities.  p47phox
    deficiency is milder than X-linked CGD in every registry, and in this model
    that is represented ONLY by giving it a higher phi_res.  If any part of the
    AR/XL difference is not residual ROS, this model cannot see it.

10. NO R TOOLCHAIN was available in this environment, so cgd_mrgsolve_model.R
    and cgd_shiny_app.R mirror the equations executed here but have not
    themselves been run.  Parameters are injected into them mechanically by
    sync_r_params.py from cgd_calibration.json, so they cannot drift, but the
    R syntax has not been exercised by an interpreter.
