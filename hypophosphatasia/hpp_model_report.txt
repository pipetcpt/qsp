[T1] THRESHOLD, NOT GRADIENT — the severity spectrum is ONE hyperbola
==============================================================================
Mrel(E) = (1 + PPi0/Ki) / (1 + (Jppi + kout*PPi_pl) / (Ki*(kcat*E + kout)))
  E50 (local activity giving half the maximal mineral apposition):
     0.766 of normal activity from the EXACT quadratic QSS solution;
     0.691 from the linearised hyperbola above (PPi << Km limit).
  Either way the steep part of the curve lies BELOW ~0.2 of normal, which
  is why heterozygous carriers at ~0.5 are near-normal and why the
  perinatal-to-odonto spectrum compresses into one order of magnitude.

  11.7x more enzyme (infantile 3% -> adult 35%) buys only 3.39x mineral apposition;
  and 1.4x more again (adult -> carrier 50%) buys a further 1.30x only.
  Osteoid volume fraction (normal <2%, osteomalacia >10%) reproduces the
  histomorphometric spectrum without having been fitted to it.

[NUMERICAL CHECKS] the disagreements, reported rather than absorbed
==============================================================================
  RSS(182 d) at h = 0.25 / 0.125 / 0.0625 d: 0.774618 / 0.774612 / 0.774611
  |h=.125 - h=.0625| = 8.69e-07  => discretisation is not the story
  EXACT quadratic QSS vs the 32-state integration, perivesicular PPi:
  worst relative deviation = 0.00e+00  (identical by construction).
  Same comparison on Mrel: 22.6% - that entire gap is the OSTEOPONTIN
  co-inhibition state, which the PPi-only closed form does not contain.
  LINEARISED hyperbola vs integration: worst deviation 44.1% (at E=0.10).
  That residual is the Michaelis-Menten term the linearisation drops -
  perivesicular PPi reaches 43-95% of Km in the severe phenotypes.
  The hyperbola is therefore used ONLY to expose structure; every number
  reported above comes from the exact solution.
