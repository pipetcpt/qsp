 EXPERIMENT 4 -- Reperfusion is also an injury: the pH paradox, minute by minute
==============================================================================

Reperfusion necrosis comes from mPTP opening, and the pore is gated by
tissue acid: gate = 1/(1+H/HP50).  Acid keeps the pore SHUT, and acid is
removed only by flow.  Restoring flow therefore strips the protection at
the very instant it ignites the succinate pool.

 EXPERIMENT 6 -- Fibrinolysis versus primary PCI: where the crossover falls, and why
==============================================================================

Two different mechanisms of reperfusion sharing one clock.
  - Tenecteplase is available immediately, but patency has to be EARNED by
    plasmin, and plasmin also activates platelets, so the artery can reocclude.
  - PCI restores patency in one step, but it arrives later.
Nothing tells the model which is better, or when.

==============================================================================

MVO has its own equation -- endothelial energy failure graded
transmurally, neutrophil plugging, distal embolisation, slow resolution

 EXPERIMENT 8 -- The remodelling bifurcation: a critical infarct size, computed
==============================================================================

Laplace: wall stress = P*r/(2h).  Dilation raises r, which raises stress,
which drives dilation -- a POSITIVE loop.  Concentric and eccentric
hypertrophy raise wall mass, which lowers stress -- the NEGATIVE loop.
Crucially, SCAR CANNOT HYPERTROPHY, so the brake is scaled by the
surviving fraction of the ventricle.  Whether a ventricle converges or
diverges is therefore a race between two rates.

READ THE VOLUMES ONLY ON THE STABLE BRANCH.  A run marked DIVERGED has
run into the model's growth ceiling; past that point it is reporting a
verdict (this ventricle does not stabilise), not millilitres.  No GDMT
anywhere in this table.

==============================================================================
 EXPERIMENT 10 -- IV beta-blockade before reperfusion: rate control is part of the clock
==============================================================================

Metoprolol has no anti-necrotic term anywhere in this model.  Coronary
perfusion happens mostly in DIASTOLE, so slowing the heart lengthens
diastole and raises COLLATERAL flow -- and collateral flow is exactly what
the wavefront is racing.  The timing prediction is therefore forced: it
can only help while the wavefront is still moving.

Note what this implies, and note that the model was not tuned to avoid it.
Rate control cannot change the BASAL metabolic bill (basal metabolism is
rate-independent by definition), so it cannot rescue tissue whose supply is
already below basal -- it can only raise supply.  The predicted effect on
infarct size is therefore SMALL.
